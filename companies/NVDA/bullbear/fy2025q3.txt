NVIDIA FY2025 Q3 10-Q (filed November 20, 2024; quarter ended October 27, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2025 Q2 10-Q. Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $35.1B, +94% y/y and +17% q/q (Q2: +122% y/y). Data Center +112%; compute $27.6B
   (+132%), networking $3.1B (+20%, -15% q/q). Cloud providers about 50% of Data Center (MD&A,
   Third Quarter Summary). Growth is volume-led on Hopper/H200; no pricing data disclosed.

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Gross margin 74.6% (Q2 75.1%), down sequentially on "a mix shift from H100 systems to more
   complex and higher cost systems". Net inventory provisions cost 2.2 points. Operating margin 62.3%;
   operating expenses 12.3% of revenue vs 16.5% a year earlier.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Other, net only $36M; tax rate 13.5% (12.2%). Earnings are operating. A state valuation-allowance
   release is flagged as possible "in the near term" (a future one-off benefit).

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Nine-month operating cash flow $47.5B vs net income $50.8B (93%), "partially offset by advanced
   payments on supply agreements"; receivables helped by $1.7B paid before due date. Q3 FCF $16.8B
   = 87% of net income (Q2 81%). DSO 46 days.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Q3 buybacks $11.1B (92M shares), dividends $245M; Q3 capex $0.8B. $46.4B authorization left after
   the August 2024 $50B add. All funded from operating cash; debt unchanged at $8.46B.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Cash and securities $38.5B; debt $8.46B, nothing due within a year. Supply and capacity
   obligations $28.9B (Q2 $27.8B) "primarily due to commitments for Blackwell capacity" plus $13.2B
   other, incl. $11.3B multi-year cloud. Warranty liability $1.0B vs $306M at FY2024 year-end.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   Three direct customers at 12% each (36%); one indirect customer at 10%+ via Customer C. Four
   customers hold 18/13/11/11% of receivables. Non-U.S. billing 58% of revenue.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   No critical-estimate changes. Note adds that customers A-D "may represent different customers
   than those reported in a previous period", so the series is not comparable. Unrecognized tax
   benefits $1.9B.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Blackwell systems (GB200 NVL72/36, B200) named into the export-license scope; new risk on U.S.
   automotive import restrictions and on China steering buyers to local competitors. Securities
   case: Supreme Court heard argument November 13, 2024.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   "Blackwell production shipments are scheduled to begin in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025";
   Blackwell demand "is expected to exceed supply for several quarters in fiscal year 2026"; "We plan
   to continue share repurchases this fiscal year." FY2025 capex guide ($3.5-4.0B, from the FY2024
   10-K) not updated; nine-month capex $2.2B.
