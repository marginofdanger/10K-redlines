NVIDIA FY2026 Q2 10-Q (filed August 27, 2025; quarter ended July 27, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2026 Q1 10-Q.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $46.7B, +56% y/y, +6% q/q. Data Center compute -1% q/q "driven by a $4.0 billion reduction
   in H20 sales"; excluding H20 (Q1 $4.6B, Q2 about $0.65B outside China) revenue rose about 17% q/q.
   Networking $7.3B (+98% y/y, +46% q/q). Large cloud providers about 50% of Data Center.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Gross margin 72.4% (Q1 60.5%; 75.1% a year ago) as Blackwell is "primarily of full-scale
   datacenter systems". $180M H20 reserve release. Operating margin 60.8% (62.0%).

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Other income $2.2B, mostly unrealized gains on public equities = 7.2% of pre-tax income. Small
   reserve release ($180M). Tax rate 15.3%; OBBBA "did not have a material impact".

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $15.4B vs net income $26.4B (58%); FCF $13.5B = 51%. Receivables $27.8B,
   DSO 54 days (Q1 46). Customer advances $7.5B for the half, so only about $1.3B in Q2 after $6.2B
   in Q1.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks $9.7B; dividends $244M; capex $1.9B. New $60.0B authorization (August 26, 2025). The
   "offset dilution" purpose sentence is deleted.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Cash and securities $56.8B; debt $8.47B. Total commitments $45.8B, $30.9B due in the rest of
   FY2026. Future leases not commenced $7.1B. Unrecognized tax benefits $2.9B.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   Customer A 23%, Customer B 16% (Q1 16/14%); two indirect customers 10%+ through A and B.
   Receivables 23/19/14%. Non-U.S. billing 50%; Singapore 22%.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   No estimate changes. Tax-rate driver wording moves from FDII to FDDEI under OBBBA.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   H20 licenses granted in August 2025 but no shipments; USG expects "15% of the revenue generated from
   licensed H20 sales" without a regulation; China publicly questioned H20 "built-in vulnerabilities".

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   Blackwell Ultra production shipments began in Q2: met. No H20 sales to China. No capex update
   (H1 capex $3.1B vs $3.4B for all of FY2025, consistent with the increase guide).
