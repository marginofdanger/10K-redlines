NVIDIA FY2026 Q3 10-Q (filed November 19, 2025; quarter ended October 26, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2026 Q2 10-Q.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $57.0B, +62% y/y, +22% q/q (Q2 +6%). Data Center $51.2B (+66%); compute $43.0B (+27% q/q);
   networking $8.2B (+162% y/y) on NVLink. "Blackwell Ultra is now our leading architecture across all
   customer categories." H20 sales "insignificant".

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Gross margin 73.4% (Q2 72.4%; 74.6% a year ago), up "as Blackwell ramped with an improved mix and
   cost structure". Net provisions only 0.1 point. Operating margin 63.1% (62.3%); opex 10.3% of revenue.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Other income $1.4B = 3.6% of pre-tax income. Tax rate 15.9%. Valuation-allowance release still
   flagged as possible "in the near term".

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Q3 operating cash flow $23.8B vs net income $31.9B (74%); FCF $22.1B = 69% (Q2 51%). Receivables
   $33.4B, DSO 53 days. Inventory $19.8B (+32% q/q).

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks $12.6B; dividends $243M; capex $1.6B. Investment commitments $6.5B incl. $5B in Intel;
   OpenAI letter of intent; up to $10B in Anthropic (November 2025). Authorization left $62.2B.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Cash and securities $60.6B; debt $8.47B. Commitments restated by type: manufacturing and supply
   $50.3B, cloud $26B, investments $6.5B, other $2.1B. First guarantee: partner facility lease, $860M
   maximum exposure, $470M in escrow, in exchange for warrants.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   Four direct customers 22/15/13/11% (61%); receivables 22/17/14/12%. Geography recast to customer
   headquarters: non-U.S. 31% vs 44% a year earlier.

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Geography moved from billing location to headquarters basis with prior periods recast; the
   Singapore line (22% of Q2 billings) disappears. Unrecognized tax benefits $3.2B.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Foreclosure in China now actual ("We have effectively been foreclosed"); China's antitrust
   regulator published a preliminary finding (September 15, 2025) that NVIDIA violated its Mellanox
   approval; GAIN AI Act passed the Senate; USG revenue-share expectation widened to "15% or more".

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   No numeric guidance. "Our tax payments in fiscal year 2026 will decrease due to currently
   effective OBBBA provisions." Capex nine months $4.8B, already above FY2025's $3.4B (increase guide met).
