Latest filing: 10-Q FY2027 Q2

What the FY2027 Q2 10-Q adds, for and against. The quarter is the strongest operating print in the series and the largest expansion of NVIDIA's own balance-sheet exposure to its customers; both sides are real.

Bull case

P1 Data Center demand and growthStated
Revenue $96.2B, +106% year over year and +18% sequentially; Data Center $89.0B, +117%; ACIE +138%
FY2027 Q2: "Revenue was $96.2 billion, up 106% from a year ago and up 18% sequentially."
FY2027 Q2: "ACIE revenue increased 138% from a year ago and 25% sequentially driven by end-demand from AI natives, enterprises, and sovereign customers, as well as hyperscalers utilizing AI clouds."

Size: +$49.5B of revenue year over year; one quarter equals 45% of all FY2026 revenue ($215.9B). Growth reaccelerated from +56% a year earlier and +85% in Q1.

A second straight filing of acceleration on top of a $200B+ base, with the non-hyperscale half (ACIE, $40.3B) growing faster than Hyperscale (+102%). This is what moves P1 to Strengthening.

Next filing: Q3 growth above 100% with Rubin added; ACIE share of Data Center holding near 45%.

P2 Margins and operating leverageStated
Gross margin 75.0% vs 72.4%; operating margin 66.2% vs 60.8%; opex 8.8% of revenue vs 11.6%
FY2027 Q2: "Gross margin increased to 75.0% for the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 compared to 72.4% for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026, and 75.0% for the first half of fiscal year 2027 compared to 66.6% for the first half of fiscal year 2026 due to improved mix from Blackwell Ultra."

Size: operating income $63.7B, +124%, faster than revenue (+106%); net inventory provisions cost 0.8 points (1.1 a year earlier).

Margins are back at the FY2025 peak after the FY2026 dip to 71.1% that the systems transition caused, even with rising memory prices named in the same MD&A.

Next filing: gross margin near 75% during the Rubin ramp, when new-architecture yields and provisions usually weigh.

P1 Data Center demand and growthStated
Vera Rubin shipped at the start of its guided window; customer advances doubled to $15.6B in the half
FY2027 Q2: "Our next-generation Data Center architecture, Vera Rubin, began production shipments in the third quarter of fiscal year 2027."
FY2027 Q2: "Included $15.6 billion and $7.5 billion of customer advances for the first half of fiscal years 2027 and 2026, respectively."

Size: the FY2026 10-K guided Rubin to the second half of FY2027; shipments began in its first quarter. Current deferred revenue $4.6B vs $1.4B at year-end.

The one-year cadence held for a third architecture in the window, and customers are paying ahead of delivery at twice last year's rate, a demand signal that does not rely on NVIDIA's estimates.

Next filing: Rubin revenue quantified or described as ramping; advances continuing.

P3 Customer concentrationStated
Only one direct customer above 10% (16%), against three at 21%, 17% and 16% in Q1
FY2027 Q2: "For the second quarter of fiscal year 2027, one direct customer represented 16% of total revenue, which was primarily attributable to the Compute & Networking segment."

Size: disclosed 10%+ customers 54% of revenue in Q1 → 16% in Q2. Taiwan-headquartered revenue rose from $12.0B to $27.0B in the same quarter.

The first quarter in the series with a single 10% direct customer. Part of the drop may be volume moving to Taiwan-based system builders rather than new end customers (inference), so the end-customer picture may be unchanged.

Next filing: a second quarter with one or no 10% customer would start to move P3; watch the indirect AI research company sentence.

P6 Cash conversion and capital returnStated
First full quarter of the $0.25 dividend: $6.0B paid, plus $19.7B of buybacks; diluted shares −1.0% year over year
FY2027 Q2: "We paid cash dividends to our shareholders of $6.0 billion and $6.3 billion during the second quarter and first half of fiscal year 2027, respectively."

Size: H1 returns $46.1B (buybacks $39.8B + dividends $6.3B) vs H1 free cash flow about $70.0B ($74.4B operating cash flow less $4.4B capex) = 66%. $99.3B of authorization left.

On a half-year basis capital return is still funded from free cash flow, and the dividend is now about $24B a year. The quarter alone tells a different story (see the bear card on debt).

Next filing: returns within free cash flow in Q3 without new borrowing.

P4 China and export controlsInferred
Revenue from China-headquartered customers $7.9B, up from $4.6B in Q1 and $4.0B a year earlier
FY2027 Q2: "China (including Hong Kong) | 7,880 | 3,985 | 12,430 | 13,644"

Size: 8.2% of Q2 revenue; +98% year over year. Hopper shipments to China were under 1% of Data Center revenue, so most of this is something else.

The filing says NVIDIA can still ship "uncontrolled products to China, such as gaming and workstation GPUs" but does not explain a figure larger than all of Edge Computing ($7.2B). China is not zero, though the data center market stays foreclosed. Basis is inferred because the composition is not disclosed.

Next filing: an explanation of China-headquartered revenue, or a second quarter at this level.

Bear case

P7 Commitments, guarantees and customer financingStated
Up to $105B of guarantees on OpenAI's 20-year leases at SB Energy's Ohio campus; total guarantee exposure $3.5B → $108.5B
FY2027 Q2: "SB Energy Corp. guarantees – In August 2026, we entered into guarantees, capped at a total of $105 billion, to provide credit support on a land, power, and shell buildout with affiliates of SB Energy Corp."
FY2027 Q2: "The guarantees terminate upon certain events, including OpenAI achieving a satisfactory credit rating or after each respective lease term has completed."

Size: $108.5B maximum = 83% of FY2026 operating income ($130.4B) and 31x the Q1 exposure. Phases take effect from FY2029; NVIDIA holds an option on about 3.8 more gigawatts.

The termination clause says the tenant does not now have a satisfactory credit rating. NVIDIA is underwriting a customer's rent so the campus will host its compute; the new lead risk factor says it "may assume the applicable lease" on default.

Next filing: whether the option is exercised, any change in OpenAI's credit standing, and whether more single-customer guarantees follow.

P7 Commitments, guarantees and customer financingStated
Supply and capacity commitments $119B → $279B in one quarter, with $175B due in FY2028–FY2029
FY2027 Q2: "We have significantly increased our supply and capacity commitments from $119 billion last quarter to $279 billion as of July 26, 2026 to meet future demand."
FY2027 Q2: "Supply and capacity | 92 | 87 | 88 | 6 | 5 | 1 | 279"

Size: $279B = 92% of trailing four-quarter revenue ($303.0B); total commitments table $366B, plus $56B of additional commitments. In Q1, 80% fell inside the fiscal year; now 33% does.

NVIDIA has locked in memory and manufacturing capacity for three years. If demand slows, the H20 sequence ($4.5B charge on far smaller commitments) shows how purchase obligations turn into charges. The note now says agreements "may be cancelable", where Q1 said they "are cancellable".

Next filing: excess purchase-obligation provisions (Q2: $201M) and whether FY2028 commitments keep rising.

P7 Commitments, guarantees and customer financingStated
New AI-cloud model: clouds buy NVIDIA systems and NVIDIA commits $36B to buy back their capacity; $20B of leases signed for third parties
FY2027 Q2: "Under these agreements, AI clouds procure our data center infrastructure products and we commit to cloud service agreements, which the AI clouds can unilaterally stop providing to us and sell to third-party customers at more advantageous rates."
FY2027 Q2: "Under the agreements, if AI clouds do not successfully sell committed capacity to third-party customers, we have agreed to purchase that capacity."

Size: $36B of six-year capacity backstops = 35% of FY2026 operating cash flow ($102.7B); $20B of leases NVIDIA expects to reassign. None of the $36B is payable this fiscal year.

Part of ACIE demand now comes with NVIDIA standing behind the buyer's utilization. The clouds keep the upside (they can resell at better rates); NVIDIA keeps the downside. The MD&A says these customers "currently lack" investment-grade financing.

Next filing: how much backstopped capacity NVIDIA actually uses or pays for, and how much ACIE revenue is tied to these agreements.

P6 Cash conversion and capital returnStated
Receivables $40.7B → $63.1B on new payment terms of up to one year; Q2 free cash flow 36% of net income
FY2027 Q2: "In certain cases, for investment-grade customer purchases, we have and may in the future provide longer payment terms ranging from 90 days up to one year to assist customers with large data center builds depending on size."
FY2027 Q2: "Cash provided by operating activities increased in the first half of fiscal year 2027 compared to the first half of fiscal year 2026 due to higher revenue, partially offset by an increase in accounts receivable due to extended payment terms on large multi-quarter agreements with certain investment-grade customers."

Size: receivables +$22.4B in the quarter = 23% of Q2 revenue; DSO 60 days (Q1 45). Q2 operating cash flow $24.1B vs net income $59.7B; FCF $21.4B.

Vendor financing in another form: NVIDIA is carrying its largest buyers' payments for up to a year. The MD&A says these terms "will continue to affect the timing of our operating cash flows".

Next filing: DSO back below 50 days, or a further rise that confirms the terms are structural.

P6 Cash conversion and capital returnInferred
$25.0B of notes issued; debt $8.5B → $33.4B in the quarter NVIDIA returned $25.7B
FY2027 Q2: "In June 2026, we issued an aggregate of $25.0 billion of senior unsecured notes across seven tranches for general corporate purposes."

Size: Q2 buybacks $19.7B + dividends $6.0B = $25.7B vs Q2 free cash flow $21.4B; interest expense $227M vs $62M a year earlier.

The notes are cheap relative to NVIDIA's cash generation, and liquidity is large ($56.6B of cash and debt securities). But the quarter's returns exceeded its free cash flow and matched the borrowing, and the 10-Q added a debt risk factor for the first time since the FY2025 10-K deleted it.

Next filing: commercial paper use, further issuance, and returns vs free cash flow.

P5 Earnings qualityStated
Equity portfolio reaches $99B; H1 gains $23.7B = 17% of pre-tax income; warrants booked with the benefit deferred
FY2027 Q2: "These include equity investments of $99 billion and equity investment commitments of $25 billion as of July 26, 2026."
FY2027 Q2: "Gains from equity securities, net, were primarily driven by unrealized gains in equity securities."

Size: Q2 gains $7.8B = 10.9% of pre-tax income ($71.5B); H1 $23.7B of $141.4B. $36.9B of public holdings are under lock-up; warrants $4.8B notional, $824M fair value.

The holdings include "AI model makers, infrastructure financiers, and other private companies", some of them customers. Marks on these move net income in both directions and are not cash.

Next filing: other income as a share of pre-tax income, and any impairments once lock-ups lapse.
