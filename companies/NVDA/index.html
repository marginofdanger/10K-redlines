NVDA 10-K Redline Analysis

Year-over-year textual analysis of NVIDIA SEC filings from FY2020 through FY2026. Revenue grew from $10.9B to $215.9B. Self-description changed from "pioneered accelerated computing" to "data center scale AI infrastructure company." Export control language progressed from absent to "effectively foreclosed."
Source: SEC EDGAR (CIK 0001045810) | Fiscal Years 2020-2026 | Analysis: March 2026; FY2025 and FY2026 transitions re-done with sentence signals and phrase histories, October 2026
FY2020 → FY2021

The Mellanox Pivot

Segments renamed GPU/Tegra → Graphics/Compute & Networking. Mellanox acquired. Arm deal announced ($40B). Revenue $10.9B → $16.7B (+53%). Interconnect competitors first listed. Amazon added to custom silicon competitor list.

FY2021 → FY2022

Arm Termination & ESG High-Water Mark

Arm deal terminated ($1.36B charge). CMP (crypto mining) disclosed at $550M revenue. ESG section added with demographics and 100% renewable goal. Revenue $16.7B → $26.9B (+61%). Data Center moved to #2 market listing.

FY2022 → FY2023

LLM and Export Control Entry

Data Center became #1 market listing. "Large language models" and "generative AI" entered filing. H100/Hopper launched. China export controls first disclosed. Revenue flat ($27B). Gross margin declined to 56.9% on $2.17B inventory provisions.

FY2023 → FY2024

Infrastructure Company

Self-description changed to "full-stack computing infrastructure company reshaping industry." Revenue $27B → $60.9B (+126%). Gross margin recovered to 72.7%. Customer concentration re-emerged at 13% direct. Huawei and Microsoft added as named competitors.

FY2024 → FY2025

Blackwell Ships, Disclosure Thins

Blackwell shipped from Q4 on a new yearly cadence, and the architecture-transition risk was restated as having happened ("have been ... negatively impacted"). Revenue $60.9B → $130.5B (+114%). Three direct customers at 12/11/11%; the indirect "approximately 19%" became "10% or more." The inference share (~40%), cloud-provider share, China percentage and capex range were all dropped, as were "demand remains very strong" and "We plan to continue share repurchases." Supply obligations $16.1B → $30.8B. The AI Diffusion IFR took export controls worldwide. Indebtedness risk factor deleted. Gross margin 75.0%.

FY2025 → FY2026

China Foreclosed, Customers Financed

Revenue $130.5B → $215.9B (+65%). Harm from export controls went from "may" to "will"; NVIDIA "effectively foreclosed" from China, with a $4.5B H20 charge and Beijing having "discouraged" purchases. Direct customers at 22% + 14%; the count of 10%+ indirect customers became "some." $3.5B of lease guarantees and $17.5B of private investments, some in AI model makers that buy NVIDIA products. Commitments $95.2B on "longer future ordering horizons"; commercial paper capacity $575M → $25.0B. Groq license for $17B. DGX Cloud, diversity data and the renewable commitment deleted. Gross margin 71.1%.

Product Transitions & Inventory Risk

▶Architecture-transition risk restated as having happened: "may be negatively impacted" → "have been and may in the future be"Changed

Realized: hypothetical → actual; certainty: may → have (+)

FY2024: "If we are unable to execute our architectural transitions as planned for any reason, our financial results may be negatively impacted."
FY2025: "Our financial results have been and may in the future be negatively impacted if we are unable to execute our architectural transitions as planned for any reason. ... History: the old sentence stood unchanged in every filing from Q2 FY2024 to Q3 FY2025 (six periods). "have been and may in the future be negatively impacted" first appears in FY2025 and recurs through Q1 FY2027. "low-yielding Blackwell material" first appears in the Q2 FY2025 10-Q.

The 10-K admits a missed transition has already cost money, and names the Blackwell yield problem as the example.

A yearly cadence multiplies the transitions the previous finding says have already hurt results. The same risk factor warns of "reduced demand for current generation architectures when customers anticipate transitions."

Price reductions "due to our channel pricing programs" are new to the list of things that have already happened. That sits oddly beside the record margins of the same year; the filing does not say which products were affected.

Export Controls & Supply Geography

▶AI Diffusion IFR: worldwide licensing for A100 through GB200; "over 150 countries" designatedNew

Named: new rule and products; scope widened (+ disclosure)

FY2024: "In October 2023, the USG announced new and updated licensing requirements ... for exports to China and Country Groups D1, D4, and D5"
FY2025: "Unless a license exception is available, the worldwide licensing requirements will apply to the following NVIDIA products, and any others we develop that meet the characteristics of 3A090.a or 4A090.a, including but not limited to: A100, A800, H100, H200, H800, B100, B200, GB200, L4, L40S, and RTX 6000 Ada."

▶China regulators investigating whether complying with U.S. controls "discriminates unfairly"New

The first sign of China pushing back as well as the U.S. restricting. The listed penalties gain "restrictions regarding our networking products and services," which points at the Mellanox approval. The investigation reaches a preliminary finding against NVIDIA in September 2025.

▶Supply dependence "especially in Taiwan" → "especially in Taiwan and South Korea"Changed

Named: South Korea added; scope widened

FY2024: "Finally, our business depends on our ability to receive consistent and reliable supply from our overseas partners, especially in Taiwan."
