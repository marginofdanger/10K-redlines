AMAZON FY2024 10-K (filed February 7, 2025; year ended December 31, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q3 2024 10-Q (trend items) and FY2023 (annual items).

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Sales $638.0B, +11%. North America +10%, International +9%, AWS +19% ($107.6B). Q4 AWS about +19%
   (derived: annual less nine months). Backlog $177B (Q3 $164B), weighted-average life 4.1 years.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Operating income $68.6B vs $36.9B; margin 10.8% vs 6.4%. North America 6.4% (4.2%), International 2.7%
   (loss), AWS 37.0% (27.1%). Q4 operating income $21.2B, above the $16.0-20.0B guide.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   2024 server life extension benefit: $3.2B lower depreciation ($2.5B net income). Q4 includes about $920M
   of accelerated depreciation from early server retirements. Effective tax rate 13.5% (19.0%) with stock
   compensation benefits. Rivian loss $1.6B in other expense (Note 1, MD&A Income Taxes).

4. Cash conversion [HELD]
   Operating cash flow $115.9B (+36%); free cash flow $38.2B vs $36.8B (+4%); FCF 64% of net income $59.2B
   (MD&A Non-GAAP). DSO 32 days (33).

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Cash capex $77.7B vs $48.1B (+61%); "We expect cash capital expenditures to increase in 2025". Anthropic:
   $1.3B third note, $2.7B more due by Q4 2025. No buybacks or dividends.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [MIXED: WORSE]
   Notes $58.0B (face $67.2B a year earlier): debt repaid. But leases not yet commenced $61.6B vs $38.2B and
   unconditional purchase obligations $50.4B vs $35.5B; total commitments $333.0B vs $300.4B (Note 7).

7. Concentration [HELD]
   AWS 17% of sales, 58% of operating income. No vendor 10% or more of purchases.

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Effective January 2025: heavy equipment life 10 to 13 years (+$0.9B 2025 operating income); a subset of
   servers 6 to 5 years (-$0.7B); accelerated depreciation continues (-$0.6B). Reverses part of 2024's
   extension, citing the pace of AI development (Note 1).

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [HELD]
   Kove appeal pending; DC Attorney General antitrust suit revived (August 2024); Arizona AG suit (May 2024).
   No new quantified losses.

10. Guidance and commitments [BETTER]
   Q4 2024 guide met on sales ($187.8B, top of range) and beaten on operating income ($21.2B vs $16.0-20.0B).
   Q1 2025 guide: sales $151.0-155.5B, operating income $14.0-18.0B, with a $2.1B FX headwind.
