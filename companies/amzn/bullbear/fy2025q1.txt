AMAZON Q1 2025 10-Q (filed May 2, 2025; quarter ended March 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2024 10-K.

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   Sales $155.7B, +9% (+10% ex-FX). North America +8%, International +5% (+8% ex-FX), AWS +17% (Q4 2024
   about +19%). Backlog $189B (FY2024 $177B).

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Operating income $18.4B vs $15.3B; margin 11.8% vs 10.7%. North America 6.3% (5.8%), International 3.0%
   (2.8%), AWS 39.5% (37.6%).

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Other income $2.7B, mainly the gain reclassified on Anthropic notes converted to preferred stock (12% of
   pre-tax income $21.7B). Tax provision includes $559M of discrete expense.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Q1 operating cash flow $17.0B vs $19.0B. Trailing free cash flow $25.9B vs $50.1B a year earlier (FY2024
   $38.2B). Q1 free cash flow about -$8.0B (XBRL). DSO 32 days (30).

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Cash capex $24.3B vs $13.9B (+75%). Capex to "increase in 2025". No buybacks or dividends.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Notes $58.75B face. Cash and marketable securities $94.6B (FY2024 $101.2B). Commitments $357.3B;
   leases not yet commenced $67.2B.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   AWS 19% of sales, 63% of operating income.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Server life change to 5 years for a subset and heavy equipment to 13 years took effect; no effect quantified.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Luxembourg court dismissed Amazon's appeal of the EUR 746M CNPD (GDPR) fine (March 2025); appeal filed.

10. Guidance and commitments [BETTER]
   Q1 guide beaten: sales $155.7B vs $151.0-155.5B; operating income $18.4B vs $14.0-18.0B.
   Q2 guide: sales $159.0-164.0B, operating income $13.0-17.5B.
