AMAZON Q3 2025 10-Q (filed October 31, 2025; quarter ended September 30, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 2025 10-Q.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Sales $180.2B, +13% (+12% ex-FX). North America +11%, International +14% (+10% ex-FX), AWS +20%
   (Q2 +17%), the first reacceleration in the window. Backlog $200B (Q2 $195B).

2. Margins and unit economics [WORSE, on charges]
   Operating income $17.4B, flat, after a $2.5B FTC settlement and $1.8B severance. North America 4.5% (5.9%;
   about 6.9% before the FTC charge), International 2.9% (3.6%), AWS 34.6% (38.1%).

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Other income $10.2B (Anthropic: $7.2B mark-up plus $2.3B reclassified gain) = 36% of pre-tax income
   $28.2B. Net income $21.2B exceeds operating income. 2025 Tax Act raised the provision.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Q3 operating cash flow $35.5B vs $26.0B; trailing free cash flow $14.8B (Q2 $18.2B; a year earlier $47.7B).
   DSO 31 days.

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Cash capex $34.2B vs $21.3B; nine months $89.9B. Capex to increase "in 2025 and 2026" (first mention of
   2026). Anthropic: $1.4B more due in Q4 2025.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Notes $54.3B. Commitments $376.0B; leases not yet commenced $75.0B. FTC settlement to be paid from cash.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   AWS 18% of sales, 66% of operating income (charges fell mostly on North America).

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   No estimate changes; severance recorded as estimated costs ($2.0B nine months).

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [MIXED: BETTER]
   FTC lawsuit settled for $2.5B. Italian court affirmed the ICA decision but cut the fine to EUR 752M
   (already paid). Antitrust suits in the U.S., Canada and U.K. continue, seeking structural relief.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   Q3 guide: sales beaten ($180.2B vs $174.0-179.5B); operating income met ($17.4B vs $15.5-20.5B) despite
   $4.3B of charges. Q4 guide: sales $206.0-213.0B, operating income $21.0-26.0B.
