Latest filing: 10-Q Q2 2026

What the Q2 2026 10-Q adds, for and against. Operating results were the strongest in the window, led by AWS; but free cash flow turned negative, borrowing doubled in six months, and two-thirds of pre-tax income came from marking up the Anthropic stake.

Bull case

P1 AWS growth and backlogStated
AWS sales +37% (Q1 +28%); AWS operating income $10.160B → $16.621B, margin 32.9% → 39.4%
Q2 2026: "AWS sales increased 37% in Q2 2026, and 33% for the six months ended June 30, 2026 compared to the comparable prior year periods."
Q2 2026: "AWS | 10,160 | 16,621 | 21,707 | 30,782"

Size: AWS sales $42.2B (+$11.4B year over year); AWS produced 61% of operating income. Up to $551M of the quarter's AWS income is unrealized energy-contract gains; without all of it the margin is still about 38%.

The fourth straight quarter of acceleration since growth bottomed at 17% in Q1 and Q2 2025. Margin expanded while capex nearly doubled, so the new capacity is being filled.

Next filing: AWS growth above 30% and margin above 35% in Q3 2026; a fall back toward 20% would refute.

P1 AWS growth and backlogStated
Backlog $364B → $496B in one quarter (a year earlier $195B); Anthropic expands its AWS commitment by more than $100.0B
Q2 2026: "For contracts with original terms that exceed one year, those commitments not yet recognized were approximately $496 billion as of June 30, 2026."
Q2 2026: "In Q2 2026, AWS and Anthropic announced an expansion of the strategic collaboration and existing multi-year commitment by more than $100.0 billion over 10.0 years, which includes contractual obligations related to the performance of AWS chips."

Size: $496B = 3.3x trailing AWS sales of about $148B; weighted-average life 6.4 years (5.5 in Q1), about $78B a year if spread evenly.

Contracted demand now covers several years of the capacity being built, which is the main answer to the free-cash-flow bear case. Its quality depends on two counterparties (see bear side).

Next filing: backlog holding near $500B as revenue is recognized against it; any cancellation or reduction language.

P2 Retail marginsStated
Operating income $27.5B beats the $20.0–24.0B guide; North America $7.517B → $9.123B, International $1.494B → $1.717B
Q2 2026: "Operating income increased from $19.2 billion in Q2 2025 to $27.5 billion in Q2 2026, and increased from $37.6 billion for the six months ended June 30, 2025 to $51.3 billion for the six months ended June 30, 2026."

Size: $3.5B above the top of guidance; about $2.3B above it even without the $640M of tariff refunds and $551M of energy gains. Consolidated margin 13.7% vs 11.4%.

Retail segments grew operating income 21% (North America) and 15% (International) on unit sales and advertising, while Leo spending ran through technology and infrastructure costs.

Next filing: Q3 2026 operating income inside or above $22.5–26.5B with no refunds.

P6 AI-lab investments and concentrationStated
Anthropic holdings marked to about $190B: preferred stock $14.8B → $92.5B, notes $45.8B → $97.9B since December
Q2 2026: "As of December 31, 2025 and June 30, 2026, the amounts recorded on our consolidated balance sheets for nonvoting preferred stock were approximately $14.8 billion and $92.5 billion."

Size: about $190B of carrying value against $8.0B of notes and $10.0B of 2026 preferred purchases; larger than 2025 operating income ($80.0B) by 2.4x. Level 3, unrealized.

The marks follow Anthropic's own priced fundings, so the stake has real economic value and could be monetized after an IPO lock-up. It is the strongest offset to the cash being spent on capex.

Next filing: further priced rounds or an IPO filing; any impairment would refute.

P4 Balance sheet and fundingStated
Liquidity held: cash and marketable securities $123.0B, no commercial paper or revolver borrowings, a new $17.5B term loan undrawn
Q2 2026: "We had no borrowings outstanding under the two unsecured revolving credit facilities or the commercial paper programs as of June 30, 2026."

Size: $20.0B of revolvers, $30.0B of commercial paper capacity and $17.5B of term loan all unused; quarterly operating income covers interest about 21 times.

Amazon raised notes in four currencies between March and July (stated rates 0.84–6.05%) and kept its backup lines untouched. Funding is a cost question, not an access question.

Next filing: whether the term loan was drawn before its September 30, 2026 deadline, and cash after the $21.3B OpenAI payment.

Bear case

Q2 2026: "Free cash flow | 18,184 | (7,604)"

The filing says capex will rise further in 2026. Free cash flow has fallen in every filing in the window, and the company's own stated focus is "long-term, sustainable growth in free cash flow".

Next filing: trailing free cash flow below −$7.6B confirms; Q4 seasonal cash inflow returning it to positive would ease it.

Next filing: other income as a share of pre-tax income; any downward adjustment.

Amazon is funding its two largest AWS commitments: $50.0B into OpenAI, $10.0B into Anthropic plus a facility that reopens as AWS delivers compute
Q2 2026: "Additionally, we entered into a financing arrangement to make available to Anthropic an aggregate facility not to exceed $20.0 billion that will expire 30 months after an Anthropic liquidity event, including an initial public offering"
Q2 2026: "Our maximum exposure to loss is generally limited to the current carrying values of these investments and any future funding commitments."

Size: OpenAI ($138B) and Anthropic (more than $100B added) commitments are at least $238B, about 48% of the $496B backlog if fully counted; private holdings $122.3B plus notes $97.9B = about 2.7x 2025 operating income.

The facility becomes available "as we reach certain delivery milestones of compute capacity", so part of the AWS revenue from Anthropic may be financed by Amazon itself. The backlog split by customer is not disclosed.

Next filing: draws on the $15.0B remaining facility, new commitments, and any customer-concentration disclosure.
