APO Q3 2024 10-Q (filed November 6, 2024; quarter ended September 30, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the trend in the Q2 2024 10-Q (first filing in the window,
so markers compare with the same quarter a year earlier). Figures from the filing text.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   FRE $531M vs $472M (+12.5%). Management fees +9.6%: Credit +20.2%, Equity -11.5% (Fund X
   catch-up fees in 2023). Total AUM $733.2B (+5.3% in the quarter); fee-generating AUM $551.0B.
   Athene gross organic inflows $56.8B in nine months (MD&A, Asset Management and Athene sections).

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   FRE margin about 57.3% of fee revenue ($926M) vs 56.6%. Net investment spread 1.83% vs 2.13%
   (-30bps); cost of funds 3.34% vs 2.67%. SRE $856M vs $873M (-1.9%).

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   $172M guaranty-association expense for the BLIC/CBLIC insolvencies (Retirement Services
   results). GAAP net income to AGM $787M vs $660M; ANI $1,132M vs $1,045M.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Nine-month operating cash flow $3,257M vs $4,258M against GAAP net income of $4,711M.
   (Operating cash flow for an insurer-asset manager mixes policyholder flows; read with caution.)

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Nine-month buybacks $788M; quarterly dividend $0.4625 declared. AGM issued $750M 5.80% 2054
   notes in May.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Total debt $9.807B. Unrestricted cash $16.3B plus $4.9B of facilities. Guaranty association
   liability $177M.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   Athene is the main fee driver: $64M of the $62M quarterly management-fee increase came from
   Athene. Atlas warehouse facilities added $11.1B of AUM leverage.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Favourable unlocking and a pension group annuity methodology refinement reduced cost of funds.
   No critical-estimate change disclosed.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [HELD]
   Harbinger ($1.9B claim, dismissed as time-barred, on appeal), Anguilla derivative suit over the
   $570M TRA payments, Frank Funds and AT&T matters continue.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   "we expect those assessments to continue for the foreseeable future" (guaranty associations);
   Athene expects to recover $5M through premium tax credits.
