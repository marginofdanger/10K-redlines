Latest filing: 10-Q Q2 2026

What the Q2 2026 10-Q adds, for and against. Fee earnings kept compounding and the spread recovered from Q1's low, but the spread is still below a year ago and mortgage-loan delinquencies rose sharply.

Bull case

P1 Asset management growthStated
FRE +25.2% to $785M; management fees +22.7%, capital solutions fees +28.2%
Q2 2026: "FRE was $785 million in 2026, an increase of $158 million compared to $627 million in 2025."
Q2 2026: "Capital solutions fees and other, net | 277 | 216 | 61 | 28.2"

Size: +$158M year over year; FRE margin 58.5% of fee revenue ($1,343M) vs 57.3%. H1 FRE $1,513M (+27.6%) already 60% of 2025's $2,528M.

Sixth straight filing of FRE growth above 20%, with margin widening even as compensation grows 22.9%. Part of the fee increase is acquired (Bridge, Athora's PIC deal), but credit fees alone rose 19.3%.

Next filing: FRE growth holding above 20% once Bridge laps its September 2025 close.

P2 Retirement services spreadStated
SRE +6.8% to $877M; spread recovers to 1.47% from Q1's 1.34%; alternative earned rate back to 9.04%
Q2 2026: "SRE was $877 million in 2026, an increase of $56 million, or 7%, compared to $821 million in 2025."

Size: +$56M; the spread gained 13bps sequentially, worth roughly $100M a quarter on Q2's net investment spread of $1,129M (arithmetic on disclosed rates).

Q1's collapse in alternative returns (5.79%) did not repeat, so part of Q1's SRE decline was a mark, not a trend. Fixed income earned 5.05% vs 4.97%.

Next filing: SRE growth in Q3 2026 without a rebound in alternative marks doing the work.

Next filing: cash held above $20B and Athene RBC disclosed for 2026 at or above 441%.
