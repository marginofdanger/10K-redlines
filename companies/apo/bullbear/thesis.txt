APOLLO GLOBAL MANAGEMENT (APO): INVESTMENT THESIS PILLARS
Written October 2026 from the 10-K dashboard themes (origination platform, Athene integration,
perpetual capital, Leon Black arc), the 10-Q scanner, and the eight filings from the Q3 2024 10-Q
to the Q2 2026 10-Q; updated November 2026 for the Q3 2026 10-Q. Stances move only when two or
more filings point the same way.

P1 Asset management growth (FRE and AUM)
  Bull assumes: fee-related earnings compound 15-25% a year as origination, wealth (ADS and other
    perpetual vehicles), Athene/Athora inflows and acquisitions (Bridge) lift fee-generating AUM,
    with FRE margin steady or rising.
  Bear fears: growth is bought (Bridge, Athora's PIC deal) rather than organic; equity fees fade
    as Fund IX/X fee periods roll off; compensation and headcount outrun revenue.
  Stance: Strengthening (FRE growth 12.5% in Q3 2024, 16.7% in FY2024, 21-23% through 2025,
    30.2% in Q1 2026, 25.2% in Q2 2026 and 23.0% in Q3 2026; AUM $733B to $1.07T). Moved at the
    Q1 2025 10-Q, the second filing in a row with FRE growth above 16%. Watch: from Q3 2026 PII
    takes capital solutions fees "earned above certain performance thresholds" out of FRE, amount
    not given.
  Tests: FRE growth and margin, management fee growth by strategy, organic subscriptions vs
    acquired AUM, redemptions.

P2 Retirement services spread (Athene SRE)
  Bull assumes: Athene's inflows ($71B in 2024, $83B in 2025) keep growing net invested assets,
    and asset yields keep pace with crediting rates so SRE grows with assets.
  Bear fears: cost of funds keeps rising (institutional mix, higher crediting rates, DAC
    amortization) faster than yields; the net investment spread compresses and SRE stalls.
  Stance: Weakening (net investment spread lower year over year in every filing covered:
    1.83% vs 2.13% in Q3 2024, 1.78% vs 1.93% for 2024, 1.61% vs 1.78% for 2025, 1.34% vs 1.65%
    in Q1 2026, 1.47% vs 1.58% in Q2 2026, 1.38% vs 1.60% in Q3 2026; SRE -3.1% in Q3 2026;
    inflows down 23% in Q1 2026, 11% in H1 2026 and 13% in the nine months). Moved at the FY2024
    10-K, the second filing in a row with spread down.
  Tests: net investment spread, cost of funds rate, SRE growth, gross organic inflows.

P3 Earnings quality
  Bull assumes: adjusted net income (ANI) reflects recurring fee and spread earnings; GAAP noise
    comes from non-cash marks and tax items that reverse.
  Bear fears: alternative investment income depends on valuation marks of affiliated platforms
    (Atlas, Wheels, Venerable, Athora); GAAP earnings diverge from ANI; one-off charges recur.
  Stance: Weakening (FY2025: GAAP net income to AGM -24% while ANI +14%, alternative income
    helped by an "initial mark from cost to fair value" on Atlas; Q1 2026: alternative earned
    rate 10.08% -> 5.79% on an Atlas impairment, $1.7B tax charge, GAAP loss $1.93B; Q3 2026:
    GAAP loss $(1,057)M vs ANI $1,293M, $515M Athene unlocking charge, $(3.4)B investment-related
    losses, an unnamed origination asset written down). Moved at the Q1 2026 10-Q.
  Tests: alternative net investment earned rate, GAAP vs ANI gap, transaction and other
    non-operating costs.

P4 Athene portfolio credit quality
  Bull assumes: the portfolio stays investment-grade (97%+ of AFS in NAIC 1-2) and private
    origination produces yield without extra losses.
  Bear fears: commercial and residential mortgage loans, affiliated origination platforms and
    related-party asset purchases hide credit losses that surface with a lag.
  Stance: Weakening (CML 90+ days/non-accrual principal $195M at end-2024 -> $992M at end-2025
    -> $1,027M at June 2026 -> $1,393M at September 2026; RML $826M -> $1,912M -> $2,060M;
    mortgage fair-value mark -$725M -> -$1,623M -> -$3,402M; Atlas impairment in Q1 2026; $8.7B
    CML portfolio bought from affiliate ARI; Q3 2026 concentration table names Paramount Skydance
    $3.6B, SoftBank Group and SVF II Finco $4.8B, Valor Compute Infrastructure $2.8B; Big Sky
    $691M held and $4.9B committed; Atlas-affiliate AFS $6.5B). Moved at the Q2 2026 10-Q.
  Tests: delinquent and non-accrual mortgage loans, fair-value marks, NAIC 1-2 share,
    related-party purchases.

P5 Balance sheet and liquidity
  Bull assumes: large cash balances, Athene RBC above 400%, long-dated holding-company debt and
    surrender protection keep liquidity risk low.
  Bear fears: debt grows faster than earnings; funding agreements and institutional liabilities
    raise run risk.
  Stance: Intact, under watch (total debt $9.81B at Sept 2024 -> $14.75B at Sept 2026, but
    unrestricted cash $16.3B -> $21.3B, RBC 441% vs 430%, surrender-protected deferred annuities
    87%). Q3 2026 bear: AGM stockholders' equity $21.0B -> $15.9B on AOCI $(3.2)B -> $(7.0)B and a
    GAAP loss, while commitments rose (Big Sky $6.2B, Mubadala $1.25B, ACMP $500M CDS). Q2 2026
    pointed the other way (debt repaid, cash up), so the stance holds; a second filing like Q3
    would move it to Weakening.
  Tests: total debt, unrestricted cash, RBC ratios, surrender protection, maturities.

P6 Capital return and dilution
  Bull assumes: the dividend grows each year and buybacks offset equity issuance.
  Bear fears: stock-funded acquisitions, charitable share issuance, mandatory convertible
    conversion and equity compensation grow the share count faster than buybacks shrink it.
  Stance: Intact (dividend intention $2.04 -> $2.25, held through Q3 2026's GAAP loss; buyback
    authorization $3.0B -> $4.0B; nine-month 2026 buybacks $847M; against 9.4M shares for Bridge,
    $200M of stock to charity, 14.6M shares from the mandatory convertible and 1.04M for the
    employee-partnership buyout; Q2-Q3 2026 buybacks only 1.9M shares; ANI shares 613.1M ->
    623.5M in 2025 -> 624.1M at Sept 2026).
  Tests: dividend declared vs intention, buybacks, ANI shares outstanding.

P7 Legal, regulatory and tax
  Bull assumes: legacy founder-era litigation winds down; tax structure (Bermuda) is favourable.
  Bear fears: Leon Black / Epstein matters return as securities litigation; tax authorities
    (Germany) assess; Bermuda tax changes force write-offs.
  Stance: Intact (Q1 2026 bull: Harbinger's $1.9B claim ended when the Court of Appeals denied
    leave on April 21, 2026; Q2 2026 bull: Anguilla derivative suit in mediation; bear: two
    Epstein-related securities class actions filed March and April 2026, a possible material
    German tax assessment first disclosed in the FY2025 10-K, $1.7B Bermuda DTA written off;
    Q3 2026: no new matters, oral argument on the stockholders-agreement suit November 17, 2026).
  Tests: status of the securities class actions, Anguilla mediation, German assessment.
