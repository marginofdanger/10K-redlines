APPLOVIN FY2024 10-K (filed February 27, 2025; year ended December 31, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q3 2024 10-Q. Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $4.709B, +43%. Advertising revenue $3.224B, +75%: installations +50%, net revenue per
   installation +22% (MD&A, Revenue). Apps revenue $1.485B, +3%.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Adjusted EBITDA $2.720B, margin 57.8% vs 45.8%. Operating income $1.87B (XBRL), 40% of revenue.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $1.580B. Income tax was a $3.8M benefit on $1.58B pre-tax income (XBRL): the provision
   fell on "an increase of $172.9 million of stock-based compensation benefit". Loss on debt
   extinguishment $28.4M.

4. Cash conversion [HELD]
   Operating cash flow $2.099B; FCF $2.073B = 131% of net income. Operating assets and liabilities
   absorbed $349.5M; DSO 110 days vs 106 (XBRL).

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]

   cover employee award taxes than to buybacks. Authorization +$3.3B in 2024, $2.3B left, capped each
   quarter at prior-quarter FCF.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [BETTER]

   revolver replaced the secured term loans ($4.2B repaid). Covenant: net debt / EBITDA at most 3.50x.
   Cash $741.4M. Cloud commitment $1.3B over three years.

7. Concentration [HELD]

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
