APPLOVIN FY2025 10-K (filed February 19, 2026; year ended December 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2024 10-K (prior-year figures as restated for the Apps
sale). Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $5.481B, +70% (2024 +75%): installations +3%, net revenue per installation +72%. Volume
   growth slowed from +50% to +3%; growth is now almost all price per installation.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Operating income $4.152B = 76% of revenue (59%). Adjusted EBITDA $4.512B, margin 82.3% vs 74.8%.
   Cost of revenue +28% vs revenue +70%. Headcount 898 vs 1,563 (Apps sale). G&A +42%: professional
   services +$31.6M, bad debt +$24.5M "primarily related to new initiatives".

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Tax $519.7M = 13.1% vs 1.4%: higher pre-tax income, Pillar 2 ($82.7M off the Singapore benefit),
   smaller SBC benefit. Net loss from discontinued operations $99.4M (goodwill impairment $188.9M).
   Valuation allowance +$215.7M.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   OCF $3.971B; FCF $3.952B = 119% of net income ($3.334B). DSO 121 days (XBRL, year-end).

5. Capital allocation [BETTER]
   Buybacks $2.2B (5.5M shares) + withholding taxes $392.4M = 66% of FCF; $3.3B authorization left.
   Diluted shares 342.0M (-1.7%).

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [BETTER]
   Cash $2.49B vs $3.6B notes (net debt about $1.1B, 0.25x Adjusted EBITDA). Revolver undrawn.
   Remaining purchase commitments $702.8M.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   No customer at 10% of revenue or receivables. United States 52%. Tripledot related-party revenue
   $19.0M since closing.

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Unrecognized SBC $489.0M vs $145.6M (new grants, 1.95-year period). New critical estimate: equity
   method investments (Tripledot).

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Motion to dismiss filed November 2025, hearing March 2026. New risk language: the company has been
   "the target of incomplete, inaccurate, and misleading or false statements". Google retired Privacy
   Sandbox.

10. Guidance and commitments [WORSE]
   The Q3 2025 statement that Axon Ads Manager would "launch more broadly in the first half of 2026"
   was removed; risk factors now say only that "since 2024 we have begun expanding our customer base
   to include web-based e-commerce advertisers".
