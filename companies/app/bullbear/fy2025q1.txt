APPLOVIN Q1 2025 10-Q (filed May 7, 2025; quarter ended March 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2024 10-K. Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $1.484B, +40%. Advertising revenue $1.159B, +71%: net revenue per installation +49%,
   installations +22% (MD&A). Apps revenue $325.0M vs $379.7M (-14%).

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Adjusted EBITDA about $1.0B, margin 67.7% vs 51.9%.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $576.4M after a $188.9M goodwill impairment on the Apps reporting unit (term sheet to
   sell to Tripledot). Excluding it, net income would be higher; the impairment is non-cash.

4. Cash conversion [HELD]
   Operating cash flow $831.7M; FCF $825.7M = 143% of net income (lifted by the non-cash impairment).
   Receivables $1.58B; DSO 124 days (XBRL).

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Buybacks $1.0B (2.9M shares) exceeded FCF; February 2025 Board change made $500M "immediately
   available" notwithstanding the FCF cap; $200.0M revolver draw "to fund share repurchases".
   Withholding taxes $185.7M.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Revolver drawn $200.0M (repaid $100M in April, $100M in May); availability $793.7M. Cash $551.0M.
   Notes $3.55B unchanged.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   United States 55% of revenue. Apps sale agreed May 7: $150.0M cash, $250.0M 11% promissory note,
   about 20% of Tripledot.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Interim goodwill test on Apps (Level 3 DCF). No other changes.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   New: three securities class actions (from March 5, 2025) and a derivative suit, alleging false
   statements about advertising solutions and growth; class period May 10, 2023 to March 26, 2025.
   No loss estimate.

10. Guidance and commitments [WORSE]
   The FCF cap on buybacks (FY2024 10-K) was loosened.
