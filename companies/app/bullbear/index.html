Latest filing: 10-Q Q2 2026

What the Q2 2026 10-Q adds, for and against. Revenue and margins kept compounding; the new weak spots are cash conversion in the quarter and the return of stock-based compensation.

Bull case

P1 Ad engine growth and pricingStated
Revenue +53% to $1.924B on net revenue per installation +58%; the installation decline narrows to −2% from −18% in Q1
Q2 2026: "For the three months ended June 30, 2026, our revenue increased by $664.9 million, or 53%, compared to the same period in the prior year due primarily to improved AppLovin Ads performance, where net revenue per installation increased 58%, partially offset by a decrease in the volume of installations of 2%."

Size: +$664.9M year over year; +4.4% sequentially ($1.842B → $1.924B). Rest of world +56%, United States +50%.

Revenue still grows by half a year after the Apps exit, and the volume drag that dominated Q1 largely faded.

Next filing: installations back to growth, or revenue growth holding above 40% if they do not.

P2 Margins and operating leverageStated
Adjusted EBITDA margin 83.9% vs 80.9%; G&A down 27% as bad debt falls
Q2 2026: "Adjusted EBITDA margin | 83.9% | 80.9% | 84.2% | 80.9%"
Q2 2026: "General and administrative expenses in the three months ended June 30, 2026 decreased by $14.7 million, or 27%, compared to the same period in the prior year, due to a decrease of $9.5 million in bad debt expense"

Size: Adjusted EBITDA $1.614B (+58%); operating income $1.494B = 78% of revenue (76%). Total costs and expenses 22% of revenue (24%).

Incremental revenue converts to profit at about 90 cents on the dollar; the bad-debt problem from 2025's "new initiatives" is reversing.

Next filing: margin at or above 83% with cost of revenue growing no faster than revenue.

P4 Cash conversion and capital returnStated
Six-month buybacks $1.5B inside $2.15B of free cash flow; diluted shares −1.5%
Q2 2026: "During the six months ended June 30, 2026, we repurchased and retired 3.3 million shares of Class A common stock for $1.5 billion."
Q2 2026: "Free Cash Flow | 2,150,065 | 1,593,794"

Size: buybacks = 70% of six-month FCF; withholding taxes on awards only $46.5M (H1 2025: $256.7M). Diluted shares 337.0M vs 342.2M; $1.8B authorization left.

Capital return stays inside internally generated cash, and the cash cost of net share settlement has collapsed.

Next filing: buybacks still within FCF; a new authorization once the $1.8B is used.

P5 Balance sheet and liquidityStated
Cash $3.1B against $3.6B of notes; revolver undrawn
Q2 2026: "As of June 30, 2026, we had cash and cash equivalents of $3.1 billion, consisting primarily of cash in checking and interest-bearing deposit accounts, as well as investments in money market funds."

Size: net debt about $0.5B = 0.08x annualized Adjusted EBITDA ($6.5B); interest income rose $21.3M in the quarter.

The company is close to net-cash with fixed-rate debt maturing from 2029; the 3.50x leverage covenant is irrelevant at this level.

Next filing: cash above the notes balance, or a stated use for it.

Bear case

Q2 2026: "Net cash provided by operating activities was $2.2 billion for the six months ended June 30, 2026, primarily consisting of $2.5 billion of net income, adjusted for certain non-cash items"
Q2 2026: "which were partially offset by a net decrease in operating assets and liabilities of $504.8 million"

Size: Q2 operating cash flow about $869M vs $1.291B in Q1 (six-month total less Q1); $490M of the $504.8M working-capital drain fell in Q2. Receivables $1.96B → $2.17B; DSO 97 → 103 days (XBRL).

P3 Earnings qualityStated
Stock-based compensation $85.8M vs $34.6M (+148%); R&D +127%, all of it SBC payroll
Q2 2026: "Research and development expenses in the three months ended June 30, 2026 increased by $55.9 million, or 127%, compared to the same period in the prior year, due primarily to an increase of $54.8 million in personnel-related expenses related to an increase in stock-based compensation-related payroll costs."

Size: SBC = 4.5% of revenue (2.7%) and 5.7% of pre-tax income; it is excluded from Adjusted EBITDA. The FY2025 10-K put unrecognized SBC at $489.0M over 1.95 years.

The 2025 margin story partly reflected SBC falling to $208M after the 2024 PSU cycle; new grants are now running back through the income statement.

Next filing: SBC near $85M a quarter (on schedule) or higher (new grants); share count still falling net of awards.

P1 Ad engine growth and pricingStated
Growth slows for a fourth straight filing; six-month installation volume −10%
Q2 2026: "For the six months ended June 30, 2026, our revenue increased by $1.3 billion, or 56%, compared to the same period in the prior year due primarily to improved AppLovin Ads performance, where net revenue per installation increased 75%, partially offset by a decrease in the volume of installations of 10%."

Size: quarterly growth 77% (Q2 2025) → 68% → 59% → 53%; all of the growth is price per installation.

Revenue now depends on extracting more per install from a shrinking install base, which has a ceiling the filing does not discuss.

Next filing: installation volume growth, or a disclosed contribution from e-commerce / web advertisers.

P3 Earnings qualityStated

Q2 2026: "The increase was primarily driven by higher pre-tax income from business operations during the three months ended June 30, 2026, foreign income taxed at different rates, and a decrease in stock-based compensation benefits"
Q2 2026: "due primarily to a net fair value remeasurement gain of $31.3 million related to certain non-marketable equity securities in the current period"

Size: net income from continuing operations +64% vs pre-tax income +70%; the 3.2-point rate rise cost about $48M in the quarter. Other income $62.4M = 4.1% of pre-tax income.

Net income grows slower than operations as the stock-award tax shield shrinks; a small part of pre-tax income is marks on private-fund stakes.

Next filing: effective rate stable near 16%; other income without fair-value gains.
