APPLOVIN (APP): INVESTMENT THESIS PILLARS
Written October 2026 from the 10-K dashboard themes (AXON/AI engine, Apps arc, EBITDA margin
trajectory, capital structure, e-commerce expansion), the 10-Q scanner, and the eight filings from
the Q3 2024 10-Q to the Q2 2026 10-Q. A stance moves only when two or more filings point the same way.
Baseline: every pillar started the window (October 2024) at Intact.

P1 Ad engine growth and pricing
  Bull assumes: Axon keeps raising advertiser returns, so net revenue per installation and total
    ad revenue keep compounding well above the ad market, first in gaming and then beyond it.
  Bear fears: growth is all price and no volume; installation volume stalls as mobile gaming
    saturates, and the growth rate keeps decaying toward the market.
  Stance: Strengthening (moved at the FY2024 10-K: Software Platform / Advertising revenue +66% in
    Q3 2024 and +75% for 2024; then +71%, +77%, +68%, +70% (FY2025), +59%, +53%). Held there despite
    installation volume falling in Q3 2025 (-1%), Q1 2026 (-18%) and Q2 2026 (-2%), because revenue
    growth in the same filings stayed above 50%.
  Tests: revenue growth, net revenue per installation, installation volume, sequential revenue.

P2 Margins and operating leverage
  Bull assumes: a lean, software-only cost base (datacenter, a few hundred engineers) turns most
    incremental revenue into EBITDA; exiting Apps lifted margins structurally.
  Bear fears: datacenter costs and stock-based compensation rise faster than revenue once the
    pricing gains slow.
  Stance: Strengthening (moved at the Q2 2025 10-Q: Adjusted EBITDA margin 67.7% in Q1 2025 and
    80.9% in Q2 2025 after the Apps exit; 82.4%, 82.3%, 84.5%, 83.9% since).
  Tests: Adjusted EBITDA and operating margin, cost of revenue growth vs revenue, SBC.

P3 Earnings quality
  Bull assumes: continuing-operations earnings are clean: impairments and the Apps loss sit in
    discontinued operations, and operating income is 76-78% of revenue.
  Bear fears: earlier EPS was flattered by a near-zero tax rate from stock-award deductions; tax
    normalizes (Pillar 2, smaller SBC benefit) and stock-based compensation, excluded from Adjusted
    EBITDA, climbs again.
  Stance: Weakening (moved at the Q1 2026 10-Q: FY2025 tax rate 13.1% vs 1.4% in 2024 (continuing operations) and
    unrecognized SBC $145.6M -> $489.0M; Q1 2026 tax +$154.7M on a smaller SBC benefit and R&D SBC
    payroll +$36.5M; Q2 2026 SBC $85.8M vs $34.6M and tax +$126.8M).
  Tests: effective tax rate, SBC expense and unrecognized balance, non-operating gains in other income.

P4 Cash conversion and capital return
  Bull assumes: free cash flow at or above net income funds buybacks that shrink the share count,
    without borrowing.
  Bear fears: buybacks outrun free cash flow (as in Q1 2025, when the FCF cap was loosened and the
    revolver drawn), receivables build, and net share settlement of awards eats the cash.
  Stance: Strengthening (moved at the FY2025 10-K: Q3 2025 and FY2025 both show buybacks inside
    free cash flow with cash rising; FY2025 FCF $3.95B = 119% of net income).
  Tests: FCF vs net income, receivables / DSO, buybacks and withholding taxes vs FCF, diluted shares.

P5 Balance sheet and liquidity
  Bull assumes: $3.6B of fixed-rate unsecured notes (2029-2054) with an undrawn $1.0B revolver and a
    cash pile that now nearly matches debt.
  Bear fears: debt-funded buybacks or a large acquisition (the TikTok indication of interest in
    2025) re-lever the company.
  Stance: Strengthening (moved at the Q2 2025 10-Q: FY2024 10-K refinancing of secured term loans
    into unsecured notes; Q2 2025 revolver repaid and cash doubled to $1.19B; cash $3.05B by Q2 2026).
  Tests: cash vs $3.6B notes, revolver usage, net debt / EBITDA covenant (3.50x).

P6 Expansion beyond gaming and concentration
  Bull assumes: the Apps sale makes APP a pure ad platform; e-commerce / web advertisers and a
    self-serve Axon Ads Manager widen the customer base; no customer is 10% of revenue.
  Bear fears: the business remains a mobile-gaming ad network dependent on Apple and Google
    policies; new verticals bring credit losses and the self-serve launch slips.
  Stance: Intact (bull: Apps sold for $430.6M cash plus 22% of Tripledot; bear: bad debt from "new
    initiatives" in Q2 2025, Q3 2025 and FY2025; the "launch more broadly in the first half of 2026"
    sentence of Q3 2025 dropped from the FY2025 10-K; Tripledot is now a related-party customer).
  Tests: e-commerce disclosure, bad debt, related-party revenue, geographic mix, platform policies.

P7 Legal, regulatory and reputation
  Bull assumes: short-seller claims and the follow-on suits fail; privacy regimes (ATT, Privacy
    Sandbox) do not impair targeting.
  Bear fears: the securities class action or a regulatory inquiry into data practices succeeds;
    reputation damage deters advertisers.
  Stance: Weakening (moved at the Q3 2025 10-Q: Q1 2025 securities and derivative suits filed; Q3
    2025 amended complaint plus new risk language on "improper advertising practices" and misuse of
    user data; FY2025 adds "misleading or false statements" about the company). Bull offsets: two of
    three complaints voluntarily dismissed (Q2 2025); Google retired Privacy Sandbox (Q3 2025/FY2025).
  Tests: ruling on the motion to dismiss (fully briefed February 2026), any government inquiry,
    loss accrual.
