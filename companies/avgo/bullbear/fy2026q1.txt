AVGO Q1 FY2026 10-Q (quarter ended February 1, 2026, filed March 2026): FUNDAMENTALS ANSWERS
Compared with the FY2025 10-K (and the year-ago quarter where the filing
gives it). Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL (financials.py).

1. Growth and pricing: BETTER in semiconductors, WORSE in software. Revenue
   $19,311M (+29% YoY). Semiconductor $12,515M (+52%), "primarily custom AI
   accelerators and AI networking products." Software $6,796M (+1%); excluding
   upfront license ($1,755M vs $1,972M) software grew about 7% (our
   arithmetic). RPO $33.3B -> $45.0B, 33% within 12 months (35%).

2. Margins and unit economics: HELD. Gross margin 68% (flat): "The gross
   margin benefit from our net revenue growth was offset by a higher mix of
   semiconductor solutions net revenue." Operating margin 44% (42%). R&D +32%
   on SBC. Segment margins: semiconductor 60%, software 78%.

3. Earnings quality: HELD. Other income includes a one-off $315M gain from
   reversing the VMware excise tax (4% of pre-tax income). Tax $846M (10.3%)
   is a normal-looking charge after FY2025's one-off benefits.

4. Cash conversion: HELD. OCF $8,260M; FCF $8.01B (109% of NI). DSO 41 -> 40
   days (quarterly basis); receivables $7,145M -> $8,460M on "higher
   billings and the timing of collections"; factoring $1,750M. Inventory
   $2,270M -> $2,961M.

5. Capital allocation: WORSE on funding. Buybacks $7,850M (23M shares), the
   largest in the window, plus dividends $3,086M ($0.65/share): $10.9B, 137%
   of FCF (our arithmetic). Cash fell $2,004M and debt rose. Diluted shares
   still rose to 4.89B (4.85B FY2025 average). Capex $250M.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure: WORSE. Debt $67,120M -> $67,970M
   ($4,500M of notes issued, $3,650M repaid). Risk factors and liquidity now
   say Broadcom "may agree to financial obligations, including guarantees"
   and that rack arrangements "may impose financial obligations, including
   guarantees." Purchase commitments only $54M.

7. Concentration: WORSE. Distributor 29% -> 42% of quarterly revenue; top
   five ~40% -> ~50%; distributors 55% of revenue (48% in FY2025).

8. Accounting judgments: HELD. "No significant changes" in critical
   estimates. Upfront license again reclassified to products. 67% of contract
   liabilities on terminable contracts.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance: HELD. No accrued loss contingencies.
   New risk factor on AI-native software competitors. Customers may be
   designated a "supply chain risk" by governments (new).

10. Guidance and commitments: FY2025 10-K items: global minimum tax "has
   materially increased our provision" (happened); capex higher in FY2026
   (on track: $250M vs $100M); $10B buyback extended and increased to $11B,
   $700M left, plus a new $10B program in March 2026; $0.65 dividend paid
   and declared again March 3, 2026; RPO conversion moved from 35% to 33%.
