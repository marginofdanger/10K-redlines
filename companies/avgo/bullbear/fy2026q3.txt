AVGO Q3 FY2026 10-Q (quarter ended August 2, 2026): FUNDAMENTALS ANSWERS
Compared with the Q2 FY2026 10-Q. Figures from the filing text and financials.py.

1. Growth and pricing: BETTER. Revenue $29,591M (+86% YoY; Q2 $22,187M).
   Semiconductor $20,839M (+127%) "primarily custom AI accelerators and AI
   networking products" (MD&A Net Revenue). Software $8,752M (+29%, Q2 +9%),
   of which upfront license $3,465M. RPO $164.6B -> $179.2B, with 25% due in 12
   months (30% in Q2) (Note 2). Pricing vs volume is not broken out.

2. Margins and unit economics: BETTER. Gross margin held at 69% even though
   the semiconductor mix rose to 70% of revenue. Operating margin 54% (Q2 49%).
   Segment operating margins: semiconductor 61%, software 84%. R&D fell 5% YoY
   on "a decrease in headcount" (MD&A). No rack revenue is broken out yet.

3. Earnings quality: HELD. Other income includes a $315M reversal of the
   VMware excise tax for the year to date (MD&A Non-Operating). Upfront license
   revenue ($3,465M) is recognized at signing on non-cancellable contracts. Tax
   provision $2,187M (14.3%), so less of the profit comes from tax benefits.

4. Cash conversion: HELD. OCF $14,197M vs net income $13.09B. FCF $13.66B
   (104% of NI; 110% in Q2). Receivables $10,830M -> $13,707M, DSO 44 -> 42
   days. Factoring $600M -> $1,600M, and MD&A no longer cites factoring.
   Withholding taxes are settled by selling vested shares (sell-to-cover,
   since Q3 FY2025), which removes a cash outflow ($3,860M in the prior year
   to date).

5. Capital allocation: MIXED. No buybacks in the quarter (Q2 $600M; $8,450M
   YTD; $10.1B authorized and remaining). $5,641M of senior notes retired.
   Dividends $3,103M (quarterly $0.65/share). Capex $532M (Q2 $231M). Capital
   is being directed to deleveraging and the AI XPV platform rather than
   buybacks.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure: WORSE on contingent, BETTER on debt.
   Debt principal $66,720M -> $61,079M; cash $23,975M. A $29B Backstop
   (undiscounted, at full deployment), now in Note 10 with an 85% loss
   formula. Up to $42B of customer convertible notes ("if needed"; none
   issued). Purchase commitments $126,821M, mostly FY2027-28 (Note 10). Item
   (viii) "potential financing arrangements" is added to liquidity needs, and
   the credit-rating factor now cites backstops.

7. Concentration: WORSE. One distributor is 50% of quarterly revenue (42% in
   Q2). Top five end customers ~55% (~45%). "We expect to continue to
   experience significant customer concentration in future periods." (MD&A)

8. Accounting judgments: HELD. "No significant changes in our critical
   accounting estimates." The Backstop's fair value is "not material."
   Amortization is now based on the "net book value of intangible assets."

9. Legal, regulatory and governance: HELD. No accrued loss contingencies
   (Note 10). Antitrust inquiries in Korea, Japan and the EU carried forward.
   An AI "bubble" is named in the risk factors. Apollo (named in Q2) is now
   "a financial partner."

10. Guidance and commitments: FY2025 10-K items. Capex higher in FY2026: met
   ($1,013M YTD vs $623M for FY2025). Global minimum tax to "materially
   increase" the FY2026 rate: happened (14.3% Q3; $2,186M payable). $0.65
   quarterly dividend: met. $10B buyback through Dec 2026: enlarged (to $11B,
   plus a new $10B in March 2026). OBBBA "material impact" on FY2026: CAMT
   sentences deleted, not mentioned.
