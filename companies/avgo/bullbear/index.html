What this filing adds, for and against. 3-6 of the most material findings per side; a side with nothing material says so.

Bull case

P1 AI semiconductor growthStated
Semiconductor revenue $9,166M → $20,839M year over year (+127%); management cites "unprecedented demand"
Q3 FY2026: "We see unprecedented demand for our custom AI accelerators or XPUs and AI networking solutions from our customers, including the leading frontier AI labs, due to the demand for AI compute."

Size: Semiconductor segment operating income was $12,770M (+145% YoY), equal to 80% of total operating income. Total revenue was $29,591M, up from $22,187M in Q2.

The AI franchise is growing faster than at any point in the filings covered, and with operating leverage. The growth is attributed to custom accelerators and networking, not to one-off items.

Next filing: semiconductor revenue holds or grows without any draw on the Backstop or any customer notes issued.

P1 AI semiconductor growthStated
RPO $164.6B → $179.2B, including a long-term custom AI accelerator contract
Q3 FY2026: "...the remaining performance obligations under these contracts as of August 2, 2026 were approximately $179.2 billion. These commitments include obligations under a long-term contract for custom AI accelerators entered in the fiscal quarter ended May 3, 2026."

Size: $179.2B is 2.8x FY2025 revenue ($63.9B). It was $33.3B at FY2025 year-end and $45.0B at Q1 FY2026.

Committed backlog gives multi-year visibility that a cyclical chip business rarely has. Most of it is firmly committed rather than cancellable.

Next filing: RPO holds above $170B, and the next-12-month share stops falling.

P4 Margins and operating leverageStated
Gross margin 69% (67% a year earlier) even as semiconductors reach 70% of revenue; operating margin 54%
Q3 FY2026: "The increases were primarily due to the gross margin benefit from our net revenue growth, partially offset by a higher mix of semiconductor solutions net revenue, which has a lower gross margin than infrastructure software."

Size: Operating income was $15,955M against $5,887M a year earlier. Total opex fell from $4,816M to $4,501M while revenue rose 86%.

The mix shift that the FY2025 10-K warned would compress gross margin has not yet done so. Opex fell in absolute terms (R&D -5%, SG&A -7%) on lower headcount.

Next filing: gross margin holds near 69% once rack and system revenue starts to be recognized.

P3 Infrastructure software (VMware)Stated
Software revenue +29% YoY to $8,752M; most new contracts can no longer be cancelled for convenience
Q3 FY2026: "As of the fiscal quarter ended August 2, 2026, we no longer have termination for convenience provisions for the majority of new software contracts."

Size: Software segment operating income was $7,325M (+40% YoY), an 84% margin. Software growth was 9% YoY in Q2.

Non-cancellable contracts are firmer revenue: the share of contract liabilities that customers could cancel fell from 64% to 58%. This is the VMware strategy's subscription conversion showing up in contract terms.

Next filing: software growth stays positive once the upfront license comparisons annualize.

P6 Balance sheet and contingent exposureStated
$5,641M of senior notes retired from cash flow; debt principal $66,720M → $61,079M
Q3 FY2026: "We generated $14,197 million of cash from operations."
Q3 FY2026: "We repurchased $5,641 million of our senior notes."

Size: Debt fell 8% in the quarter. Cash rose to $23,975M, so net debt is about $37B, roughly 0.6x annualized Q3 operating income (our arithmetic).

On-balance-sheet leverage is falling fast and is funded internally. Free cash flow was $13.66B against $3,103M of dividends.

Next filing: debt keeps falling or holds while the dividend is maintained.

Bear case

P6 Balance sheet and contingent exposureStated
$29B lease Backstop, plus up to $42B of convertible notes the lease customer may issue to Broadcom
Q3 FY2026: "Our maximum potential liability under the Backstop upon the deployment of all AI racks, on an undiscounted basis, was approximately $29 billion."
Q3 FY2026: "...our customer may, under certain circumstances and if needed, issue to us convertible promissory notes up to an aggregate principal amount of $42 billion."

Size: $29B is 114% of FY2025 operating income ($25.5B) and 47% of debt principal. $42B is 66% of FY2025 revenue. The Backstop's fair value is "not material," and nothing has been paid or issued.

Broadcom may both guarantee one customer's lease payments and lend that customer the money to make them (our inference from adjacent paragraphs). The credit risk of the AI build-out moves onto Broadcom's balance sheet as contingent claims, even as reported debt falls.

Next filing: whether any notes are issued, any Backstop amount paid or accrued, or a second XPV tranche added.

P2 Customer concentration and counterparty creditStated
One distributor is 50% of quarterly revenue (42% in Q2); top five end customers ~55% (~45%)
Q3 FY2026: "Direct sales to one semiconductor solutions customer, which is a distributor, accounted for 50% and 46% of our net revenue for the fiscal quarter and three fiscal quarters ended August 2, 2026, respectively"

Size: About $14.8B of the quarter's revenue goes through one distributor (our arithmetic). Its share was 21% in FY2023.

Concentration has risen across the window, from 28% of FY2024 revenue; it paused only in Q2 FY2025 and Q2 FY2026. The FY2025 10-K deleted its statement that most sales come from a few customers in the same year this share reached 32%.

Next filing: the distributor and top-five shares stop rising, or a new large customer is quantified.

P6 Balance sheet and contingent exposureInferred
$126,821M of unconditional purchase commitments, $125.6B of it due in FY2027–FY2028; CM capacity commitments now possible
Q3 FY2026: "Purchase Commitments. Represent unconditional purchase commitments to purchase goods or services, primarily inventory, that are enforceable and legally binding on us"
Q3 FY2026: "We may enter into long-term capacity commitments with our CMs, but generally our manufacturing services are on a purchase order basis with no minimum quantities."

Size: $126.8B is 2.0x FY2025 revenue. The figure was $132M at FY2025 year-end (XBRL) and jumped in Q2 FY2026.

The commitments are binding while only 25% of the $179.2B RPO is due within a year. If an AI customer cuts orders, which the risk factors now tie to "delayed or cancelled AI infrastructure buildouts," Broadcom still owes its suppliers (inference).

Next filing: commitments fall as inventory is received and shipped, and are matched by RPO conversion.

P2 Customer concentration and counterparty creditInferred

Q3 FY2026: "...which may not be sustainable due to a market correction, the perceived existence of an AI spending "bubble" or other factors"
Q3 FY2026: "Where necessary, we may provide residual value guarantees, which are contingent liabilities we believe would have a low probability of occurring, supported by the strong profitability trajectory of the leading frontier AI labs"

Size: not quantified in the filing. The XPV program targets more than 20 GW, against more than 1 GW in the $35B first tranche.

Management's "low probability" judgment on the guarantees depends on the same customer profitability the risk factors flag as uncertain.

Next filing: whether residual value guarantees are actually given and how they are sized.

P3 Infrastructure software (VMware)Inferred
Upfront license revenue $1,964M → $3,465M, 40% of software revenue, booked at signing
Q3 FY2026: "We included upfront license revenue of $3,465 million and $7,184 million within products revenue for the fiscal quarter and three fiscal quarters ended August 2, 2026, respectively."

Size: $1,501M of the $1,574M quarter-over-quarter rise in software revenue was upfront license. Other current assets, mainly software contract assets, rose to $9,968M.

Part of the software acceleration (9% to 29% YoY) reflects when revenue is recognized, not only demand, and it creates harder comparisons later (inference).

Next filing: software revenue excluding upfront license, and whether contract assets convert to cash.

P7 Tax rateStated
First figure for the global minimum tax: $2,186M payable; quarterly rate 8.1% → 14.3%
Q3 FY2026: "(a) Included $2,186 million global minimum tax payable as of August 2, 2026."

Size: the year-to-date provision is $3,853M on about $33.6B of pre-tax income (11.5%), against a -1.7% rate for FY2025. The quarter's 14.3% is still below Q3 FY2025's 21.7%.

The low-tax era the FY2025 10-K warned about has ended. Each point of tax rate is now worth about $150M a quarter at the current pre-tax run rate (our arithmetic).

Next filing: the FY2026 full-year rate, and the CAMT position (the CAMT sentences were deleted this quarter).
