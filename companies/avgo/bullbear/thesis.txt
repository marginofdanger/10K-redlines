AVGO (Broadcom) INVESTMENT THESIS: PILLARS
==========================================
Written October 2026 from the 10-K dashboard themes and tracked signals (AI
language, VMware arc, customer concentration, AI-rack purchase and lease arc,
debt and capital returns, tax arc) and from the FY2025 10-K and Q3 FY2026 10-Q.
Stances follow BULLBEAR.txt: a stance moves only when findings point the same
way across two or more filings.

P1 AI semiconductor growth
  Bull assumes: custom AI accelerators (XPUs) and AI networking keep growing
    for years on multi-year contracts with hyperscalers and frontier AI labs;
    backlog (RPO) converts to revenue.
  Bear fears: AI spending is a cycle or a "bubble"; customers design their own
    silicon ("customer-owned tooling"); growth depends on financing Broadcom
    itself arranges.
  Stance: Strengthening (FY2025 10-K and Q3 FY2026 10-Q both show accelerating
    semiconductor revenue and rising RPO).
  Tested by: semiconductor segment revenue and operating income, RPO and its
    next-12-month share, customer-count language, the "bubble" risk factor.

P2 Customer concentration and counterparty credit
  Bull assumes: the few large customers are creditworthy hyperscalers, and
    concentration is the price of winning the biggest AI programs.
  Bear fears: one distributor and five end customers are most of revenue; some
    AI customers "may be unable to pay"; Broadcom takes their credit risk.
  Stance: Weakening (distributor 28% -> 32% in FY2025, 50% of Q3 FY2026;
    ability-to-pay risk added in FY2025 and sharpened in Q3 FY2026).
  Tested by: distributor and top-five percentages, credit-risk wording,
    backstop payments, receivable allowances, DSO.

P3 Infrastructure software (VMware) franchise
  Bull assumes: VCF subscriptions give durable, high-margin, growing revenue
    with integration costs behind it.
  Bear fears: growth is pulled forward by upfront license recognition on
    non-terminable contracts; customer pushback and regulators (Korea, Japan,
    EU) constrain pricing.
  Stance: Intact (strong reported growth, but recognition-driven).
  Tested by: software segment revenue and margin, upfront license revenue,
    contract assets, termination-for-convenience share, regulatory language.

P4 Margins and operating leverage
  Bull assumes: revenue grows far faster than opex; gross margin holds near
    two-thirds.
  Bear fears: rack and system sales "will likely ... compress or lower future
    gross margin"; semiconductor mix lowers gross margin.
  Stance: Strengthening (gross margin 63% -> 68% in FY2025, 69% in Q3 FY2026;
    operating margin 54% in Q3 FY2026).
  Tested by: gross and operating margin, segment margins, R&D and SG&A trend.

P5 Cash conversion and capital returns
  Bull assumes: free cash flow at or above net income funds a rising dividend
    and buybacks without new debt.
  Bear fears: receivables and contract assets grow faster than revenue;
    factoring and sell-to-cover flatter cash flow; buybacks stop.
  Stance: Intact.
  Tested by: OCF / net income, FCF, DSO, factoring volumes, buybacks,
    dividends, SBC / OCF.

P6 Balance sheet and contingent exposure
  Bull assumes: VMware debt is paid down from cash flow; liquidity is ample.
  Bear fears: off-balance-sheet obligations (lease backstops, customer
    convertible notes, $100B+ purchase commitments) replace on-balance-sheet
    debt as the risk.
  Stance: Weakening (financing offered to customers stated in FY2025; $29B
    backstop and $42B convertible-note facility in Q3 FY2026, even as debt
    fell).
  Tested by: total debt, backstop exposure and payments, notes issued,
    purchase commitments, rating language.

P7 Tax rate
  Bull assumes: Singapore incentives and U.S. planning keep the effective
    rate low.
  Bear fears: Pillar Two and CAMT push the rate up materially from FY2026.
  Stance: Weakening (FY2025 "expect to materially increase"; Q3 FY2026 14.3%
    quarterly rate and $2,186M global minimum tax payable).
  Tested by: effective tax rate, global minimum tax payable, CAMT valuation
    allowance, incentive value.
