CARVANA (CVNA): INVESTMENT THESIS PILLARS
Written October 2026 from the 10-K dashboard themes and tracked signals, the 10-Q scanner, and the
eight filings from the Q3 2024 10-Q to the Q2 2026 10-Q. Stances move only when two or more filings
point the same way; "Last moved" names the filing that completed the run. Net income is consolidated
(including non-controlling interests) unless marked XBRL. Share data before May 2026 are pre-split.

P1 Unit growth and demand
  Bull assumes: retail units keep growing 30%+ a year from a ~1.6% share of a 37 million-unit used
    market, through existing markets, more inventory and ADESA production capacity.
  Bear fears: growth slows as the base doubles; affordability (car prices, rates, fuel) caps demand;
    headline units are flattered by partner "marketplace" cars booked net.
  Stance: Intact (never moved in the window).
  History: units +34.2% (Q3 2024), +33.1% (FY2024), +45.7% (Q1 2025), +41.2%, +43.5% (Q3 2025),
    +43.3% (FY2025), +40.0% (Q1 2026), +37.7% (Q2 2026). Growth is decelerating from the Q3 2025 peak but
    is still near 40% on a base of about 790,000 annualized units. Bear notes: stated reconditioning
    capacity cut (Q3 2024), restored to 1.5 million (FY2025), then dropped (Q1 2026); marketplace
    units never quantified; Q2 2026 revenue per unit +17.4% partly on used-price appreciation, and
    gasoline prices "have pressured" consumers.
  Tests: retail unit growth, average monthly unique visitors, website units, revenue per unit vs units.

P2 Unit economics
  Bull assumes: total gross profit per unit (GPU) holds near $7,000 while SG&A per unit keeps
    falling, so Adjusted EBITDA margin expands with scale.
  Bear fears: GPU erodes as growth is prioritized (depreciation, acquisition costs, wholesale), and
    the filings stop explaining why.
  Stance: Weakening, moved at the Q1 2026 10-Q.
  History: moved to Strengthening at the FY2024 10-K (Q3 2024 and FY2024 retail GPU up $800-900 on
    named operating causes; Q1 and Q2 2025 extended it, Adj. EBITDA margin peaked at 12.4%). Q3 2025
    was the first year-over-year decline in the window (total GPU $7,362 vs $7,427); the FY2025 10-K
    gave retail GPU "approximately flat" with no cause and an implied Q4 decline (derived: total GPU
    about $6,427 vs $6,671); Q1 2026 ($6,783 vs $6,938, margin 10.4% vs 11.5%) completed the run, and
    Q2 2026 confirmed it ($7,014 vs $7,426, margin 10.4% vs 12.4%, no cause for retail or wholesale).
    SG&A per unit fell in every filing ($4,317 Q3 2024 to $3,568 Q2 2026), so operating income still grew.
  Tests: total and retail GPU vs prior year, Adj. EBITDA margin, SG&A per unit, whether causes return.

P3 Loan sales and finance profit
  Bull assumes: loans originated on the platform keep selling at a premium to committed buyers
    (Ally forward flow, securitizations, fixed-pool buyers), so other gross profit stays near
    $2,800-3,000 per unit.
  Bear fears: spreads compress, one buyer (Ally, also the floor-plan lender) takes a rising share,
    and finance profit, 100% margin, falls straight to earnings.
  Stance: Weakening, moved at the Q2 2026 10-Q.
  History: "higher loan sale spreads" cited Q3 2024 through Q2 2025 and in the FY2025 full-year
    explanation; gain on loan sales $755M (2024) to $1.2B (2025); committed capacity grew from a $4.0B
    Ally line to Ally $6.0B (Q3 2025) and $8.0B (July 2026) plus $12B of fixed-pool agreements (FY2025).
    Q1 2026 first named "lower loan sale spreads" and deleted the loan-monetization goal; Q2 2026
    repeated it (other GPU $2,666 vs $2,869; gain about 7.4% of principal sold vs about 8.8%), with
    Ally at about 45% of Q2 loan sales and its headroom at $1.2B before the renewal.
  Tests: gain on loan sales per principal sold, other GPU, Ally share of sales, spread wording.

P4 Earnings quality
  Bull assumes: reported earnings converge on operating earnings once PIK interest, warrant marks,
    the TRA and the valuation allowance are behind the company.
  Bear fears: non-operating items (Root warrant marks, TRA expense, tax-allowance releases, PIK)
    keep driving reported net income.
  Stance: Intact, moved at the Q2 2026 10-Q (back from Weakening, reached at the Q1 2025 10-Q).
  History: FY2024 net income included a $115M warrant gain with $458M of PIK interest and $2.0B of TRA
    left off the balance sheet; Q1 2025 net income $373M included a $158M warrant gain, completing the
    run to Weakening. Q2-Q3 2025 warrant losses ($35M, $120M) swung the other way; FY2025 net income
    $1,895M rested on a $2.8B tax benefit less $2.2B of TRA expense. Q1 2026 (no PIK, no TRA expense,
    8.1% tax) and Q2 2026 (other expense zero, 11.4% tax, pre-tax +88%) are the first clean quarters.
  Tests: other expense (income), TRA expense, effective tax rate, PIK (none since 2025).

P5 Cash conversion and inventory
  Bull assumes: operating cash flow tracks earnings and funds growth without equity.
  Bear fears: inventory and receivables growth absorb the cash; earlier cash flow was flattered by
    interest paid in kind.
  Stance: Intact (never moved in the window).
  History: mixed in every year. Bear: Q3 2024 nine-month operating cash flow down $184M with $388M of
    PIK; FY2024 $918M of which $274M came from PIK timing; Q2 and Q3 2025 down on inventory; Q1 2026
    $107M vs $232M. Bull: Q1 2025 up with cash interest; FY2025 $1.036B with cash interest; H1 2026
    $345M vs $261M although H1 2025 had $146M of PIK. Inventory rose from $1.61B (Dec 2024) to $3.26B
    (June 2026), faster than units.
  Tests: operating cash flow vs Adjusted EBITDA, inventory vs units, equity issuance.

P6 Balance sheet and funding
  Bull assumes: secured debt from the 2023 exchange is retired, liquidity grows, and growth is
    self-funded.
  Bear fears: deleveraging relies on equity sales; 9% secured notes and the TRA remain large claims.
  Stance: Strengthening, moved at the FY2024 10-K.
  History: debt $6.03B (Dec 2023) -> $5.53B (Q3 2024) -> $5.47B (FY2024) -> $5.57B (Q2 2025) -> $5.06B
    (Q3 2025, $559M redeemed) -> $4.98B (FY2025) -> $5.13B (Q2 2026). Liquidity resources $4.42B ->
    $7.02B; PIK ended in 2025. Bear: $1.3B ATM in 2024 and $539M in Q3 2025, two quarters after the
    self-funding pledge; TRA liability $2.2B (current $99M at Q2 2026).
  Tests: total debt, liquidity resources, ATM use, TRA payments.

P7 Governance, legal and related parties
  Bull assumes: legal exposure is routine consumer litigation; the controlling family's interests
    align with shareholders.
  Bear fears: regulatory action (SEC, state AGs), late disclosure, and value transfer to related
    parties through the TRA and DriveTime agreements.
  Stance: Weakening, moved at the Q2 2025 10-Q.
  History: FY2024 accrual $3M -> $10M and AG claims "have resulted"; Q1 2025 dropped the "without
    merit" reassurance and the Item 1A "no material changes" line without mentioning the January 2025
    short report; Q2 2025 disclosed the June 2025 SEC subpoena. FY2025 added "lending practices" to AG
    inquiries and recorded a $2.3B TRA liability, $1.7B of it to related parties. 2026 eased: IBM and
    Erakat settled for immaterial amounts, accrual back to $10M; the SEC matter and the 2022 class
    action remain open without estimates.
  Tests: SEC matter outcome, class certification ruling, legal accrual, TRA payments to related parties.
