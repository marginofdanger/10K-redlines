FICO FY2024 10-K (filed November 6, 2024; fiscal year ended September 30, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2023 10-K. Figures from the filing and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $1.72B, +13% (FY2023 +10%). Scores $919.7M, +19%: B2B "primarily attributable to a higher unit
   price, partially offset by a decrease in volume of mortgage originations"; B2C -$5.0M (myFICO.com).
   Software $797.9M, +8%; ARR $721.2M, +8% (+22% a year earlier). RPO $507.3M vs $470.5M (Note on revenue).

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Operating margin 42.7% vs 42.5%. Scores segment margin 88% (unchanged); Software 32% vs 33%
   (prior-year data-center reimbursement). Operating income +14%.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Net income +19% vs operating income +14%; ETR 20.1% vs 22.4% on larger excess tax benefits from
   share-based compensation. No impairments or restructuring.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Operating cash flow $633.0M vs $468.9M (+35%); FCF $624.1M = 122% of net income (108%). DSO 91 days.

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Buybacks $833.3M (cash $821.7M) vs $407.3M = 132% of FCF; new $1.0B July 2024 program, $760.5M left.
   No dividends. Capex $8.9M.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Total debt $2.2B vs $1.9B: $450M term loan added; revolver $210.0M; term loans mature August 2026.
   Covenants: leverage 3.5x and interest coverage 3.0x. Stockholders' deficit $962.7M vs $688.0M.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   Three bureaus 45% of revenue (41%, 39%); one customer 10%+ of receivables.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Critical estimates unchanged in substance. ACV Bookings already demoted to "a useful supplemental
   measure" and DBNRR redefined as point-in-time (both in FY2024 10-Qs, before this window).

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   New risk factor: the CFPB's May 2024 inquiry into mortgage closing costs "including fees for credit
   reports and credit scores" may limit mortgage score price increases. First FHFA references; bi-merge
   risk added. Legal proceedings: "Not applicable."

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   No financial guidance in the filing. FICO Score 10 T "will be implemented for conforming mortgages
   based on the timeline set forth by the" FHFA. July 2024 buyback authorization $1.0B.
