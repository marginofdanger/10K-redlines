FICO FY2025 10-K (filed November 7, 2025; fiscal year ended September 30, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q3 FY2025 10-Q (annual rates compared with FY2024).

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $1.99B, +16%. Scores $1.17B, +27%; B2B $948.6M, +33%, on "a higher unit price, an increase in
   volume of mortgage originations and a multi-year license renewal" in insurance scores. Software +3%;
   ARR $747.3M, +4%; DBNRR 102%. RPO $655.7M vs $507.3M.

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Operating margin 46.5% vs 42.7%. Scores 88% (flat); Software 30% vs 32%, operating income -4% on hosting
   and personnel costs.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   ETR 18.8% vs 20.1% on excess tax benefits. Restructuring $10.9M (226 positions), the first in three years.
   Interest expense $133.6M (+27%).

4. Cash conversion [HELD]
   Operating cash flow $778.8M (+23%); FCF $769.9M = 118% of net income (122%). DSO 97 days (91).

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Buybacks about $1.4B (cash $1.41B) = 183% of FCF; $343.6M left under the June 2025 program.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Total debt $3.1B vs $2.2B. $400M 2018 notes now current (due May 2026). Leverage covenant 3.5x; interest
   coverage covenant gone. Stockholders' deficit $1.746B vs $0.963B.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   Bureaus 51% of revenue (45%, 41%); two customers 10%+ of receivables (one).

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Professional-services input-method estimate dropped from critical estimates; others unchanged.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   FHFA risk factor rewritten around the July 2025 announcement letting lenders choose the score submitted
   to the GSEs; new sentence that FICO's mortgage competitiveness "may be constrained by the pricing and
   other business practices of the consumer reporting agencies". CFPB inquiry removed from the risk factor.
   Score 10 T "was approved for conforming mortgages". Antitrust item unchanged.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   No guidance. Restructuring cash "will be paid by the end of fiscal 2026". Score 10 T moved from "will be
   implemented" (FY2024) to approved.
