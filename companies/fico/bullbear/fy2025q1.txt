FICO Q1 FY2025 10-Q (filed February 4, 2025; quarter ended December 31, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2024 10-K (growth and margin rates year over year).

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $440.0M, +15%. Scores $235.7M, +23%; B2B +$42.0M "primarily attributable to a higher unit price
   and an increase in volume of mortgage originations" (FY2024: volume was an offset). Software +$14.3M,
   helped by a large point-in-time licence renewal. ARR $729.3M, +6%; RPO $496.3M (Sept: $507.3M).

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Operating margin 40.8% vs 39.6%. Scores margin 86% vs 88% ("an increase in marketing expenses");
   Software 30%, flat.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   ETR (1.6)% vs 7.3%: tax benefit of $2.4M on $150.1M pre-tax income. Pre-tax income +15%, net income +26%;
   at a 24% rate, tax would have been about $36M (inference), roughly a quarter of net income.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Operating cash flow $194.0M vs $122.1M; FCF $193.2M = 127% of net income. DSO 73 days.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks $159.7M, within FCF; $600.7M left under the July 2024 program.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Total debt $2.4B (Sept $2.2B); revolver $425.0M (from $210.0M). Covenants unchanged (3.5x / 3.0x).
   Stockholders' deficit $1.138B.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   Bureaus 44% of quarterly revenue (39%); one customer 10%+ of receivables.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   No changes to critical estimates.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   First disclosure of consolidated antitrust class actions (N.D. Ill.); on November 24, 2024 the court let
   a Sherman Act Section 2 claim against FICO proceed to discovery. Two months earlier the 10-K said "Not
   applicable." No loss estimate; contingencies note unchanged.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   No guidance. Buyback authorization being used at about $160M a quarter.
