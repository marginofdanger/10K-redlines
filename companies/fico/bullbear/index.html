Latest filing: 10-Q Q3 FY2026

What the June 2026 quarter adds, for and against. Operating results stayed strong, but Scores revenue fell from the March quarter and the company borrowed $1.5B to buy back stock, loosening its leverage covenant to make room.

Bull case

P1 Scores pricing powerStated
Scores revenue +41% to $458.9M; segment margin 91% vs 88%
Q3 FY2026: "Scores segment operating income as a percentage of segment revenue increased to 91% from 88%, primarily due to higher business-to-business scores revenue driven by a higher mortgage origination scores unit price."

Size: B2B Scores +$131.6M = 96% of the $137.8M revenue increase; Scores operating income +$132.2M exceeds the $100.1M rise in total operating income.

Price on mortgage scores is still adding about $130M a quarter year over year at almost no incremental cost.

Next filing: FY2026 10-K Scores growth for the year near the nine-month +45%, with margin at or above 90%.

P4 Software platform transitionStated
Platform ARR +62% to $412.8M, overtaking non-platform; total ARR +10% and DBNRR 109% for a second quarter
Q3 FY2026: "Platform | 227.0 | 227.7 | 234.7 | 254.2 | 263.6 | 302.6 | 348.8 | 412.8"
Q3 FY2026: "Dollar-Based Net Retention Rate for our Software segment was 109% as of June 30, 2026."

Size: total ARR $815.8M (+10%, vs +4% a year earlier); platform DBNRR 148%; nine-month ACV Bookings $95.2M vs $69.7M (+37%).

The transition that stalled through FY2025 (DBNRR 102–103%) has two quarters of double-digit ARR growth, enough to move the pillar back to Intact.

Next filing: ARR growth of 10% or more and DBNRR at or above 105% in the FY2026 10-K.

P6 Cash conversion and capital returnStated
Nine-month operating cash flow +40% to $777.9M; Q3 free cash flow $379.6M = 160% of net income
Q3 FY2026: "Net cash provided by operating activities increased to $777.9 million during the nine months ended June 30, 2026 from $555.1 million during the nine months ended June 30, 2025."

Size: nine-month operating cash flow = 118% of net income ($660.0M); capex plus capitalized software only $27.8M.

Earnings convert to cash, and the growth is not a working-capital build: timing items were −$1.3M.

Next filing: full-year operating cash flow growth at least in line with net income (+33% for nine months).

P5 Margins and earnings qualityStated
Operating margin 53.8% vs 48.9%, with no tax help: nine-month tax rate 23.5% vs 17.2%
Q3 FY2026: "Cost of revenues as a percentage of revenues decreased to 13% during the quarter ended June 30, 2026 from 16% during the quarter ended June 30, 2025, primarily due to increased sales of our higher-margin Scores products."
Q3 FY2026: "The effective income tax rate was 24.6% and 23.3% during the quarters ended June 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively, and 23.5% and 17.2% during the nine months ended June 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively."

Size: operating income +38% on revenue +26% (operating expenses +14%); nine-month pre-tax income +44%, net income +33%.

FY2025's earnings growth leaned on excess tax benefits from equity awards; this year's comes from operations, and no one-off item is named in the attribution.

Next filing: operating margin above 50% in the FY2026 10-K with no one-time licence in the Scores sentence.

P6 Cash conversion and capital returnStated
Diluted shares −7.7% lift EPS +41% on net income +30%
Q3 FY2026: "Diluted EPS was $10.45 during the quarter ended June 30, 2026, a 41% increase from the quarter ended June 30, 2025"

Size: 22.7M diluted shares vs 24.6M; shares outstanding 23.3M → 21.6M during the quarter; the ASR's remaining ~20% of shares arrive at settlement in Q4.

Per-share compounding accelerated. Whether that is a bull point depends on funding, which this quarter was borrowing (see the bear side).

Next filing: ASR settlement share count and the average price paid.

Bear case

P7 Balance sheet and leverageStated
$1.5B term loan borrowed to fund a $1.5B ASR; debt $3.6B → $5.6B; leverage ceiling raised from 3.5x to 4.5x
Q3 FY2026: "In June 2026, we amended our credit agreement to provide for a $1.5 billion term loan, the proceeds of which were used to fund an accelerated share repurchase agreement"
Q3 FY2026: "The credit agreement contains certain restrictive covenants including a maximum consolidated leverage ratio of 4.5 to 1.0 through December 30, 2026, 4.0 to 1.0 during December 31, 2026 through December 30, 2027, and 3.5 to 1.0 during December 31, 2027 and thereafter"

Size: debt about 4.5x trailing-twelve-month operating income of $1.24B (inference; covenant EBITDA is not disclosed). Interest expense $59.9M, +82%, = 17% of operating income (12.5% a year earlier). Floating-rate debt $2.21B; $300M of term-loan principal due within 12 months.

The company needed a looser covenant to do this buyback, and the ceiling steps back down to 4.0x on December 31, 2026, so debt must fall or earnings rise within two quarters. The 10-Q's risk factors still say "no material changes."

Next filing: the $75M September amortization paid, revolver below $710M, and leverage headroom under the 4.0x step-down.

P6 Cash conversion and capital returnStated
Buybacks $2.3B in the quarter and $3.1B in nine months, against $777.9M of operating cash flow; stockholders' deficit $4.10B
Q3 FY2026: "Total share repurchases during the quarter ended June 30, 2026 were $2.3 billion, compared with $511.3 million during the quarter ended June 30, 2025"
Q3 FY2026: "Total stockholders’ deficit | ( 4,097,135 ) | ( 1,745,784 )"

Size: nine-month buybacks = 4.0x operating cash flow; Q3 buybacks = 6x the quarter's FCF; average price $1,148.84 a share. The deficit grew $2.35B since September.

Capital return is now set by borrowing capacity, not by free cash flow, after a quarter in which Scores revenue fell sequentially.

Next filing: buybacks back within free cash flow once the ASR settles, or another debt-funded program.

P1 Scores pricing powerStated
B2B Scores fell from $417.6M in Q2 to $400.0M; the quarter's driver narrows to the mortgage score price alone
Q3 FY2026: "The increase in business-to-business scores revenue was primarily attributable to a higher mortgage origination scores unit price."
Q3 FY2026: "Business-to-business Scores | 400,043 | 268,479 | 87 | 83 | 1,066,285 | 693,364 | 86 | 81"
Q2 FY2026: "Business-to-business Scores | 417,629 | 242,494 | 88 | 82 | 666,242 | 424,885 | 85 | 80"

Size: −$17.6M (−4%) sequentially; year-over-year growth slowed from +72% to +49%. In FY2025 the same quarter added $26.0M sequentially.

Mortgage volume dropped out of the quarterly driver, so all of the growth now hangs on one price; a single regulator or bureau decision on that price would hit the segment directly.

Next filing: B2B Scores in the September quarter versus $400.0M, and whether volume returns to the attribution.

P4 Software platform transitionStated
Software margin 26% vs 32%, segment operating income −19%; RPO falls for the first time between 10-Qs
Q3 FY2026: "Software segment operating income as a percentage of segment revenue decreased to 26% from 32%, primarily attributable to a decrease in sales of higher-margin software recognized at a point in time and an increase in third-party data center hosting costs."
Q3 FY2026: "Revenue allocated to remaining performance obligations was $680.4 million as of June 30, 2026"

Size: Software operating income $55.0M vs $67.9M; non-platform ARR $403.0M (−17%, DBNRR 82%); RPO −$37.3M from March; professional services −24%.

Platform growth is costing margin: the shrinking licence book carried the segment's profit, and hosting costs rise as SaaS replaces it.

Next filing: Software margin back to 28% or better and RPO growth.

P3 Bureau channel concentrationStated
The three bureaus are 63% of quarterly revenue (54%) and 60% of nine-month revenue
Q3 FY2026: "Revenues collectively generated by agreements with these customers accounted for 63% and 54% of our total revenues in the quarters ended June 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively"

Size: about $425M of the quarter's $674.2M; three customers each 10%+ of receivables (two at September 30).

The bureaus own VantageScore and, per the FY2025 10-K, can constrain FICO's mortgage pricing; their share of FICO's revenue rose 9 points in a year.

Next filing: FY2026 10-K annual share (FY2025: 51%) and any change to the bureau-pricing risk factor.
