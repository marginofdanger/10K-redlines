Latest filing: 10-K FY2026

What the full year adds, for and against. Revenue +27% and an operating margin near 50% survived a $65.0M antitrust settlement and a 15% workforce cut, and the fourth quarter went to repaying debt instead of buybacks. Against that, FHFA's acceptance of VantageScore is now written into the risk factors as damage already done to the stock, and B2B Scores fell for a second straight quarter. P1 moves from Strengthening to Intact; no other stance moves. Fourth-quarter figures are derived as the year less the three 10-Q quarters.

Bull case

P1 Scores pricing powerStated
Scores revenue +41% to $1.65B with no one-off in the attribution; segment margin 90%
FY2026: "Segment operating income as a percentage of segment revenue for Scores increased to 90% from 88%, primarily due to higher business-to-business scores revenue driven by both a higher unit price and an increase in volume of mortgage origination scores."

Size: B2B +$473.6M = 87% of the $544.0M revenue increase; Scores operating income +$463.6M exceeds the $340.8M rise in total operating income. Scores 65% of revenue (59%).

FY2025's growth included an up-front insurance licence; this year's is mortgage price and volume alone, at almost no incremental cost.

Next filing: Q1 FY2027 B2B Scores against $248.6M a year earlier, the first quarter after FHFA's September 2026 all-lender announcement.

P2 Mortgage regulation, competition and litigationStated
Antitrust class action settling for $65.0M
FY2026: "In consideration of a cash payment by FICO to the plaintiffs of $65.0 million and certain compliance undertakings, the settlement would fully resolve all remaining claims asserted against FICO in the lawsuits."

Size: $65.0M = 5% of FY2026 operating income and 6% of operating cash flow; accrued in fourth-quarter SG&A, not yet paid.

The one claim that survived dismissal, Sherman Act Section 2, would be resolved without an admission. The compliance undertakings are not described, so the settlement's cost to the pricing model is unknown.

Next filing: definitive agreements (expected in Q1 FY2027), court approval, and what the compliance undertakings require.

Bear case

FHFA accepts VantageScore from all GSE lenders; the risk factor says a competing score has already hurt the stock
FY2026: "The ability to use a competing credit score model in the U.S. mortgage market, including by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac, has, and could continue to have, a material adverse effect on our stock price"
FY2026: "...the FHFA announced in April 2026 that the Enterprises would accept VantageScore from approved lenders and further announced in September 2026 that the Enterprises would accept VantageScore from all lenders."

The bear case moved from an announced policy (FY2025 10-K) to an implemented one, and the 10-K concedes market damage. The Q2 and Q3 10-Qs, filed after the April decision, said "no material changes" to the risk factors.

Next filing: VantageScore usage at the GSEs and FHA, mortgage score volume and price in the FY2027 10-Qs, and a Score 10T implementation date.
