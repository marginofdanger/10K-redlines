ALPHABET FY2024 10-K (filed February 5, 2025; year ended December 31, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2023 10-K. Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $350.02B, +14% (+15% constant currency; 2023 +9%). Search & other $198.08B, +13%; paid
   clicks +5%, cost-per-click +7%. YouTube ads +15%. Network -3% ("a decrease in Google Ad Manager and
   AdMob revenues"). Cloud $43.23B, +31%. Backlog $93.2B, about half to be recognized in 24 months.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Operating margin 32% vs 27%. Operating expenses +2% on lower legal, severance ($1.0B, -$1.1B) and
   office charges ($796M, -$1.3B). Cloud operating income $6.11B vs $1.72B (margin 14.1% vs 5.2%).
   Depreciation $15.3B vs $11.9B (+29%).

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Net income $100.12B, +36%. Equity-security gains $3.71B = 3% of pre-tax income. Tax rate 16.4% vs
   13.9% (2023 had a one-time IRS rule benefit). A January 2025 $8.0B unrealized gain on a private
   stake is disclosed as a subsequent item.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $125.30B (+23%). Capex $52.53B (+63%). FCF $72.76B (+5%); FCF/net income 72.7%
   vs 94.2%. DSO 55 days (57).

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks $62.0B and dividends $7.4B (first paid June 2024): $69.4B = 95% of FCF. Diluted shares
   -2.2%. $44.7B of authorization left.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Notes $11.9B; commercial paper $2.3B; cash and short-term securities $95.7B. Purchase commitments
   $55.4B ($32.5B short-term). Unstarted leases $7.3B, commencing 2025-2028.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   US 49% of revenue (47%). No customer above 10%; backlog "primarily related to Google Cloud".

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Servers depreciated over six years, unchanged. New risk factor that asset useful lives could
   change. No restatements.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   DOJ Search remedy framework "including structural remedies"; remedies hearing April 2025, decision
   expected second half of 2025. Ad-tech trial concluded. EC appealed the AdSense annulment. The
   standalone ESG risk factor was removed.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   2024 capex increase delivered ($52.5B vs $32.3B). New: "We also expect to increase, relative to
   2024, our investment in our technical infrastructure". Pillar Two "could increase" sentence dropped
   from the MD&A. No dollar targets.
