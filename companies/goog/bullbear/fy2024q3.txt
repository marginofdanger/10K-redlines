ALPHABET Q3 2024 10-Q (filed October 30, 2024; quarter ended September 30, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 2024 10-Q. Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL
(quarterly cash-flow values derived from year-to-date).

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $88.27B, +15% (+16% constant currency; Q2 +14%). Search & other $49.39B, +12% (Q2 +14%);
   paid clicks +4%, cost-per-click +8%. YouTube ads +12%. Network -2% (impressions -14%). Cloud
   $11.35B, +35% (Q2 +29%). Backlog $86.8B, +$8.0B in the quarter (Note 2). Pixel launch timing
   pulled devices revenue into Q3.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Operating margin 32% vs 28% a year earlier (Q2 also 32%). Cloud operating income $1.947B vs $266M
   (margin 17.1% vs 3.2%; Q2 11.3%). Operating expenses +5% including $607M of office space charges.
   Depreciation 9M $11.1B vs $8.6B (+29%).

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Net income $26.30B, +34%. Equity-security gains $1.82B = 6% of pre-tax income. Tax rate 17.0% vs
   7.1%; the 2023 quarter carried a one-time IRS rule benefit, so the year-over-year EPS comparison
   understates underlying growth.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $30.70B, including the $3.0B payment for the 2017 EC Shopping fine. Capex
   $13.06B (+62%). FCF $17.64B vs $22.60B a year earlier; FCF/net income 67%. DSO 51 days.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks $15.3B, dividends $2.5B: $17.75B, about 101% of FCF (86% excluding the fine). April 2023
   authorization exhausted; $59.7B left on April 2024's. Character.ai licence: $2.7B cash, booked as
   $2.7B goodwill and $413M intangibles.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Notes $11.9B unchanged. First commercial paper in the window, $1.0B (Q2: none). Cash and
   short-term securities $93.2B. Purchase commitments $50.8B ($31.1B short-term; Q2 $54.9B). Unstarted
   leases $6.1B (Q2 $5.2B). Credit facilities $10.0B undrawn, priced partly on sustainability goals.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   US 49% of revenue (47% a year earlier). Backlog "primarily related to Google Cloud". No customer
   concentration disclosed.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Character.ai licence plus hires accounted for as a business combination (ASC 805). Servers still
   depreciated over six years. No critical-estimate changes.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   DOJ Search ruling (August 2024); DOJ remedy framework "including structural remedies". Epic remedies
   ordered October 2024. ECJ upheld the 2017 Shopping fine ($3.0B paid). The General Court annulled the
   €1.5B AdSense fine (EC may appeal).

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   Capex: "We expect to increase, relative to 2023, our investment in our technical infrastructure"
   (9M $38.3B vs $21.2B, already met). No dollar guidance. Pillar Two: tax rate and cash taxes
   "could increase in future years".
