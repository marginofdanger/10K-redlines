ALPHABET FY2025 10-K (filed February 5, 2026; year ended December 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2024 10-K. Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $402.84B, +15% (2024 +14%). Search & other $224.53B, +13%; paid clicks +6%, CPC +7%. YouTube
   ads +12%. Network -2% (AdSense). Subscriptions, platforms and devices +19%. Cloud $58.71B, +36%
   (Q4 alone about $17.66B). Backlog $242.8B vs $93.2B; "just over 50%" in 24 months.

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Operating margin 32%, flat, after a $3.5B EC fine, a $1.4B legal accrual and a $2.1B Waymo
   valuation-based compensation charge. Cloud operating income $13.91B vs $6.11B (23.7% vs 14.1%; Q4
   about 30%). Other Bets loss $7.5B vs $4.4B. Depreciation $21.1B vs $15.3B (+38%). TAC rate 20.3%
   vs 20.7%.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $132.17B, +32%, against operating income +15%. Equity gains $24.08B = 15% of pre-tax
   income (3% in 2024). Tax rate 16.8%.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $164.71B (+31%, helped by OBBBA expensing). Capex $91.45B (+74%). FCF $73.27B,
   flat; FCF/net income 55% vs 73%. DSO 57 days (55).

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Buybacks $45.7B (-27%) and dividends $10.1B: 76% of FCF. Notes issued: $37.3B. Intersect agreed
   ($4.8B plus debt); Wiz pending. Waymo $16.0B round in February 2026, mostly Alphabet-funded.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Long-term debt $46.55B vs $10.88B; no commercial paper. Purchase commitments $149.1B ($113.0B
   short-term), including "energy take-or-pay contracts". Backstops: $5.7B financial guarantees plus
   $16.9B credit derivatives = $22.6B (terms up to 15 years). Unstarted leases $58.5B. January 2026
   power purchase agreement to be booked as a lease: $9.9B over 2027-2047. Legal accruals $15.6B.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   US 48% of revenue (49%). Backlog "primarily related to Google Cloud".

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Server lives unchanged at six years. Backstop guarantees and credit derivatives disclosed with fair
   value "not material". Waymo charge "based on estimated stock valuation".

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Search final judgment December 2025 (distribution limits, data sharing, syndication); Alphabet
   appealed January 2026, DOJ cross-appealed February 2026. Ad-tech remedies decision pending (DOJ
   seeks structural remedies). New risk factor: "excess capacity that we cannot easily redeploy".

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   2025 capex exceeded 2024 as guided ($91.4B). 2026: "significantly increase" capex; infrastructure
   operating costs "are expected to significantly increase". Intersect expected to close in H1 2026.
   New trend bullet on debt "or other forms of financing" and further credit support.
