ALPHABET Q1 2025 10-Q (filed April 25, 2025; quarter ended March 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the filing before (FY2024 10-K; growth rates vs Q4 2024 run-rate).

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   Revenue $90.23B, +12% (+14% constant currency; FY2024 +14%). Search & other $50.70B, +10%, the
   slowest in the window; paid clicks +2%, CPC +7%. YouTube ads +10%. Network
   -2%. Cloud $12.26B, +28%. Backlog $92.4B vs $93.2B, the only decline in the window; 55% now due within
   24 months ("approximately half" before).

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Operating margin 34% vs 32%. Cloud operating income $2.18B vs $0.90B (17.8% vs 9.4%). Depreciation
   $4.5B vs $3.4B (+32%), absorbed.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $34.54B, +46%, against operating income +20%. OI&E $11.2B includes an $8.0B unrealized gain
   on a private-company stake; equity gains $9.76B = 23% of pre-tax income. Tax rate 17.3%.

4. Cash conversion [HELD]
   Operating cash flow $36.15B; capex $17.20B; FCF $18.95B = 55% of net income (about 67% excluding the
   after-tax effect of the $8.0B mark; inference). DSO 51 days.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks $15.1B (cash) and dividends $2.4B: 92% of FCF. New $70.0B authorization (April 2025); dividend +5%
   to $0.21. Wiz agreed for $32.0B in cash, expected to close in 2026.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]

   (from $7.3B), now "primarily related to data centers" and running to 2031. Credit facilities renewed

7. Concentration [HELD]
   US 49% of revenue (48%). No customer concentration disclosed.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   The $8.0B mark-up comes from the measurement alternative (an observable transaction). No
   critical-estimate changes.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
