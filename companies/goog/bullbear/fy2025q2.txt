ALPHABET Q2 2025 10-Q (filed July 24, 2025; quarter ended June 30, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q1 2025 10-Q.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $96.43B, +14% (Q1 +12%). Search & other $54.19B, +12% (Q1 +10%); paid clicks +4%, CPC +8%.
   YouTube ads +13%. Network -1%. Cloud $13.62B, +32% (Q1 +28%). Backlog $108.2B, +$15.8B in the quarter.

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]

   charge. Cloud operating income $2.83B vs $1.17B (20.7% vs 11.3%). H1 depreciation $9.5B vs $7.1B.

3. Earnings quality [BETTER]
   Net income $28.20B, +19%. Equity gains $1.29B = 4% of pre-tax income. Tax rate 16.9% vs 14.3%, raised
   by a non-deductible legal settlement.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Buybacks $13.6B (lowest quarter to that point) and dividends $2.5B: $16.2B, about 3x FCF. The gap was
   covered by the first bond issue. $86.3B of authorization left.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]

7. Concentration [HELD]

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
