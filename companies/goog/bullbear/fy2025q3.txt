ALPHABET Q3 2025 10-Q (filed October 30, 2025; quarter ended September 30, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 2025 10-Q.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $102.35B, +16% (Q2 +14%). Search & other $56.57B, +15% (Q2 +12%); paid clicks +7%, CPC +7%.
   YouTube ads +15%. Network -3%. Cloud $15.16B, +34%. Backlog $157.7B, +$49.5B (+46%) in the quarter;
   "for future services" dropped from the backlog definition.

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Operating margin 31% vs 32%; the $3.5B EC fine cost about 3.4 points (about 34% without it).
   Cloud operating income $3.59B vs $1.95B (23.7% vs 17.1%). 9M depreciation $15.1B vs $11.1B (+36%).

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $34.98B, +33%, against operating income +9%. Equity gains $10.73B = 24% of pre-tax income.
   Tax rate 20.5%, the window's high, on the non-deductible fine.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Operating cash flow $48.41B, including OBBBA expensing of domestic R&D and accelerated depreciation.
   Capex $23.95B. FCF $24.46B = 70% of net income. DSO 51 days.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks $11.6B and dividends $2.5B: 59% of FCF. 9M capex $63.6B already above FY2024's $52.5B.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Notes $23.6B; commercial paper $3.0B. First credit backstops of third-party data-center leases:
   maximum exposure $6.5B, terms up to 10 years. Purchase commitments $93.7B ($72.5B), now led by
   "energy contracts". Unstarted leases $42.6B ($23.9B).

7. Concentration [HELD]
   US 48% of revenue. Backlog still "primarily related to Google Cloud".

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Backlog definition broadened; credit backstops accounted for as credit derivatives (fair value "not
   material"); data-center leasing entities added to unconsolidated VIEs.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [HELD]
   Bull: Search remedies (September 2025) restrict distribution and require data sharing and
   syndication, with no structural remedy. Bear: EC ad-tech decision, €3.0B ($3.5B) accrued; Epic appeal
   denied, remedies implemented October 2025. Risk factors: no material changes, third quarter in a row.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   "We expect full year 2025 capital expenditures to exceed full year 2024" (already true). New: "In
   2026, we expect to significantly increase, relative to 2025" infrastructure investment. Final Search
   judgment expected late 2025. OBBBA effects reflected as said.
