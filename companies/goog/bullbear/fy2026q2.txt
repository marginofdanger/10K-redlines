ALPHABET Q2 2026 10-Q (filed July 23, 2026; quarter ended June 30, 2026)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q1 2026 10-Q. Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL
(quarterly cash-flow values derived from year-to-date).

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $119.80B, +24% (Q1 +22%). Search & other $63.27B, +17% (Q1 +19%); paid clicks +13%, CPC +3%.
   YouTube ads +13%. Network -1% (impressions -12%). Subscriptions, platforms and devices +15%. Cloud
   $24.77B, +82% (Q1 +63%), including the first TPU system sales (not quantified). Backlog $519.5B
   ($513.9B Cloud), +$51.9B; "just over 50%" in 24 months.

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Operating margin 34% vs 32% (Q1 36%); the $1.5B PriceRunner damages charge cost about 1.3 points.
   Cloud operating income $8.81B vs $2.83B (35.6%; Q1 32.9%). Alphabet-level AI costs $5.79B vs $3.37B.
   H1 depreciation $13.6B vs $9.5B (+43%). TAC rate 19.8% vs 20.6%.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $112.19B vs $28.20B. Equity gains $99.03B = 71% of pre-tax income, "primarily related to
   unrealized gains in our equity securities portfolio from SpaceX and a private company". $80.0B of
   SpaceX shares are under short-term sale restrictions and $14.1B through Q3 2027. Equity-derivative
   loss $457M on the contingent funding commitment. Excluding the gains at the 21% statutory rate, net
   income would be about $34B, +20% (inference).

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $39.07B (+41%); capex $44.92B; FCF -$5.86B, the first negative quarter in the
   window. H1 FCF $4.3B against H1 net income $174.8B. Inventory $2.4B -> $10.0B in six months (TPU
   systems). DSO 53 days. Accrued capex $8.9B -> $16.2B.

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   No buybacks for a second quarter. $49.6B of equity raised: $20.5B common offering, $10.0B Berkshire
   Hathaway placement, $19.0B 6.25% mandatory convertible preferred ($1.0B capped call). $40.0B ATM,
   proceeds "primarily intended" for employee equity-grant tax obligations. H1 dividends $5.2B exceeded
   H1 FCF. Shares outstanding 12,088M -> 12,230M (+1.2%).

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Long-term debt $98.2B ($77.5B in Q1; $46.5B at December), Q2 notes $20.3B in EUR, CAD and JPY;
   credit facilities $1.3B drawn. Interest expense $1,278M vs $261M. Cash and securities $242.5B,
   including $87.1B of marketable equity, mostly restricted SpaceX stock. Purchase commitments $811.0B
   ($707.0B long-term, "fixed or guaranteed"). Backstops $7.6B plus $43.8B = $51.4B, plus an
   estimated $24.1B more agreed. Unstarted leases $85.2B plus a $5.8B short-term lease. Energy
   obligations through 2054.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   Cloud is 98.9% of backlog; TPU systems sold under "a limited number of agreements". US 51% of
   revenue (48%).

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   SpaceX now a Level 1 marketable security; $87.9B of private stakes remeasured in the quarter under
   the measurement alternative. Tax rate up on deferred tax for unrealized gains. Credit-derivative
   liability $815M (was $69M).

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   PriceRunner (Stockholm) damages about $2.1B accrued; first quantified follow-on award. Android EC
   fine final; $5.2B paid in July 2026. Epic settlement motion withdrawn; Google complying with the
   2024 remedies. Ad-tech remedies judgment still pending. Accruals $17.4B (Q1 $15.6B). DMA risk adds
   Android access for third-party AI companies.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   TPU revenue began in Q2, ahead of "later in 2026". 2026 capex "significantly increase" held (H1
   $80.6B = 88% of FY2025). Buyback risk factor: "no assurances that we will make repurchases in the
   near term or at all". Backstop pipeline $33.3B -> estimated $24.1B. GFiber close still late 2026.
