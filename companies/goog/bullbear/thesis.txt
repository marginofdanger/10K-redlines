ALPHABET (GOOG): INVESTMENT THESIS PILLARS
Written October 2026 from the 10-K dashboard themes and tracked signals, the 10-Q scanner,
and the eight filings from the Q3 2024 10-Q to the Q2 2026 10-Q. Every pillar started the window
at Intact; a stance moves only when two or more filings point the same way.

P1 Search and advertising durability
  Bull assumes: Google Search & other keeps growing at or above 10% as AI features add queries and
    paid clicks, with the TAC rate stable or falling.
  Bear fears: AI assistants take queries and ad budgets; distribution remedies from the DOJ case
    raise TAC or cost default placements; Google Network keeps shrinking.
  Stance: Strengthening (Search growth +10% in Q1 2025, then +15% in Q3 2025, +19% in Q1 2026 and
    +17% in Q2 2026; paid clicks +13% in both 2026 quarters; TAC rate 20.6% -> 19.8%. Network revenue
    down in every filing in the window).
  Last moved: Q1 2026 10-Q.
  Tests: Search & other growth, paid clicks vs cost-per-click, TAC rate, Network revenue.

P2 Google Cloud growth and margin
  Bull assumes: Cloud grows 25%+ a year and its operating margin rises toward the company average as
    infrastructure and TPU demand is monetized; backlog converts on schedule.
  Bear fears: AI infrastructure is sold at low margins to a few large buyers; backlog depends on a
    handful of TPU and capacity contracts that Alphabet itself credit-supports.
  Stance: Strengthening (operating margin 17.1% in Q3 2024 -> 35.6% in Q2 2026; revenue growth 35% ->
    82%; backlog $86.8B -> $519.5B, of which $513.9B is Cloud).
  Last moved: Q2 2025 10-Q (Q1 and Q2 2025 both lifted the Cloud margin 8+ points year over year).
  Tests: Cloud revenue growth and operating margin, backlog and the share recognized in 24 months,
    TPU system revenue once disclosed.

P3 Capital intensity and commitments
  Bull assumes: capex is matched by contracted demand; depreciation growth is absorbed by revenue so
    operating margin holds; off-balance-sheet obligations stay modest.
  Bear fears: overbuild: capex, unstarted leases, purchase commitments and third-party backstops grow
    faster than contracted revenue, leaving "excess capacity that we cannot easily redeploy".
  Stance: Weakening (Q3 2025 first data-center backstops, $6.5B; FY2025 commitments $149.1B and
    backstops $22.6B with an overbuild risk factor; Q2 2026 commitments $811.0B against $519.5B of
    backlog and backstops $51.4B, plus $24.1B more agreed. Operating margin still rose to 34-36%).
  Last moved: FY2025 10-K.
  Tests: capex vs operating cash flow, purchase commitments vs backlog, backstop exposure,
    depreciation growth vs revenue growth, operating margin.

P4 Cash conversion and capital return
  Bull assumes: free cash flow covers buybacks and dividends and shrinks the share count 1-3% a year.
  Bear fears: capex absorbs free cash flow; buybacks stop and the share count rises.
  Stance: Broken (FY2024 FCF $72.8B covered $69.4B of buybacks and dividends; FY2025 FCF flat at $73.3B
    on net income +32%, buybacks -27%; Q1 2026 no buybacks and FCF $10.1B; Q2 2026 FCF negative
    ($-5.9B), $49.6B of new equity, a $40B ATM, and shares outstanding +1.2% in six months).
  Moved: Weakening at the FY2025 10-K (Q2 2025 and FY2025); Broken at the Q2 2026 10-Q (Q1 and Q2 2026).
  Last moved: Q2 2026 10-Q.
  Tests: FCF vs net income, buybacks resumed or not, ATM usage, diluted share count.

P5 Balance sheet and funding
  Bull assumes: a net-cash balance sheet funds the build-out with no meaningful leverage.
  Bear fears: the build-out becomes debt-funded; interest expense erodes the interest-income cushion;
    leases, backstops and commitments add leverage that is not on the debt line.
  Stance: Weakening (notes $11.9B unchanged to Q1 2025; first bond issue Q2 2025; $37.3B of notes in
    2025; long-term debt $98.2B by June 2026; quarterly interest expense $54M -> $1,278M. The June 2026
    equity raise lifted cash and securities to $242.5B).
  Last moved: FY2025 10-K.
  Tests: long-term debt, net interest income, credit-facility drawings, cash excluding restricted
    equity stakes.

P6 Earnings quality
  Bull assumes: reported EPS reflects operations; non-operating items are small.
  Bear fears: unrealized mark-ups on private stakes (SpaceX and an unnamed private company) and other
    one-offs drive EPS; they are not cash and can reverse.
  Stance: Weakening (equity-security gains 6% of pre-tax income in Q3 2024, 23% in Q1 2025, 24% in
    Q3 2025, 48% in Q1 2026 and 71% in Q2 2026. Operating income itself grew 30% in both 2026 quarters).
  Last moved: Q3 2025 10-Q.
  Tests: equity-security gains as a share of pre-tax income, operating income growth, effective tax rate.

P7 Legal and regulatory
  Bull assumes: remedies stay behavioral, fines are absorbable, no structural break-up.
  Bear fears: structural remedies in ad tech, distribution limits in Search, and follow-on private
    damages turn rulings into recurring cash costs.
  Stance: Intact (bull: Q3 2025 Search remedies were behavioral after the DOJ sought structural ones;
    AdSense fine annulled. Bear: $3.0B, $1.4B, $3.5B, $2.1B and $5.2B of fines, settlements and
    damages; accruals $17.4B; ad-tech remedies, where the DOJ seeks structural changes, still pending).
  Last moved: none in the window.
  Tests: ad-tech remedies judgment, Search appeal, accrued fines, follow-on damages awards.
