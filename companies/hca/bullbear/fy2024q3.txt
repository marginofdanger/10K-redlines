HCA Q3 2024 10-Q (filed October 31, 2024; quarter ended September 30, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 2024 10-Q. Figures from SEC XBRL (financials.py) unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $17.487B, +7.9% (Q2 2024 +10.3%); equivalent admissions +5.0%, revenue per equivalent
   admission +2.8% (MD&A, Operations Summary). The prior-year quarter included $397M of Florida
   directed-payment revenue for the year ended September 30, 2023, so underlying growth was faster
   (about +10.6% excluding it; arithmetic). Admissions +4.5%, ER visits +4.4%, surgeries -0.8%.
   Managed care and insurers $8.497B, +10.5%.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Pre-tax income $1.906B, +17.0%; margin 10.9% vs 10.1%. Salaries 45.0% vs 46.6% of revenue (contract
   labor -17.8%; salaries per equivalent admission -0.9%). Supplies 15.2% vs 14.9% (supply cost per
   equivalent admission +4.8%, devices +6.8%). Other operating expenses 21.2% vs 20.8% (provider fees,
   professional fees, Helene repairs). Hurricane Helene cost about $50M in the quarter.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Facility-sale loss $4M. Effective tax rate 25.0% vs 24.8%. Nine-month equity-award tax benefits $93M.
   The $397M Florida catch-up in the prior-year base flatters nothing this year; it depresses reported growth.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Operating cash flow $3.515B vs $2.479B (+$1.036B), led by $859M of working capital from lower
   receivables. Q3 FCF $2.32B = 183% of net income. DSO 52 (Q2 53).

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Nine months: buybacks $4.342B, dividends $525M, capex $3.590B; net debt increase $3.269B.
   Nine-month FCF $4.365B, so shareholder returns were 111% of FCF. Capex guide about $5B for 2024.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Debt $42.965B (February and August note issues of $4.5B and $3.0B at 5.45-6.10%). Secured facility
   availability $7.986B. Working capital $1.762B vs $2.272B at year-end. Variable-rate debt $1.256B.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   98 hospitals in Texas and Florida: 59% of admissions, 51% of revenue, 74% of uninsured admissions.
   Florida and Carolinas facilities exposed to the 2024 hurricanes.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Net self-insured professional liability reserves $1.980B vs $1.926B at year-end. Cost-to-charges
   ratio 10.3% vs 10.9%. No critical-estimate changes disclosed.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [HELD]
   Legal proceedings unchanged. May 2024 CMS Medicaid managed care final rule on SDP arrangements
   (effective through 2028) noted; financial impact "unable to estimate". IRS examining 2016-2018.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   2024 capex "approximate $5 billion" held. New: Q4 2024 hurricane hit of $200-300M ($0.60-0.90 per
   share), excluding insurance recoveries. Construction pipeline $4.7B.
