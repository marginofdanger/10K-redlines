HCA Q1 2025 10-Q (filed May 1, 2025; quarter ended March 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2024 10-K (the prior filing). Figures from SEC XBRL unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   Revenue $18.321B, +5.7% (FY2024 +8.7%); equivalent admissions +3.1%, revenue per equivalent
   admission +2.5% (MD&A). Admissions +2.8%, ER visits +3.7%, outpatient surgeries -2.5%. Managed
   care and insurers $9.041B, +5.8%; managed Medicaid -10%.

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Pre-tax income $2.327B (12.7%) vs $2.247B (13.0%), but the prior year included a $201M facility-sale
   gain; excluding it pre-tax income rose about 14% (arithmetic). Salaries 43.6% vs 44.4%; supplies 15.1%
   vs 15.4%; other operating expenses 21.0% vs 20.9%. Salaries per equivalent admission +0.6%.

3. Earnings quality [BETTER]
   No material gains (facility-sale gain $1M vs $201M). EPS $6.45, +8.8%, against a base that included
   $0.57 of gains. Equity-award tax benefits $24M.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $1.651B vs $2.469B (-$818M) on $1.157B of adverse working capital. FCF $0.66B =
   41% of net income. DSO 55 vs 53.

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Buybacks $2.506B (7.8M shares) + dividends $180M = 4.1x FCF; net debt increase $1.558B. Capex
   $991M; acquisitions $227M; facility sales $161M.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Debt $44.576B (+$1.55B). February: secured ABL, term loan and cash-flow revolver replaced by an $8.0B
   five-year unsecured revolver; $5.25B notes issued; $2.6B 5.375% notes repaid. Covenant list no longer
   restricts dividends or repurchases (FY2024 10-K did); one financial ratio remains. Availability
   $7.766B, then $6.366B on April 28. Variable-rate debt $520M.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   102 Texas and Florida hospitals (from 98 at Q3 2024): 59% of admissions, 52% of revenue.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Self-insured professional liability reserves $1.944B vs $1.924B. No critical-estimate changes.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Supplemental payment reviews "could result in the payment programs being reduced or eliminated"
   (new wording). EPTC expiry at end-2025 "further extension is uncertain". Tariff language added to
   forward-looking factors. Legal proceedings unchanged.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   2025 capex $5.0-5.2B reiterated. Construction pipeline $4.7B -> $5.6B. Dividend $0.72 paid.
