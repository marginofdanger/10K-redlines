HCA Q2 2025 10-Q (filed August 4, 2025; quarter ended June 30, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q1 2025 10-Q. Figures from SEC XBRL unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $18.605B, +6.4% (Q1 +5.7%); revenue per equivalent admission +3.9%, equivalent admissions
   +2.3% (MD&A). Admissions +2.1% and ER visits +1.0%, the slowest in the window so far. Medicaid
   $1.440B vs $1.166B; managed care and insurers +6.7%.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Pre-tax income $2.415B (13.0%) vs $2.237B (12.8%), +8.0%. Salaries 43.7% vs 43.9%, but salaries per
   equivalent admission +3.5% and supply cost per equivalent admission +5.5% (devices +10.2%,
   cardiovascular). Other operating expenses 20.4% vs 20.7%.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Facility-sale loss $3M (prior year $12M gain). Effective tax rate 24.1% vs 27.4%, the prior year
   carrying an unrecognized-tax-benefit charge. H1 equity-award tax benefits $33M vs $79M.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER, flattered]
   Operating cash flow $4.210B vs $1.971B (+$2.239B): $1.186B working capital (collections) and $862M
   of income tax payments deferred to Q4 2025 by the IRS (Tennessee). Q2 FCF $3.03B; about $2.17B
   without the deferral (arithmetic). DSO 51 vs 53.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   H1 buybacks $5.011B + dividends $351M vs H1 FCF $3.69B (1.45x); net debt increase $1.341B.
   Capex guide trimmed to "approximately $5.0 billion" from $5.0-5.2B.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Debt $44.483B (flat on Q1). $4.0B commercial paper program set up in June ($1.778B outstanding);
   revolver availability net of the CP backstop $6.208B. Working capital -$376M ($1.402B excluding CP).
   Variable-rate debt $2.078B.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   102 Texas and Florida hospitals: 59% of admissions, 52% of revenue, 73% of uninsured admissions.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Self-insured professional liability reserves $1.932B. No critical-estimate changes.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   OBBBA enacted July 4, 2025: Medicaid work requirements and six-month redeterminations by end-2026;
   provider-tax freeze and phase-down; SDP rates capped at Medicare-based levels with grandfathering
   and a 10-point annual step-down from 2028; Exchange verification rules. June 2025 CMS marketplace rule.

10. Guidance and commitments [WORSE]
   2025 capex guide cut to about $5.0B (low end of $5.0-5.2B). New: "resiliency plans" to reduce costs,
   unquantified. Construction pipeline $5.9B.
