HCA Q3 2025 10-Q (filed October 30, 2025; quarter ended September 30, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 2025 10-Q. Figures from SEC XBRL unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $19.161B, +9.6% (Q2 +6.4%); revenue per equivalent admission +6.1%, "favorably impacted by
   an increase in state supplemental Medicaid revenue during the quarter related to recently approved
   programs" (amount not given). Medicaid revenue $1.783B vs $1.127B (+$656M). Admissions +2.8%,
   inpatient surgeries +2.0%, outpatient surgeries +1.6%; uninsured admissions -1.3%.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Pre-tax income $2.418B (12.6%) vs $1.906B (10.9%), +26.9%. Salaries 43.6% vs 45.0% (credited to
   "labor management initiatives"); supplies 14.5% vs 15.2% (pharmacy -8.7%). Other operating expenses
   21.8% vs 21.2% on supplemental program expenses. Segment EBITDA: National +26%, Atlantic +18%,
   American +16%.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   An unquantified share of the quarter's growth comes from newly approved supplemental programs; the
   filing does not say how much relates to prior periods. Effective tax rate 23.9% vs 25.0% on
   unrecognized-tax-benefit adjustments. Nine-month equity-award tax benefits $45M.

4. Cash conversion [HELD, flattered]
   Operating cash flow $4.416B vs $3.515B, including $406M of deferred Tennessee tax payments (nine
   months: $1.259B), all due in Q4 2025. Q3 FCF $3.13B; DSO 50.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Nine months: buybacks $7.509B + dividends $517M = $8.03B vs FCF $6.82B (118%; about 144% without the
   tax deferral). Net debt increase $1.305B. Capex $3.455B.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Debt $44.511B. Commercial paper $1.910B ($2.100B on October 27). Availability $6.076B -> $5.886B.
   Working capital -$2.611B. Revolver spread adjustment removed (October 23).

7. Concentration [HELD]
   102 Texas and Florida hospitals: 59% of admissions, 51% of revenue.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Self-insured reserves $1.917B. Cost-to-charges ratio 10.0% vs 10.3%. No critical-estimate changes.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   PAYGO sequestration may cut Medicare payments "up to 4% in early 2026"; Medicaid DSH cuts of $8.0B a
   year for FY2026-2028 began October 1, 2025 unless deferred; federal shutdown from October 1.
   Grandfathered SDP applications pending, "in some instances, increased".

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   2025 capex about $5.0B held (nine months $3.455B). Construction pipeline $6.0B.
