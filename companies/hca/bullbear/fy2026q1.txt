HCA Q1 2026 10-Q (filed April 29, 2026; quarter ended March 31, 2026)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2025 10-K (the prior filing). Figures from SEC XBRL unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   Revenue $19.109B, +4.3% (FY2025 +7.1%); equivalent admissions +1.1%, revenue per equivalent
   admission +3.1% (MD&A). Admissions +0.7%; respiratory admissions -42%; winter storm. Inpatient
   surgeries -0.4%, outpatient -2.7%. Managed care and insurers $9.084B, +0.5%; Medicaid $1.444B, +21%.

2. Margins and unit economics [WORSE]
   Pre-tax income $2.287B (12.0%) vs $2.327B (12.7%), -1.7%. Salaries 43.3% vs 43.6% (salaries per
   equivalent admission +2.4%); supplies 14.9% vs 15.1%; other operating expenses 21.9% vs 21.0%
   (supplemental program expenses, professional fees, technology). Segment EBITDA: National +11%,
   Atlantic -2%, American flat.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   EPS $7.15, +10.9%, on net income +0.6%. Equity-award tax benefits $103M vs $24M; effective tax rate
   21.0% vs 23.8%. The increment ($79M) is about 5% of net income.

4. Cash conversion [HELD]
   Operating cash flow $2.014B vs $1.651B (+$363M working capital). FCF $0.895B = 55% of net income
   (Q1 is seasonally weak). DSO 53 vs 54 (XBRL basis 54 vs 55).

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks $1.571B (3.2M shares) + dividends $183M vs FCF $0.895B (2.0x); net debt increase $1.377B.
   Acquisitions $265M.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Debt $48.023B (+$1.53B in the quarter). Commercial paper $3.650B of the $4.0B program; availability
   $4.336B (year-end $5.779B). Working capital -$3.206B ($444M excluding CP). Variable-rate debt
   $3.950B; a 1-point rate rise costs $40M.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   102 Texas and Florida hospitals: 59% of admissions, 51% of revenue, 73% of uninsured admissions.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Self-insured reserves $1.940B vs $1.906B. Cost-to-charges ratio 9.2% vs 9.6%; uncompensated care
   $13.612B vs $10.993B (+24%). Unrecognized tax benefits $535M.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   EPTCs expired; uninsured admissions +15.6% attributed to it and to administrative reforms. Some
   states have preliminary grandfathering approvals; Florida 2024-25 application pending. Legal
   proceedings unchanged.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   2026 capex $5.0-5.5B reiterated. SDP-decline guide not repeated. Construction pipeline $7.1B ->
   $8.8B. Resiliency savings not quantified.
