JPMORGAN CHASE (JPM) Q2 2026 10-Q (filed August 6, 2026; quarter ended June 30, 2026)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q1 2026 10-Q (growth rates are year on year against Q2
2025). Figures from the filing's financial highlights, MD&A and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Net revenue $57.3B, +28%; about $51.7B (+15%) excluding the $4.6B Visa gain and $1.0B of equity
   gains (our arithmetic). NII $25.5B, +10%; ex-Markets $23.7B, +4% (Q1 +9% total). Markets revenue
   $12.1B, +35%. Average loans $1.5T (+10%), average deposits $2.7T (+7%). Executive Overview.

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Noninterest expense $27.3B, +15%, "predominantly driven by higher compensation expense as a
   result of higher revenue-related compensation". Pre-provision profit $30.0B (+42%, flattered by
   the gains). ROTCE 29% (21% a year earlier); underlying expense growth about equal to underlying
   revenue growth. Consolidated Results of Operations.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $21.2B (+41%) includes $5.6B pre-tax of one-offs: the Visa B-2 exchange ($4.6B net
   gain, Corporate) and $1.0B of equity-investment gains ($763M Corporate, $263M CIB) from a
   measurement-alternative markup and transitions to fair value. About a fifth of pre-tax income.
   Effective tax rate 23.1%. Consolidated Results of Operations; Notes 2 and 5.

4. Cash conversion [HELD]
   Not a useful measure for a bank (operating cash flow swings with trading assets and securities
   financing). Capital generation: six-month net income applicable to common $37.1B against common
   dividends $8.1B and net treasury-stock purchases $13.8B, so 59% was paid out. TBVPS $113.35 (+10%).

5. Capital allocation [BETTER]
   Repurchases $6.7B (Q1 $8.3B). Board authorized a second $50B program effective July 1, 2026;
   dividend to rise to $1.65 from $1.50. Capital allocated to CIB +$8.5B "in connection with growth
   in the business". Strategic Investment Group equity $3.7B. Capital Risk Management.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   CET1 14.2% Standardized and Advanced (14.3% in Q1) against 11.5%; SLR 5.5% against 4.3%.
   Standardized RWA up $93B in the quarter ($152B in six months). HQLA ~$956B; liquidity sources
   ~$1.5T. Uninsured deposits $1,743.9B ($1,558.6B at December 31, 2025). Cash-flow-hedge losses
   expected to reach income over 12 months $(1.7)B (Q1 $(1.5)B).

7. Concentration [HELD]
   Apple Card commitment: ~$106B of exposure at closing (about $24B drawn). Deposit growth is
   wholesale-led ("net inflows related to client-driven activities primarily in Payments").
   Criticized exposure up in Commercial and Industrial, concentrated in Consumer & Retail,
   Transportation, Industrials and Oil & Gas.

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Apple Card portfolio now excluded from the allowance sensitivity analysis "until after the
   completion of the acquisition" (was: while the model is integrated). Adverse-weight overlay
   unchanged; weighted peak unemployment 5.6%, GDP gap 2.1% (Q1 2.2%). Equity gains rely on
   measurement-alternative markups. Critical Accounting Estimates.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [BETTER]
   Shareholder derivative suit dismissed with prejudice (March 2026) and removed from the note;
   interchange superseding settlement preliminarily approved; cash sweep plaintiffs moved to
   certify a class. Loss range unchanged at $0 to ~$1.3B. Note 24.

10. Guidance and commitments [BETTER]
   2026 NII guide $103B -> ~$105.5B (ex-Markets $95B -> $96.5B); adjusted expense $105B -> ~$107.5B;
   card NCO 3.4% -> ~3.2%. Card NCO 3.34% in the quarter. Visa gain booked in Q2 as Q1 said it
   "may be recorded as early as the second quarter". Outlook.
