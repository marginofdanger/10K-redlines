META 10-Q Quarterly Scanner

Quarter-over-quarter textual analysis of 9 SEC quarterly filings. The value is speed: catching language changes at quarterly cadence that annual 10-K comparisons would miss by 3–9 months.
Source: SEC EDGAR (CIK 0001326801) | 9 Quarterly Filings | Q1 2023 – Q3 2025 | Analysis: March 2026

Q1 2023 → Q2 2023

Headcount: 77K → 71K | Capex: $30-33B → $27-30B
▶ "Discipline and habits" first appears in Investment Philosophy New

Q1 2023 MDA opens with a cost-focused framing. Q2 2023 introduces a new sentence anchoring the efficiency program as cultural:

"We expect to continue to build on the discipline and habits that we developed in 2022 when we initiated several efforts to increase our efficiency, while still remaining focused on investing in significant opportunities."

Q1 2023 had no equivalent phrase — it led with "We expect our total expenses in 2023 to be driven by headcount-related expenses."

▶ "Board Chair" replaces "Chairman" Changed

Q1 2023 risk factor TOC:

"...our founder, Chairman, and CEO."

Q2 2023 risk factor TOC:

"...our founder, Board Chair, and CEO."

This title reverted to "Chairman, and Chief Executive Officer (CEO)" from Q1 2024 onward.

▶ RL loss guidance adds "continue to" Changed

Q1 2023 MDA:

"...we expect our RL operating losses to increase in 2023 and beyond."

Q2 2023 MDA:

"...we expect our RL operating losses to continue to increase in 2023 and beyond."

The addition of "continue to" signals losses were already growing, not just projected to grow.

▶ AI paragraph expands from single paragraph to multi-paragraph Signal

Q1 2023 risk factor on AI was one paragraph covering initiatives, competition, and regulation. Q2 2023 splits AI into a standalone risk factor with its own header and adds the sentence:

"We are making significant investments in artificial intelligence (AI) initiatives, including generative AI, to, among other things, recommend relevant unconnected content across our products, enhance our advertising tools, develop new products, and develop new features for existing products."

Q1 2023 had used "develop new product features using generative AI" as a single item. Q2 2023 split this into "develop new products" + "develop new features for existing products" — a broader scope claim.

▶ Capex guidance cut from $30-33B to $27-30B Signal

Q1 2023 MDA:

"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $30 billion to $33 billion in 2023."

Q2 2023 MDA:

"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $27 billion to $30 billion in 2023."

A $3B cut at both ends of the range — the efficiency era was pulling back infrastructure spend.

Q2 2023 → Q3 2023

Headcount: 71K → 66K | Capex: $27-30B → $27-29B (2023) + first 2024 guidance at $30-35B
▶ DMA and DSA first named in risk factor TOC New

Q2 2023 risk factor TOC:

"complex and evolving U.S. and foreign privacy, data use and data protection, content, competition, consumer protection, and other laws and regulations"

Q3 2023 risk factor TOC:

"complex and evolving U.S. and foreign privacy, data use and data protection, content, competition, consumer protection, and other laws and regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Digital Markets Act (DMA), and Digital Services Act (DSA)"

Naming specific laws in the TOC-level summary indicates they crossed the materiality threshold for top-line risk disclosure.

▶ "Platform Integrity" added to risk category header New

Q2 2023:

"Risks Related to Data, Security, and Intellectual Property"

Q3 2023:

"Risks Related to Data, Security, Platform Integrity, and Intellectual Property"
▶ "Misinformation and deepfakes (including related to elections)" added to AI risk New

Q3 2023 added election-specific AI risk language within the AI risk factor, referencing:

"...harmful or illegal content, accuracy, misinformation and deepfakes (including related to elections), bias, discrimination..."

Q2 2023 used "harmful content" without the "illegal" qualifier and did not mention deepfakes or elections.

▶ RL loss timeline shifts from "2023 and beyond" to "increase meaningfully in 2024" Changed

Q3 2023 MDA shifted the forward-looking RL loss guidance away from "2023 and beyond" and introduced 2024-specific language. The capex section also gave first 2024 guidance:

"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $27 billion to $29 billion and $30 billion to $35 billion in 2023 and 2024, respectively."

Q3 2023 → Q1 2024

Annual Boundary | Headcount: 66K → 69K (trough passed) | Capex: $30-35B (2024 guidance in Q3 2023) → $35-40B
▶ AI risk factor major rewrite: EU AI Act named, "data to train our models," "spear phishing" Changed

Q1 2024 expanded the AI risk factor substantially, adding:

"...which are applying, or are considering applying, platform moderation, intellectual property, cybersecurity, export controls, consumer protection, and data protection laws to AI and/or are considering general legal frameworks on AI (such as the EU AI Act)."

Also added open-source AI risk with specific attack vectors:

"...cybersecurity attacks including spear phishing attacks, data privacy violations, other activities that threaten people's safety or well-being on- or offline..."
▶ "Mixed reality" added to RL segment description New

Q3 2023 described RL as "virtual and augmented reality." Q1 2024 added:

"RL includes our virtual, augmented, and mixed reality related consumer hardware, software, and content."
▶ "Unconnected" dropped from AI content recommendation Removed

Q3 2023:

"...recommend relevant unconnected content across our products..."

Q1 2024:

"...recommend relevant content across our products..."

The qualifier "unconnected" was dropped — AI recommendations were no longer limited to content from accounts users don't follow.

▶ Capex raised to $35-40B Signal
"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $35 billion to $40 billion in 2024."

Up from $30-35B guided in Q3 2023 — a $5B raise at both ends of the range within one quarter.

Q1 2024 → Q2 2024

Headcount: 69K → 71K | Capex floor raised: $35B → $37B
▶ "Data combination" and UK DMCC first appear in risk factor TOC New

Q1 2024 risk factor TOC:

"complex and evolving U.S. and foreign privacy, data use and data protection, content, competition..."

Q2 2024 risk factor TOC:

"complex and evolving U.S. and foreign privacy, data use, data combination, data protection, content, competition, consumer protection, and other laws and regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Digital Markets Act (DMA), Digital Services Act (DSA), and the UK Digital Markets, Competition and Consumer Act (DMCC)"

"Data combination" as a standalone regulatory category and the UK DMCC as a named statute both appear for the first time.

▶ "Infrastructure and headcount" investment warning first appears in risk factor New

Q2 2024 risk factor added the sentence:

"In particular, we expect our AI initiatives will require increased investment in infrastructure and headcount. If our investments are not successful longer-term, our business and financial performance could be harmed."

Q1 2024 had the same "infrastructure and headcount" phrase, but Q2 2024 added the explicit downside warning sentence.

▶ First 2025 capex growth signal Signal
"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $37 billion to $40 billion in 2024. We currently expect our capital expenditures to grow significantly in 2025 as we invest to support our AI research and product development efforts."

The $35B floor was raised to $37B, and the first forward signal for 2025 capex growth appeared.

Q2 2024 → Q3 2024

Headcount: 71K → 72K | Capex: $37-40B → $38-40B
▶ "Computing power, and related energy requirements" added to AI infrastructure risks New

Q3 2024 added energy as a named constraint on AI deployment:

"...our ability to continue to develop and effectively deploy AI technologies is dependent on access to specific third-party equipment, technology, and other technical and physical infrastructure, such as processing hardware, network capacity, models, computing power, and related energy requirements..."
▶ Tariffs shift from future possibility to existing reality Changed

Q3 2024 risk factors moved tariff language from a hypothetical to a present-tense description:

"...could result in tariffs, sanctions, export or import controls, and other measures that restrict international trade, could reduce or eliminate the ability of our suppliers, manufacturers, or other third-party providers to continue their operations..."
▶ EU AI Act added to named regulatory frameworks New

Q3 2024 risk factor TOC added the EU AI Act:

"...including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Digital Markets Act (DMA), Digital Services Act (DSA), Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act), and the UK Digital Markets, Competition and Consumer Act (DMCC)"
▶ Capex narrowed to $38-40B with 2025 growth confirmation Signal
"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $38 billion to $40 billion in 2024 and continue to expect significant capital expenditures growth in 2025."

Q3 2024 → Q1 2025

Annual Boundary | Headcount: 72K → 77K | Capex nearly doubles: $38-40B (2024) → $64-72B (2025)
▶ "Discipline and habits" disappears from Investment Philosophy Removed

Q3 2024 MDA still contained the "discipline and habits" framing. Q1 2025 MDA replaced it with:

"We remain focused on operating efficiently while investing in significant opportunities."

The specific phrase "discipline and habits" — present since Q2 2023 — was removed after a 7-quarter lifecycle.

▶ "Reality Labs strategy" replaces "metaverse strategy" Changed

Q3 2024 risk factor:

"We may not be successful in our metaverse strategy and investments, which could adversely affect our business, reputation, or financial results."

Q1 2025 risk factor:

"We may not be successful in our Reality Labs strategy and investments, which could adversely affect our business, reputation, or financial results."
▶ "Next computing platform" replaces "next chapter of the internet" Changed

Q3 2024 MDA:

"We are investing now because we believe this is the next chapter of the internet..."

Q1 2025 MDA:

"We are investing now because we believe this will become the next computing platform..."
▶ "Content moderation" added to regulatory categories; Meta Verified added to revenue New

Q1 2025 risk factor TOC added "content and content moderation" as a regulatory category:

"complex and evolving U.S. and foreign privacy, data use, data combination, data protection, content and content moderation, competition, youth, safety, consumer protection, advertising, and other laws and regulations..."

Q1 2025 MDA also added Meta Verified to the FoA Other revenue definition:

"Other revenue consists of revenue from WhatsApp Business Platform, Meta Verified subscriptions, net fees we receive from developers using our Payments infrastructure, and revenue from various other sources."
▶ Capex nearly doubles to $64-72B Signal
"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $64 billion to $72 billion in 2025 to support our core business and generative AI efforts."

From $38-40B (2024) to $64-72B (2025) — the midpoint nearly doubled in one annual boundary crossing.

Q1 2025 → Q2 2025

Headcount: 77K → 76K | Capex: $64-72B → $66-72B
▶ "SUPERINTELLIGENCE" first appears in SEC filing New

Q1 2025 risk factor:

"We are making significant investments in AI initiatives, including generative AI, to, among other things, recommend relevant content across our products..."

Q2 2025 risk factor:

"We are making significant investments in AI initiatives, including generative AI and superintelligence, to, among other things, recommend relevant content across our products, enhance our advertising tools, develop new products, and develop new features for existing products."

"Superintelligence" inserted into the standard AI investment sentence alongside "generative AI" — not detectable from annual filings alone.

▶ "Paid messaging from WhatsApp" replaces "WhatsApp Business Platform" in revenue Changed

Q1 2025 MDA:

"Other revenue consists of revenue from WhatsApp Business Platform, Meta Verified subscriptions..."

Q2 2025 MDA:

"Other revenue consists of revenue from paid messaging from WhatsApp, Meta Verified subscriptions..."

Renaming from a platform brand to a product description signals consumer-facing messaging monetization beyond business API.

▶ "Generative" dropped from capex attribution Changed

Q1 2025:

"...to support our core business and generative AI efforts."

Q2 2025:

"...to support our core business and AI efforts."

Dropping "generative" broadens the capex justification from one AI category to all AI investment.

▶ Capex floor raised $64B to $66B Signal
"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $66 billion to $72 billion in 2025 to support our core business and AI efforts."

Headcount: 76K → 78K | Capex: $66-72B → $70-72B + 2026 growth signal
▶ "Products liability" added to AI risk enumeration New

Q3 2025 AI risk factor added "products liability" to the list of AI-specific legal risks:

"...subject us to risks related to harmful or illegal content, accuracy, misinformation and deepfakes (including related to elections), bias, discrimination, toxicity, consumer protection, products liability, intellectual property infringement or misappropriation..."
▶ FTC and Congress investigations named New

Q3 2025 added specific government actors investigating AI:

"...we have been the subject of media and government scrutiny relating to AI and AI chatbots, including inquiries and investigations by the FTC, members of Congress and state attorneys general."
▶ "We have made" replaces "We are making" (past tense shift) Changed

Q2 2025 risk factor:

"We are making significant investments in AI initiatives..."

Q3 2025 risk factor:

"We have made significant investments in AI initiatives, including generative AI and superintelligence..."

Shift from present continuous to present perfect — investment is now an established fact, not an ongoing decision.

▶ "Headcount" dropped from AI investment sentence in MDA Removed

Q2 2025 MDA Investment Philosophy:

"In particular, we expect our AI initiatives will require increased investment in infrastructure and headcount."

Q3 2025 MDA Investment Philosophy:

"In particular, we expect our AI initiatives will require significantly increased investment in infrastructure."

"Headcount" removed; "significantly" added before "increased." The AI investment sentence now frames the commitment as infrastructure-only.

▶ UK OSA added to named regulatory frameworks New

Q3 2025 risk factor TOC:

"...including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Digital Markets Act (DMA), Digital Services Act (DSA), UK Online Safety Act (OSA), Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act), and the UK Digital Markets, Competition and Consumer Act (DMCC)"
▶ Capex $70-72B + 2026 growth signal + $40B cloud commitment Signal
"We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $70 billion to $72 billion in 2025 to support our core business and AI efforts, and expect significant capital expenditures growth in 2026."

Range narrowed from $66-72B to $70-72B. A 2026 growth signal added. The risk factors also disclosed:

"...we will need an increasing amount of technical infrastructure, including network capacity, computing power, and related energy requirements..."

Themes

1. Capex as Leading Indicator

Capex guidance changed every single quarter from Q1 2023 through Q3 2025. The range went from $30-33B to $70-72B with a 2026 growth signal. Annual comparisons would miss the intra-year cuts (Q1-Q2 2023), the sequential floor raises (Q2-Q3 2024), and the forward signals for future years that appeared mid-cycle.

2. "Discipline and Habits" Had a 7-Quarter Lifecycle

First appeared Q2 2023 as an efficiency mantra. Refined to "operating efficiency" framing by Q1 2024. Removed entirely Q1 2025 when the investment posture shifted to AI infrastructure. The quarterly cadence pinpoints the exact quarter this phrase was born and the exact quarter it died — invisible in annual 10-K comparisons.

3. AI From Risk to Identity

AI risk factor went from one paragraph embedded in product risk (Q1 2023) to a multi-page section with "superintelligence" in the investment sentence, "products liability" in the risk enumeration, and FTC/Congress investigations named (Q3 2025). The word "superintelligence" first appeared in Q2 2025 — a signal not detectable from annual filings.

4. Regulatory Names Accumulate Quarterly

DMA/DSA named in TOC (Q3 2023), UK DMCC added (Q2 2024), EU AI Act added (Q3 2024), UK OSA added (Q3 2025). Each addition represents a new enforcement surface detected 3–9 months before the annual filing would capture it. By Q3 2025, the risk factor TOC named 6 specific laws where Q1 2023 named zero.
