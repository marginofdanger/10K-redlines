META Q3 2024 10-Q (quarter ended September 30, 2024; filed October 31, 2024)
Fundamentals answers vs the Q2 2024 10-Q. Figures from SEC XBRL (financials.py) unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [worse: slower, still high]
   Revenue $40.59B, +19% (Q2 +22%); constant currency +20%. Ad impressions +7% (Q2 +10%;
   +31% a year earlier), average price per ad +11% (Q2 +10%), credited to "ongoing
   improvements to our ad performance from our ad targeting and measurement tools." DAP
   3.29B, +5%; ARPP $12.29, +12%. U.S. & Canada +16%, Europe +22%, Asia-Pacific +19%, Rest
   of World +24%. RL revenue +29% ($270M). (MD&A, Revenue; Family metrics)

2. Margins and unit economics [better]
   Operating income $17.35B, +26%; margin 43% vs 40% (Q2 38%). FoA margin 54% vs 52%;
   FoA operating income +25%. Costs +14% vs revenue +19%: cost of revenue +19% on
   depreciation, R&D +21% on a 12% rise in technical headcount, M&S -2%, G&A -10% on lower
   legal costs. RL loss $4.43B, +18%. (MD&A, Segment results; Costs and expenses)

3. Earnings quality [held]
   Net income $15.69B, +35%. ETR 12% vs 17% "mostly due to excess tax benefits recognized
   from share-based compensation and an increase in research tax credits." Interest and
   other income +$200M on higher balances and rates. No large one-offs. (MD&A, Provision
   for income taxes)

4. Cash conversion [better]
   Q3 OCF $24.72B; OCF less purchases of property and equipment $16.47B vs $13.76B a
   year earlier (+20%), 105% of net income. Nine-month OCF $63.34B, +22%, despite "higher
   cash paid for income taxes" (R&D capitalization). DSO 33 days (Q2 34). SBC 17% of OCF.
   (MD&A, Liquidity; financials.py)

5. Capital allocation [held]
   Capex incl. finance leases $9.20B. Buybacks $8.86B, dividends $1.26B. Nine-month
   capital returns $33.93B plus $9.91B of RSU net-settlement taxes ($43.84B) exceeded OCF
   less capex ($38.95B); $10.43B of notes issued in August. $51.28B authorization left.
   (MD&A, Liquidity; Capital Return Program)

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [worse]
   Notes $29.0B principal (from $18.5B). Cash and securities $70.90B. Non-cancelable
   commitments $32.14B (Q2 $19.82B), "mostly related to" servers, network and RL hardware.
   Operating leases not yet commenced $18.49B (Q2 $6.20B), commencing through 2029.
   Renewable energy agreements ~$18.01B estimated spend. (Notes 8, 10, 12)

7. Concentration [held]
   No change to the China reseller disclosure. Risk factors move tariff language to the
   present tense and add "computing power, and related energy requirements" to AI
   infrastructure dependencies. (Risk Factors)

8. Accounting judgments [held]
   "There have been no material changes to our critical accounting estimates" since the
   FY2023 10-K. Restructuring charges continue to roll off. (MD&A, Critical Accounting
   Estimates)

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [held]
   Legal-related accruals $7.48B (Q2 $7.96B). EU Marketplace case: "we have accrued a
   significant amount." DMA preliminary finding (July 2024) that "subscription for no
   ads" does not comply. EU DSA proceedings on minors and elections. New Mexico AG trial set
   for May 2025; Delaware derivative trial April 2025. IRS 2010 transfer-pricing case
   still in Tax Court; uncertain tax positions $9.17B. (Note 12; Note 14; Legal Proceedings)

10. Guidance and commitments [held / moved]
   2024 capex $37-40B -> $38-40B, plus "significant capital expenditures growth in 2025."
   Q4 2024 tax rate "in the low-teens." RL losses "to increase meaningfully in 2024"
   reaffirmed. Dividend intent restated.
