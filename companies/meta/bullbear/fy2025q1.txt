META Q1 2025 10-Q (quarter ended March 31, 2025; filed May 1, 2025)
Fundamentals answers vs the FY2024 10-K (and the same quarter of 2024). Figures from SEC XBRL
(financials.py) unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [worse]
   Revenue $42.31B, +16% (FY2024 +22%), the slowest quarter in the window; constant
   currency +19%. Ad impressions +5% (+20% a year earlier), price per ad +10% (+6%). DAP
   3.43B, +6%. U.S. & Canada +18%, Europe +14%, Asia-Pacific +13%, Rest of World +20%. RL
   revenue -6% (Quest down, Ray-Ban Meta up). Macro paragraph adds "volatility around
   international trade." (MD&A, Revenue; Other Business and Macroeconomic Conditions)

2. Margins and unit economics [held]
   Operating income $17.56B, +27%; margin 41% vs 38%. FoA margin 52% vs 49%. Costs +9%:
   cost of revenue +14% (with a "decrease in the depreciation growth rate" from longer
   server lives), R&D +22%, G&A -34% "primarily driven by lower legal-related costs." RL
   loss $4.21B, +9%. "Discipline and habits" language replaced by "operating
   efficiently." (MD&A, Costs and expenses; Investment Philosophy)

3. Earnings quality [held: flattered]
   Net income $16.64B, +35%. G&A down $1.18B on legal costs and ETR 9% on SBC excess tax
   benefits flatter growth; ex the G&A decline, operating income about +19% (computed).
   (MD&A, G&A; Provision for income taxes)

4. Cash conversion [worse]
   OCF $24.03B, +25%. OCF less purchases of property and equipment $11.09B vs $12.85B
   (-14%), 67% of net income: the first year-on-year decline in the window. Cash taxes
   $448M, with a $3.72B payment in April. DSO 31 days. (MD&A, Liquidity; financials.py)

5. Capital allocation [held]
   Capex incl. finance leases $13.69B (vs $6.72B). Buybacks $13.40B; dividend raised to
   $0.525 from $0.50 ($1.33B paid). Capital returns $14.08B plus RSU taxes $4.88B =
   $18.96B vs OCF less capex incl. finance leases $10.34B. Authorization $37.95B left.
   (MD&A, Liquidity; Capital Return Program)

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [worse]
   Cash and securities $70.23B, down $7.58B, the first decline in the window. Notes
   $29.0B. Leases not yet commenced ~$35.27B (commencing through 2034). Commitments
   $30.05B, now "primarily related to" servers, network infrastructure and content
   costs. Uncertain tax positions $10.92B. (MD&A, Liquidity; Notes)

7. Concentration [worse]
   Europe (by customer address) $9.62B, 23% of revenue, now exposed to DMA ad-model
   changes. Trade-policy uncertainty named in the MD&A; tariff risk language expanded.
   China reseller language unchanged. (Note 2; MD&A; Risk Factors)

8. Accounting judgments [worse]
   Useful-life extension in effect; the 10-Q describes only a "decrease in the
   depreciation growth rate" and gives no quarterly benefit. No change to critical
   estimates. (MD&A, Cost of revenue)

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [worse]
   DMA: April 2025 decision that "subscription for no ads" does not comply, fine EUR 200M;
   Meta expects modifications that could cause "a significant impact to our European
   business and revenue as early as the third quarter of 2025." FTC antitrust trial began
   April 14, 2025. EU Marketplace fine under appeal. (Note on Commitments and
   Contingencies; Legal Proceedings)

10. Guidance and commitments [moved]
   2025 capex $60-65B -> $64-72B one quarter after it was set. 2025 tax rate 12-15%
   reaffirmed. RL losses "to increase in 2025" reaffirmed.
