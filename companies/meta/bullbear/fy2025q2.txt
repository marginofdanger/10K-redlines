META Q2 2025 10-Q (quarter ended June 30, 2025; filed July 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers vs the Q1 2025 10-Q. Figures from SEC XBRL (financials.py) unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [better]
   Revenue $47.52B, +22% (Q1 +16%). Ad impressions +11% (Q1 +5%), price per ad +9% (Q1
   +10%). DAP 3.48B, +6%. U.S. & Canada +21%, Europe +24%, Asia-Pacific +19%, Rest of World
   +24%. FoA other revenue +50%, "mostly driven by paid messaging from WhatsApp and Meta
   Verified subscriptions." RL revenue +5% on Ray-Ban Meta AI glasses. "Superintelligence"
   enters the AI investment sentence. (MD&A, Revenue; Investment Philosophy)

2. Margins and unit economics [better]
   Operating income $20.44B, +38%; margin 43% vs 38%, the high point of the window. FoA
   margin 53% vs 50%. Costs +12%: cost of revenue +16%, R&D +23%, M&S +9%, G&A -27% on
   lower legal costs. RL loss $4.53B, +1%. (MD&A, Segment results)

3. Earnings quality [held]
   Net income $18.34B, +36%. Lower G&A (-$995M) still helps; ex that, operating income
   about +31% (computed). Tax Court opinion led to a remeasurement that "increased" the
   tax provision (amount not given); ETR 11%. (MD&A; Note on Income Taxes)

4. Cash conversion [worse]
   OCF $25.56B, +32%; OCF less purchases of property and equipment $9.02B vs $11.20B
   (-19%), 49% of net income. OBBBA (enacted July 4) expected to cut U.S. federal cash
   taxes "for the remainder of 2025 and future years." H1 cash taxes $5.54B. DSO 32 days.
   (MD&A, Liquidity; Provision for income taxes)

5. Capital allocation [worse]
   Capex incl. finance leases $17.01B; 2025 guide floor raised to $66B. Scale AI stake:
   $13.79B in non-marketable equity (measurement alternative); H1 equity investments
   $15.21B. Buybacks $9.76B, dividends $1.33B. (MD&A, Liquidity; Notes)

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [worse]
   Cash and securities $47.07B, down $30.74B in H1: uses of $80.48B vs $49.59B of OCF
   (computed). Leases not yet commenced ~$52.56B (Q1 $35.27B). Commitments $27.95B, now
   including data centers. Notes $29.0B. Uncertain tax positions $12.05B. (MD&A,
   Liquidity; Leases and Contractual Commitments)

7. Concentration [held]
   No change to China reseller language. Europe grew 24% despite less personalized ads
   modifications made after the DMA decision. (MD&A; Risk Factors)

8. Accounting judgments [held]
   Scale AI carried at cost under the measurement alternative (no fair-value marks unless
   an observable price change or impairment). No change to critical estimates. (Notes)

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [worse]
   Tax Court (May 22, 2025) valued the 2010 IP transfer at $7.79B, "$1.48 billion higher
   than we reported"; IRS applies the same method to later years. DMA decision appealed
   July 4, 2025; impact window now "as early as later in the third quarter of 2025."
   (Note on Income Taxes; Legal Proceedings)

10. Guidance and commitments [moved]
   2025 capex $64-72B -> $66-72B, now "to support our core business and AI efforts"
   ("generative" dropped). 2025 tax rate now expected to be "higher than our tax rate in
   the second quarter of 2025" because of OBBBA (not quantified). RL losses to increase in
   2025 reaffirmed.
