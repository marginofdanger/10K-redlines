META Q2 2026 10-Q (quarter ended June 30, 2026; filed July 30, 2026)
Fundamentals answers vs the Q1 2026 10-Q. Figures from SEC XBRL (financials.py) unless quoted.

1. Growth and pricing [worse: decelerating from a high level]
   Revenue $60.80B, +28% (Q1 +33%); constant currency +27% (Q1 +29%). Ad impressions +14%
   (Q1 +19%), price per ad +12% (Q1 +12%). Europe +24% (Q1 +39%), Asia-Pacific +19%
   (+29%), U.S. & Canada +32% (+29%). DAP 3.60B, +3%; the Q1 Iran disruption "largely
   restored." Other revenue +73% ($1.01B); "Meta Verified" no longer named. RL revenue
   +16%, led by AI glasses. Macro "has impacted" results. (MD&A, Revenue; Key metrics)

2. Margins and unit economics [worse]
   Operating income $18.78B, -8% YoY; margin 31% vs 43% a year earlier and 41% in Q1.
   Costs +55%: R&D +67% (36% of revenue), G&A +111% ($2.40B legal charges), M&S +15% on
   "third-party AI token costs and severance expenses." FoA operating income -6%. Excluding
   $1.18B severance and $2.40B legal charges, about $22.36B, +9% (computed). (MD&A, Costs
   and Expenses; Segment Results)

3. Earnings quality [better: fewer flattering items]
   Q1 net income ($26.77B) included an $8.03B CAMT relief benefit; Q2 ($15.85B) has none
   and instead carries $3.58B of one-off charges (severance, legal). ETR 16%. H1 loss on
   equity investments $1.05B (unrealized marks). (MD&A, Provision for Income Taxes;
   Interest and other income)

4. Cash conversion [worse]
   Q2 OCF $31.86B against purchases of property and equipment of $30.12B, so OCF less
   capex was $1.75B (Q1 $13.23B); FCF / net income 11%. H1 OCF $64.09B, +29%, helped
   by "a decrease in cash tax payments." Receivables $21.75B, DSO 33 days (Q1 28): no
   sentence explains it. SBC $7.66B = 24% of OCF. (MD&A, Liquidity; financials.py)

5. Capital allocation [worse]
   H1 capex incl. finance leases $50.92B; 2026 guide floor raised to $130B. No share
   repurchases in H1 ($25.03B authorization unused). Dividends $1.35B per quarter held.
   $25.00B of notes issued in May (4.55%-6.45%). El Paso venture agreed (contribute ~$2.3B
   assets, receive ~$1B). 20M stock options granted in H1. (MD&A, Liquidity; Notes 8, 10)

   Notes outstanding $84.00B (Q1 $59.00B); interest expense +225% YoY. Cash and

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [worse]
