Latest filing: 10-Q Q2 2026

What this filing adds, for and against. 3-6 of the most material findings per side; a side with nothing material says so.

Bull case

P1 Core ad engineStated
Price per ad +12% for a second straight quarter; ad revenue +$12.80B (+27%) YoY
Q2 2026: "During the three and six months ended June 30, 2026, the average price per ad increased by 12% in both periods, year-over-year, as compared with increases of 9% and 10%, respectively, in the same periods in 2025."

Size: ad revenue $59.36B in Q2, 98% of total revenue; constant-currency revenue growth 27%

Pricing, not just ad load, is still rising, which the filing attributes to "ongoing improvements to our ad performance from our ad targeting and measurement tools." That is the clearest evidence in the filing that AI spending is reaching the core product.

Next filing: price per ad growth holds near 10%+ as impression growth slows; a fall in price with rising impressions would point to ad load doing the work.

P3 AI capex return and cash conversionStated
H1 operating cash flow $64.09B, +29% YoY, outgrowing revenue (+30%) after taxes fell
Q2 2026: "The increase in cash flows from operating activities during the six months ended June 30, 2026, compared to the same period in 2025, was due primarily to an increase in cash collections from our customers driven by higher revenue, and a decrease in cash tax payments, partially offset by higher operational spending."

Size: H1 OCF $64.09B vs $49.59B (+$14.50B); cash taxes paid $2.00B in H1

The engine that funds the build is still compounding, and the OBBBA expensing rules lower cash taxes even while CAMT raises the book rate. Part of the gain is tax timing, not operations.

Next filing: OCF growth stays above 20% without a further drop in cash taxes; receivables (DSO up to 33 days from 28) should not keep rising.

P6 Legal, regulatory and tax exposureStated
The one tried youth demand shrank: New Mexico abatement $3.7B → $953M; Flo Health class 1.6M → 1.25M
Q2 2026: "Following that trial, the New Mexico Attorney General is seeking $953 million in abatement costs and a broad set of injunctive terms, which include requests for extensive changes to the manner in which we provide our services in New Mexico."
Q2 2026: "Plaintiffs are seeking $5,000 in statutory damages per class member and have asserted that there are up to approximately 1.25 million class members."

Size: NM demand down ~$2.75B (74%); Flo ceiling $8.0B → $6.25B (computed); together ~$4.5B lower, about 24% of Q2 operating income

When claims reach a courtroom, the numbers have come down. The second user bellwether was dismissed by the plaintiff and the first school-district bellwether settled.

Next filing: the NM court decision on abatement and the August 2026 MDL state-AG trial; a large award would refute this.

P2 Margins and operating disciplineInferred
~8,000-person reduction (about 11% of reported headcount) should lower run-rate compensation from Q3
Q2 2026: "Our reported headcount includes approximately 8,000 employees impacted by the May 2026 headcount reduction, the majority of whom will no longer be reflected in our headcount by the end of the third quarter of 2026."

Size: savings not quantified in the filing; $1.18B severance in Q2; 8,000 / 75,472 = 11% (computed)

Ex-severance and the $2.40B legal charge, Q2 operating income was about $22.36B, up ~9% (computed). The cut is the first concrete response to the cost growth that FY2025 flagged.

Next filing: Q3 headcount near 67-68K and R&D growth slowing below revenue growth excluding SBC.

P7 Reality Labs pathStated
RL revenue +16% with AI glasses now the lead driver; H1 RL loss $8.65B vs $8.74B, on the "similar to 2025" guide
Q2 2026: "The increases were driven by higher sales of AI glasses, partially offset by lower Meta Quest sales."

Size: RL revenue $431M in Q2 (0.7% of revenue); H1 RL loss down $92M (1%)

Q1 described the reverse order (Quest decline offset by glasses). The 70% wearables budget from the FY2025 10-K is showing in revenue mix, and losses have stopped growing.

Next filing: full-year RL loss tracking to about $19B; glasses growth continuing in the holiday half.

Bear case

P3 AI capex return and cash conversionStated
Capex floor raised to $130B while Q2 purchases of property and equipment ($30.12B) used 95% of Q2 operating cash flow
Q2 2026: "We anticipate making capital expenditures of approximately $130 billion to $145 billion in 2026 to support our AI efforts and core business."

Size: Q2 OCF less capex $1.75B (11% of net income) vs $13.23B in Q1; H2 needs ~$79B of capex to reach the floor, 1.6x H1 (computed)

Free cash flow is effectively gone at the current guide, and the filing states no return or utilization measure for the spend. Second-half capex is likely to exceed operating cash flow.

Next filing: Q3 OCF less capex; whether the $145B ceiling moves; any stated return metric or utilization disclosure.

P4 Balance sheet and off-balance-sheet obligationsStated
Debt now funds the build: notes $59.00B → $84.00B; commitments $349.31B; unstarted leases $278.99B plus ~$68B in July
Q2 2026: "We have incurred significant indebtedness and other contractual commitments, including lease obligations, to finance these investments and expect to continue to incur additional obligations."

Size: commitments plus unstarted leases $628.30B, up ~$208B in one quarter, about 3.1x FY2025 revenue (computed); interest expense +225% YoY

Most of the stack is non-cancelable and sits outside debt. The filing also says the commitment total counts variable-term agreements only at minimums, and El Paso adds up to ~$13B of guarantees.

Next filing: another notes issue or equity-linked financing; commitments and lease totals; maximum exposure on the ventures.

P2 Margins and operating disciplineStated
Operating income −8% on +28% revenue; costs +55%, with "third-party AI token costs" a new named driver
Q2 2026: "The increase in costs and expenses was primarily due to increases in employee compensation, including severance expenses; infrastructure expenses related to our data centers, technical infrastructure, and third-party cloud services; legal-related costs; and third-party AI token costs."

Size: operating margin 31% vs 43% a year earlier; R&D 36% of revenue vs 27%; token costs not quantified

Even ex one-offs, operating income grew ~9% against 28% revenue growth (computed). Depreciation, cloud capacity and token costs are recurring and will rise with the capex already committed.

Next filing: Q3 margin without severance; whether token costs are quantified; FoA operating income growth.

P6 Legal, regulatory and tax exposureStated
$2.40B of legal charges in one quarter; youth claims restated to "more than a trillion dollars"
Q2 2026: "The increases were primarily due to $2.40 billion of charges related to legal proceedings in the three months ended June 30, 2026."
Q2 2026: "Across the cases described above, the damages, disgorgement, or penalties that plaintiffs have indicated they could seek range widely in amount, including in certain cases up to more than a trillion dollars."

Size: $2.40B = 13% of Q2 operating income (computed); the trillion-dollar figure is theoretical and not accrued

Legal cost has become a recurring line, not a one-off, with 29 state AGs seeking disgorgement, an EU preliminary finding on "potentially addictive design features," and nine new AI-copyright suits.

Next filing: Q3 legal charges; the MDL state-AG trial outcome; any EU DSA decision.

P5 Capital return and dilutionStated
No buybacks for a second quarter while SBC reached 24% of operating cash flow
Q2 2026: "We did not repurchase any shares of Class A common stock during the six months ended June 30, 2026."

Size: H1 2026 repurchases $0 vs $22.92B in H1 2025; Q2 SBC $7.66B; $25.03B authorization unused

Without repurchases, SBC and the new stock options (20M granted in H1) are no longer offset. Diluted shares have stopped falling (+0.1% QoQ).

Next filing: any resumption of repurchases, and the diluted share count trend.

P1 Core ad engineStated
Growth slowed in every region but North America; macro risk restated as "has impacted"
Q2 2026: "In the second quarter of 2026, revenue increased by 32% in United States & Canada, 24% in Europe, 19% in Asia-Pacific, and 36% in Rest of World, in each case relative to the same period in 2025."
Q2 2026: "We are currently subject to increased business, macroeconomic, and geopolitical uncertainty, including as a result of the conflict in the Middle East and volatility around international trade, which has impacted and could continue to impact our financial results."

Size: total growth 33% → 28%; Europe 39% → 24%; Asia-Pacific 29% → 19%; FX benefit $685M vs $1.75B in Q1

Part of Q1's strength was currency; ex-FX the slowdown is smaller (29% to 27%). The macro hit is stated but not quantified.

Next filing: constant-currency growth and impression growth; whether Asia-Pacific stabilizes.
