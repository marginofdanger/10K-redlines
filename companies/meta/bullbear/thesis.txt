META INVESTMENT THESIS: PILLARS
Written October 2026 from the 10-K dashboard themes (Capital Structure Transformation,
Legal Risk Compounding, Metric Transparency Declining, The Superintelligence Bet), the
10-Q scanner's tracked signals (capex guidance, commitments, RL loss guidance, legal
exposure) and the eight filings from the Q3 2024 10-Q (filed Oct 31, 2024) through the
Q2 2026 10-Q (filed Jul 30, 2026). Stances are as of the Q2 2026 10-Q. Every pillar
started the window at Intact; a stance moves one step only when findings in two or more
filings point the same way, and "Path" records each move and the filing that made it.

P1 Core ad engine
  Bull assumes: Family of Apps advertising keeps compounding 20%+ on both volume
    (impressions) and price, helped by AI ranking and new surfaces (Threads, WhatsApp).
  Bear fears: growth is leaning on ad load and FX; user growth is saturating (first DAP
    dip Q1 2026); regulation (EU less-personalized ads, youth laws, scam rules) caps
    targeting.
  Stance: Strengthening.
  Path: Intact -> Strengthening at the Q3 2025 10-Q (revenue growth 16% in Q1 2025, then
    22% and 26%, with impressions and price both rising). Q1 2026 (+33%) confirmed; Q2
    2026 (+28%, impressions +14%) is one filing the other way and does not move it.
  Tested by: ad revenue growth, impressions vs price split, DAP, regional growth, the
    EU ads model, ad frequency language, and any change to the China-based advertiser
    disclosure.

P2 Margins and operating discipline
  Bull assumes: "operating efficiently" holds; cost growth stays near revenue growth;
    headcount cuts offset AI and legal costs.
  Bear fears: AI infrastructure, depreciation, SBC, token costs and legal charges grow
    faster than revenue; the 2022-23 efficiency era is over.
  Stance: Weakening (operating margin 43% Q3 2024, 42% FY2024, 43% Q2 2025 peak, 40% Q3
    2025, 41% FY2025, 41% Q1 2026 with FoA margin 52% -> 48%, 31% Q2 2026).
  Path: Intact -> Strengthening at the FY2024 10-K (Q3 2024 and FY2024 margin expansion);
    -> Intact at the FY2025 10-K (Q3 2025 first YoY margin decline, FY2025 41% vs 42%);
    -> Weakening at the Q2 2026 10-Q (Q1 2026 FoA margin down 4 points, Q2 2026 31%).
  Tested by: cost growth vs revenue growth by line, FoA operating margin, named cost
    drivers, severance and legal one-offs, the depreciation effect of longer server lives.

P3 AI capex return and cash conversion
  Bull assumes: capex ($39B in 2024, $72B in 2025, $130-145B guided for 2026) earns a
    return through better ads and new AI products, and operating cash flow keeps pace.
  Bear fears: capex outruns cash generation, free cash flow goes to zero or negative,
    and the spend is sized for frontier models rather than measurable demand.
  Stance: Weakening.
  Path: Intact -> Weakening at the Q2 2025 10-Q (OCF less capex down year on year in Q1
    and Q2 2025 while the 2025 guide was raised twice). Q3 2025, FY2025 and Q2 2026
    ($1.75B) confirmed; the annual guide was raised six times in the window after being
    set. Broken would need OCF less capex negative for a full half-year.
  Tested by: OCF less capex each quarter, capex guide moves, any stated return or
    utilization metric, impairment language, cash taxes.

P4 Balance sheet and off-balance-sheet obligations
  Bull assumes: cash and securities ($70-90B) and investment-grade debt make the build
    easy to fund.
  Bear fears: debt ($29B -> $84B), non-cancelable commitments ($32B Sep 2024 -> $349B),
    unstarted leases ($18B -> $279B + $68B) and venture guarantees become a fixed-cost
    stack that cannot be cut if AI demand disappoints.
  Stance: Weakening.
  Path: Intact -> Weakening at the Q3 2025 10-Q (Q2 2025 cash and securities down $30.74B
    in six months; Q3 2025 commitments tripled to $81.19B plus $40B, and the liquidity
    sentence added outside financing). FY2025, Q1 2026 and Q2 2026 confirmed.
  Tested by: notes outstanding and coupons, commitments and lease totals, escrow, VIE
    maximum exposure, RVGs, interest expense.

P5 Capital return and dilution
  Bull assumes: buybacks and the dividend continue from free cash flow and offset SBC.
  Bear fears: buybacks stop (demoted to "any return of capital" in FY2025, $0 since),
    SBC rises, stock options appear, share count drifts up.
  Stance: Weakening.
  Path: Intact through Q2 2025 (dividend started 2024 and raised Feb 2025; FY2024 returns
    inside FCF, but Q1 2025 returns exceeded FCF); -> Weakening at the FY2025 10-K (Q3
    2025 buybacks -64% to $3.16B; FY2025 "any return of capital" and ~$0 in Q4). Q1 and
    Q2 2026 ($0) confirmed.
  Tested by: repurchases, dividends, SBC / OCF, diluted share count, new equity issuance.

P6 Legal, regulatory and tax exposure
  Bull assumes: Meta wins the existential cases (FTC antitrust won Nov 2025), and the
    rest settle for amounts that are small relative to earnings.
  Bear fears: youth/addiction litigation (state AGs, MDL, mass arbitration), EU DSA/DMA
    orders, AI copyright, and the IRS transfer-pricing case produce recurring
    multi-billion charges and product constraints.
  Stance: Weakening.
  Path: Intact -> Weakening at the Q3 2025 10-Q (Q2 2025 Tax Court opinion against Meta's
    IP value; Q3 2025 $15.89B IRS notice, "hundreds of billions" aggregate exposure, G&A
    +88% on legal). FY2025 FTC win is the main offset; Q1 2026 verdicts and Q2 2026
    $2.40B of charges confirmed.
  Tested by: legal charges, verdicts and amounts sought, the aggregate-exposure
    sentence, EU findings and fines, IRS/Tax Court outcome, uncertain tax positions.

P7 Reality Labs path
  Bull assumes: losses plateau around $19B and AI glasses become a real product line.
  Bear fears: RL keeps losing ~$19B a year with revenue near $2B and inventory
    commitments add losses.
  Stance: Strengthening.
  Path: Intact -> Strengthening at the Q1 2026 10-Q (Q3 2025 RL loss flat, FY2025 guide
    "remain similar to 2025," Q1 2026 RL loss -4%). Q2 2026 (H1 loss -1%, AI glasses now
    the lead revenue driver) confirmed.
  Tested by: RL operating loss vs "remain similar to 2025," RL revenue and the glasses vs
    Quest mix, the RL spending split.
