MICROSOFT (MSFT) 10-K FOR FY2026 (filed July 29, 2026; fiscal year ended June 30, 2026)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2025 10-K. Figures from SEC XBRL and the filing text.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $331.84B, +18% (FY2025 +15%). "Microsoft Cloud revenue increased 27% to $214.4 billion"
   (+23%). Azure and other cloud services +41% (34%). Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud +17%, seats +6%.
   "Commercial remaining performance obligation increased 84% to $678 billion", 30% due in 12 months
   (40%), weighted-average duration 2.3 years. OpenAI revenue $24.1B, 7.3% of the total.

2. Margins and unit economics [WORSE]
   Microsoft Cloud gross margin 66% (69%) "driven by continued investments in AI infrastructure and
   growing AI product usage". Company gross margin 67.9% (68.8%). Operating margin 46.8% (45.6%):
   operating leverage offset the gross-margin decline. Headcount 223,000 (228,000).

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $133.75B (+31%) includes $6.5B pre-tax ($5.0B after tax) of OpenAI gains, mostly the
   dilution gain from the October 2025 recapitalization; adjusted net income $128.79B (+22%). FY2025
   carried a $3.6B after-tax OpenAI loss. Effective tax rate 19.4% (17.6%). XBOX impairment cited,
   not quantified.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $182.94B (+$46.8B, +34%), "primarily due to an increase in cash received from
   customers". Capex $115.95B (+80%). FCF $66.99B (-6%); FCF / net income 50% (70%). DSO 89 days (91).
   Current portion of server-component receivables $27.8B ($8.2B).

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Buybacks under the program $16.7B ($13.0B); dividends declared $27.0B ($24.7B); together $43.7B,
   65% of FCF. $40.6B of the $60.0B authorization left. OpenAI funding $11.9B of $13.0B. Capex
   funded from operating cash flow; no new long-term debt.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Cash and short-term investments $76.8B ($94.6B). Long-term debt $31.07B ($40.15B). Leases not
   yet commenced $329.1B ($92.7B), "with some arrangements subject to certain contractual conditions
   being met", commencing FY2027-FY2033. Restricted supplier investments $11.3B. Finance lease
   liabilities $66.6B.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   OpenAI first disclosed as a related party: $24.1B revenue (7.3%), $6.0B receivable (7.4% of
   receivables). New risk factor: partners that are "significant customers of Azure" may reduce
   consumption. Supplier under the restricted-investment agreement not named.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   RPO a critical accounting estimate ("potential renegotiation of commitments"), as in the Q2 and
   Q3 FY2026 10-Qs. HLBV method for OpenAI. Microsoft 365 Consumer subscribers removed as a metric;
   Gaming renamed XBOX. Useful-life range unchanged at two to six years, relabeled from "computer
   equipment" to "servers and network equipment".

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Tariffs and export controls "has and may continue to increase operational costs" (could). New
   risk factor on AI capex "in advance of fully developed revenue streams" that "may lead to
   impairment of assets". AI "will likely continue to raise energy use and emissions". IRS NOPAs
   $28.9B unchanged; reasonably possible losses $400M ($600M).

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   No numeric guidance. Forward-capex sentence: "We will continue to invest in capital expenditures"
   (purpose now "AI training and other infrastructure"). 2030 sustainability goals restated with
   the AI concession. Lease commencement window extended to FY2033.
