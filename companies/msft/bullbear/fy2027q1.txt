MICROSOFT (MSFT) Q1 FY2027 10-Q (filed October 28, 2026; quarter ended September 30, 2026)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2026 10-K (growth rates are year on year against Q1
FY2026). Figures from SEC XBRL and the filing text. Segments, Azure and Microsoft Cloud are reported
on new definitions from this quarter, with prior periods recast.

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   Revenue $92.28B, +19% (FY2026 +18%). Agents and Infra $76.80B, +25%; Devices and Consumer
   $15.48B, -3%. "Azure revenue increased $10.8 billion or 48%" (new definition, no old-basis
   figure); Microsoft Cloud $63.9B, +30% (new definition). Microsoft 365 commercial cloud +17% on
   revenue per user (Copilot, E5, E7, GitHub Copilot); seats +5% (FY2026 +6%). Commercial RPO $677B
   (+73% year on year, $678B in June), ~30% due in 12 months, duration 2.3 years. OpenAI revenue
   $10.0B (10.8% of total). Better on growth; backlog flat on June (Q1 FY2025 also dipped).

2. Margins and unit economics [HELD]
   Operating margin 49.8% (48.9% a year earlier; FY2026 46.8%). Company gross margin 67.8% (69.0%).
   Microsoft Cloud gross margin 66% (68%), now "driven by sales mix shift to Azure"; AI no longer
   named as the cause. Agents and Infra: cost of revenue +35% vs revenue +25%, operating margin 52.8%
   (53.1%). Opex +6% with headcount -2%. Gross margin worse, operating margin better.

3. Earnings quality [BETTER]
   Net income $36.63B (+32%); adjusted $36.42B (+18%). OpenAI net gain $272M pre-tax ($207M after
   tax) vs a $4.1B loss a year earlier, so the GAAP/adjusted gap is now small. Other income $38M.
   Effective tax rate 20% (19%). No one-off charges named.

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $58.00B (+$12.9B, +29%), "primarily due to an increase in cash received from
   customers and a decrease in cash used to pay income taxes". Capex $39.37B (+$20.0B). FCF $18.63B
   ($25.66B a year earlier, -27%); FCF / net income 51% (FY2026 50%; Q1 FY2026 92%). $6.7B more cash
   used "to facilitate the purchase of components". DSO 61 days (62). Unearned revenue (current)
   $66.87B (+13%).

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Repurchased $2.9B under the program ($4.0B); $37.7B left of $60.0B. Dividend $0.98 per share
   ($0.91, +8%); dividends declared $7.3B. Buybacks plus dividends declared $10.2B = 55% of FCF.
   Capex and component financing from operating cash flow; long-term debt flat at $31.09B, $4.9B
   more debt repaid than a year earlier.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Cash and short-term investments $64.5B ($76.8B in June). Leases not yet commenced $364.6B
   ($329.1B), commencing FY2027-FY2034 (FY2033). Finance lease liabilities $70.97B ($66.59B);
   operating lease liabilities $27.48B ($21.93B); interest expense up "primarily due to higher
   finance lease interest expense". Server-component receivables $35.7B ($27.8B); restricted supplier
   investments $12.6B ($11.3B).

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   OpenAI revenue $10.0B in the quarter = 10.8% of revenue (FY2026 7.3%); receivable $7.2B = 11.6% of
   receivables (7.4%). Stake "approximate 24%" (25%). Supplier under the restricted agreement still
   not named.

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Three segments become two; Azure, Microsoft 365 commercial cloud, Microsoft Cloud and Industry
   solutions redefined; seat metric adds GitHub. Prior periods recast, but no old-basis Azure growth,
   which breaks the series the AI thesis is tested on. RPO critical estimate unchanged.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   AI regulation "is increasing costs and may create liability" (could). New datacenter paragraph:
   rules "are restricting and delaying infrastructure development, increasing costs". IDPC "has
   appealed" a ruling in LinkedIn's GDPR case. Reasonably possible losses $400M (unchanged). IRS NOPAs
   $28.9B unchanged. None of the regulatory costs is quantified.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   No numeric guidance. Forward-capex sentence unchanged ("We will continue to invest in capital
   expenditures"). 12-month RPO share unchanged at ~30%. 2030 sustainability goals now face
   "challenging the viability of these goals". Lease commencement window extended to FY2034.
