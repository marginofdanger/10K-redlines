Latest filing: 10-Q Q1 FY2027

What the Q1 FY2027 10-Q adds, for and against. Operating income rose 21% and the OpenAI line, a $4.1B loss a year earlier, is now a small gain; against that, free cash flow fell 27%, commitments rose again, and OpenAI became 10.8% of revenue. The quarter also introduced two segments and a new Azure definition, so several growth rates have no like-for-like comparison.

Bull case

P1 Azure and AI demandStated
Azure revenue +$10.8B (+48%); Agents and Infra revenue +$15.1B (+25%)
Q1 FY2027: "Azure revenue increased $10.8 billion or 48% driven by demand for services across the platform with continued growth across all workloads."

Size: Azure near $33.3B for the quarter, 36% of revenue (computed from $10.8B = 48%). Agents and Infra added more than the company's whole increase ($14.6B), as Devices and Consumer shrank.

Growth accelerated from 41% (FY2026, old definition) to 48%, but the definition changed this quarter (GPU infrastructure and Foundry in; GitHub and Health and Life Sciences out) and no old-basis rate is given, so part of the step-up may be definitional.

Next filing: Azure growth on the new basis holds in the 40s; any bridge to the old definition; commercial RPO growth after June's $678B.

P2 Cloud margins and AI unit economicsStated
Operating margin 49.8% (48.9%): opex +6% with headcount down 2%
Q1 FY2027: "Operating expenses increased $975 million or 6% driven by continued investments in research and development compute capacity and talent."

Size: Operating income +$7.95B (+21%) on revenue +19%; sales and marketing flat at $5.72B; R&D +11%.

Operating leverage more than offset a lower gross margin (67.8% vs 69.0%), the pattern of FY2026 (operating margin 46.8% vs 45.6%) continued for a second filing.

Next filing: operating margin above 48% if the Microsoft Cloud gross margin falls below 66%.

P6 Productivity franchiseStated
Microsoft 365 commercial cloud +17% on revenue per user: Copilot, E5 and the new E7
Q1 FY2027: "Microsoft 365 commercial cloud revenue increased 17% driven by higher revenue per user, reflecting continued upsell to premium seat offerings, including Microsoft 365 Copilot, E5, and E7, as well as increased consumption of usage-based services, primarily in GitHub Copilot."

Size: Microsoft 365 cloud revenue +$4.1B. Growth matches FY2026 (+17%) on a new definition that adds GitHub cloud services.

Pricing, not seats, is carrying the franchise: seats grew 5%, so revenue per user rose about 11% (computed).

Next filing: seat growth and the revenue-per-user language; any Copilot seat or revenue figure.

P3 Capital intensity and cash conversionStated
Operating cash flow +$12.9B (+29%) to $58.0B
Q1 FY2027: "Cash from operations increased $12.9 billion to $58.0 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, primarily due to an increase in cash received from customers and a decrease in cash used to pay income taxes, offset in part by an increase in cash paid to suppliers."

Size: Operating cash flow is 158% of net income ($36.63B); current unearned revenue $66.87B (+13%).

Customers are paying ahead: the cash engine grew faster than revenue for a second filing (FY2026 +34%).

Next filing: operating cash flow growth against capex growth (+103% this quarter).

Bear case

P4 OpenAI relationshipStated
OpenAI revenue $10.0B in one quarter: 10.8% of revenue (FY2026: 7.3%); receivable $6.0B → $7.2B
Q1 FY2027: "For the three months ended September 30, 2026, we recorded revenue from commercial arrangements with OpenAI, inclusive of revenue-sharing payments, of $10.0 billion."
Q1 FY2027: "Accounts receivable from OpenAI as of September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026 was $7.2 billion and $6.0 billion, respectively."

Size: 10.8% of revenue and 11.6% of receivables ($62.08B); one quarter equals 41% of FY2026's $24.1B. The stake fell to an "approximate 24%" (25%).

One counterparty that no longer has an exclusive arrangement with Azure, and whose revenue share now runs one way to Microsoft, is a growing share of sales and of credit exposure. The FY2026 risk factor says such partners may cut consumption.

Next filing: OpenAI revenue and receivable share; any disclosure of OpenAI's share of RPO or of collection terms.

P3 Capital intensity and cash conversionStated
Free cash flow $18.63B vs $25.66B a year earlier (−27%) as capex rises $20.0B
Q1 FY2027: "Cash used in investing increased $7.4 billion to $42.0 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, primarily due to a $20.0 billion increase in additions to property and equipment and a $6.7 billion increase in cash used in other investing primarily to facilitate the purchase of components, offset in part by a $19.1 billion increase in cash from net investment purchases, sales, and maturities."

Size: Capex $39.37B = 68% of operating cash flow; FCF / net income 51% (FY2026 50%, Q1 FY2026 92%). Cash and short-term investments fell $12.3B in the quarter to $64.5B.

The second filing in a row with FCF falling while net income rises; component financing adds $6.7B of investing cash outside capex.

Next filing: FCF / net income above 50%; whether capex growth slows from +103%.

P5 Off-balance-sheet commitments and supplyStated
Leases not yet commenced $329.1B → $364.6B, now running to FY2034
Q1 FY2027: "As of September 30, 2026, we had additional leases, primarily for datacenters, that had not yet commenced of $364.6 billion, with some arrangements subject to certain contractual conditions being met."

Size: $364.6B = 3.1 times FY2026 capex ($115.95B); plus $35.7B of server-component receivables and $12.6B of supplier-restricted investments. Finance lease liabilities already $70.97B (June: $66.59B).

Fixed commitments keep rising ahead of revenue. The FY2026 10-K's new risk factor warns that overbuilding "may lead to impairment of assets on our balance sheet".

Next filing: lease commencements, how much of the total is conditional, and any change to the impairment language.

P2 Cloud margins and AI unit economicsInferred
Agents and Infra cost of revenue +35% vs revenue +25%; Microsoft Cloud margin 66% (68%), now blamed on "sales mix shift to Azure"
Q1 FY2027: "Cost of revenue increased $6.2 billion or 35% driven by investments in datacenter infrastructure to support growing customer demand across Azure and Microsoft 365 cloud."
Q1 FY2027: "Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage decreased to 66% driven by sales mix shift to Azure, offset in part by product-level margin improvements, primarily in Azure."

Size: Agents and Infra gross margin 68.9% vs 71.3% a year earlier (computed); company gross margin 67.8% (69.0%).

Azure now explicitly includes GPU infrastructure-as-a-service, so "mix shift to Azure" is a shift to the AI infrastructure business; the cause was renamed, not removed (inference).

Next filing: Microsoft Cloud gross margin below 66%, or a stated stabilization.

P7 Consumer and devicesStated
Windows OEM and devices revenue −22% on "inventory drawdowns" and "weaker PC market demand"
Q1 FY2027: "Windows OEM and devices revenue decreased $1.0 billion or 22% driven by the benefit from Windows 10 end of support demand in the prior-year, inventory drawdowns by OEM and channel partners, as well as weaker PC market demand."

Size: −$1.0B; Devices and Consumer revenue −3%. XBOX −2% after −7% in FY2026.

The elevated channel inventory flagged since Q3 FY2025 ("due to tariff uncertainty") is unwinding, as the 10-Q scanner anticipated.

Next filing: Windows OEM and devices growth; whether Devices and Consumer operating income keeps rising on lower Activision amortization.

P1 Azure and AI demandInferred
Commercial RPO $678B → $677B; growth slows from +84% to +73%
Q1 FY2027: "Commercial remaining performance obligation increased 73% to $677 billion."

Size: −$1B on June; about 70% is due after 12 months (weighted-average duration 2.3 years).

A first-quarter dip has precedent (Q1 FY2025: $269B → $259B), so this is weak evidence on its own, but backlog has added only $52B since December 2025 after $233B in the quarter before.

Next filing: commercial RPO at December 2026 versus $625B a year earlier.
