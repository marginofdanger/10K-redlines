PGR Q3 2024 10-Q (filed November 4, 2024; quarter ended September 30, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 2024 10-Q. XBRL figures from the financials extract;
the rest from the filing text (MD&A and notes).

1. Growth and pricing [BETTER]
   NPW $19.5B, +25%; NPE +23%; PIF 33.9M, +14% (+1.6M in the quarter). Personal Lines NPW +29%
   (agency +26%, direct +31%); new personal auto applications +110% on advertising and lifted non-rate
   restrictions. Personal auto rates +<1%; written premium per policy +5% (2023 rate increases).
   Commercial Lines +7%, Property +9% (MD&A Overview).

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Combined ratio 89.0 vs 92.4 (underwriting margin 11.0% vs 7.6%). Accident-year loss ratio 68.8 vs
   76.1. Personal auto frequency -5%, severity +1%. Expense ratio +4.8 points on advertising up nearly
   400% ($2.8B year to date). Hurricane Helene 3.1 points; catastrophes 4.0 points vs 3.2.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Favorable prior-year development $137M (0.7 points); 1.2 points of favorable development on
   first-half 2024 storms. Recurring investment income +45%; net realized gains vs losses a year ago.
   Net income $2.33B (+$1.2B). Underwriting drives the result.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Nine-month operating cash flow $12.1B vs $8.2B. Q3 operating cash flow $4.61B = 198% of net income
   (XBRL); capex $57M. Float growth from premium growth.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   Repurchased 0.7M shares ($134M) year to date, mostly for equity-award withholding; quarterly
   dividend $0.10; Series B preferred redeemed in Q1 ($508M). No acquisitions.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [BETTER]
   Total capital $34.1B; debt-to-total capital 20.2% (25.4% at year-end 2023), policy below 30%. No
   debt maturities until 2027; $300M line undrawn. Statutory surplus $27.2B. Portfolio $79.4B, nearly
   60% in short-term and Treasuries.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   Personal auto drives about 80% of PIF growth. Property shrinking in coastal/hail states (-9% PIF)
   while growing in less volatile states (+19%). Florida non-renewals slowed by hurricane moratoriums.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Property still a separate segment (folded into Personal Lines in the FY2024 10-K). No
   critical-estimate changes disclosed.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Total-loss valuation classes certified in eight states (Ohio added) plus a data-security class.
   Losses "reasonably possible but neither probable nor reasonably estimable".

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   Goal restated: "growing as fast as we can, at or below a companywide 96 combined ratio"; 9-month
   margin 10.9%. Rates expected "to stay stable in the fourth quarter 2024". Milton estimate: about
   $140M property and about $600M vehicle (Helene plus Milton).
