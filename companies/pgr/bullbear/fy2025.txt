PGR FY2025 10-K (filed March 2, 2026; year ended December 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q3 2025 10-Q (annual comparisons against FY2024 in the text).

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   NPW $83.2B, +12% (2024 +21%); NPE +15%; PIF 38.6M, +10%. Personal Lines NPW +14% (agency +11%,
   direct +19%, property +1%); Commercial Lines -3% (TNC policies not renewed; Lyft gone, Uber 16 to
   14 states). Rates: personal auto -<1%, property +10%, core commercial auto +9%.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Underwriting margin 12.6% vs 11.2% despite the $1.2B Florida credit (1.5 points); Personal Lines
   ex-credit 14.2%. Loss ratio -3.2 points (frequency, catastrophes, development). Severity +6%, bodily
   injury +10%. Advertising $5.1B (+$1.1B, 0.6 points). Personal property margin 24.9%.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Favorable development $1,394M (1.7 points; about $1,190M personal auto, mainly Florida) offsets the
   $1,224M Florida credit. Net income $11.31B (+$2.8B); tax rate 20.5%.

4. Cash conversion [HELD]
   Operating cash flow $17.55B (+$2.4B) = 155% of net income; FCF $17.20B (XBRL).

5. Capital allocation [BETTER]
   Dividends declared $13.90 per share ($8.1B; $13.50 variable paid January 2026) = 72% of net
   income. Buybacks 0.7M shares ($166M); repurchase policy wording "and" becomes "or" opportunistically.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Debt-to-total capital 18.5% (21.2%); $7.0B notes, next $1.0B due 2027. Statutory surplus $28.4B
   after the dividend; NPW-to-surplus 2.9x (2.7x). Purchase obligations $3.0B vs $1.6B.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   TNC limited to Uber (14 states). Platinum agents nearly 6,000 vs 7,100; new risk on agency-channel
   consolidation. Homeowners new applications -45%; Robinsons PIF flat.

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Assumed accident-year severity for personal auto liability reserves lowered to 7.2% (8.8% a year
   earlier) while reported bodily injury severity rose 10%; commercial auto raised to 6.5% (1.7%).
   Florida statute basis restated from calendar years to accident years.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Florida excess profits "exceeded" the limit: $1.2B credit; risk factors add "limit the profit an
   insurer may earn". Certified total-loss states three (CO, NC, OH). Agentic AI risk added.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   2025 guides met: auto rates stable, property up (~10%), commercial auto up (~9%), advertising
   floor met ($5.1B) then dropped. 2026: "increasing rates modestly" in property and commercial auto;
   tariff wording softened to "may".
