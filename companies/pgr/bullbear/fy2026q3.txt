PGR Q3 2026 10-Q (filed November 2, 2026; quarter ended September 30, 2026)

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   NPW $22.3B, +4% (Q2 +5%); NPE +5%; PIF 40.5M, +6% (Q2 +7%), +0.4M in the quarter. Personal vehicle
