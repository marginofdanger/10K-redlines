UNH FY2024 10-K (filed February 27, 2025; year ended December 31, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2023 10-K.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $400.28B, +8%; UnitedHealthcare +6%, Optum +12%, Optum Rx +15% (MD&A). Domestic people
   served +2.1M (commercial), Medicaid down on redeterminations; Medicare Advantage 7.845M.

2. Margins and unit economics [WORSE]
   MCR 85.5% vs 83.2%; operating income flat at $32.29B on +8% revenue; operating margin 8.1% vs 8.7%.
   MCR drivers: Medicare funding cuts, Medicaid rate mismatch, coding intensity, specialty drugs,
   member mix, cyberattack accommodations ($640M). Optum Insight earnings $3.10B vs $4.27B.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Net income $14.40B (-36%) after $8.3B of South America losses ($5.0B currency translation).
   Favorable development $700M vs $840M. Optum Health's $7.77B helped by "gains related to business
   portfolio refinement". Tax rate 24.1% (non-deductible losses).

4. Cash conversion [WORSE]
   Operating cash flow $24.20B vs $29.07B; FCF $20.70B = 144% of net income (the ratio is flattered by
   the non-cash South America loss).

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Acquisitions $13.4B, provider loans $9.03B (repaid $4.5B), buybacks $9.0B, dividends $7.53B, capex
   $3.5B. Net debt issuance $14.66B: buybacks plus dividends ($16.5B) were within FCF, but deals and
   provider loans were borrowed.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Debt $76.18B vs $61.47B (note table). Covenant ratio no longer disclosed ("in compliance").
   Cash for general corporate use about $800M. Purchase obligations $11.5B. Ratings all Stable.
   Statutory capital $37.8B vs $20.4B required; subsidiaries paid the parent $9.2B.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   CMS premiums 40% of revenue (40% in 2023). U.S. customers about 99% of revenue after Brazil.

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Development "included no individual factors that were significant"; IBNR $23.7B. 1% reserve
   sensitivity about $260M of net earnings. Risk factor "80% to 85%" MCR range deleted (10-K dashboard).

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   ~190 million individuals affected by the Change Healthcare breach. 2026 Advance Notice "also well
   short" of cost trend. Three of seven executive officers replaced (10-K dashboard).

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   "Our 2025 benefit design approach contemplates these trends"; Medicaid gap "will narrow in 2025";
   Change Healthcare impacts "at a lesser extent in 2025"; pending acquisitions about $4B.
