UNH Q3 2024 10-Q (filed November 4, 2024; quarter ended September 30, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 2024 10-Q. Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL
(scratchpad financials extract); quarterly cash flow is derived from year-to-date figures.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $100.82B, +9% (Q2 +6%); UnitedHealthcare +7%, Optum +13%, Optum Rx +19% (MD&A, Results
   Summary). Domestic people served +2.0M on commercial growth; Medicaid -615K on redeterminations;
   Medicare Advantage 7.81M (+165K). Brazil sale cut Global revenue -68%.

2. Margins and unit economics [WORSE]
   MCR 85.2% vs 82.3%; operating margin 8.6% vs 9.2%. New cost drivers: hospital "coding intensity"
   and specialty-drug prescribing (Medical Cost Trends). UnitedHealthcare earnings $4,212M vs $4,592M
   (-8%); Optum Health 8.3% margin (series high); Optum Insight -29% on the cyberattack.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   Investment and other income $1,643M vs $997M (+65%; the $646M increase = 7.4% of operating income). Optum Health gains
   partly from "business portfolio and asset dispositions". Nine-month favorable development $600M vs
   $760M. Tax rate 17.8% vs 21.5%. Nine-month EPS $9.53 includes the $8.3B South America losses.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Q3 operating cash flow $13.95B vs net income $6.05B (XBRL, derived); nine months $21.8B vs $34.3B,
   the 2023 figure inflated by the $11.9B October CMS payment received in September 2023 (MD&A).

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Nine months: acquisitions $11.7B, provider loans $8.9B (repaid $3.2B), buybacks $4.0B (Q3 $956M),
   dividends $5.6B, capex $2.6B; net debt issuance $15.1B. Pending transactions about $5B.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Total debt $78.0B ($74.1B long-term) after $18.0B of 2024 note issues; commercial paper $1.2B.
   Covenant ratio 43% vs 60% maximum. Cash for general corporate use $5.2B. Ratings all Stable.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   Medicare Advantage 7.81M and Medicaid 7.45M members; CMS premiums about 40% of revenue (FY2024
   10-K confirms 40%). Global commercial members 1.34M vs 5.48M after Brazil.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Development "included no individual factors that were significant". South American operations
   held for sale with a $1.245B remeasurement. $10.4B of intangibles recorded on 2024 deals.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [HELD]
   DOJ risk-adjustment case still described through its 2018-2019 procedural history. Change
   Healthcare impact "will continue at a lesser extent in 2025" (MD&A, per the 10-Q scanner).

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   No numeric guidance in the 10-Q. Pending transactions about $5B; dividend $8.40 annual rate;
   42M shares of authorization left.
