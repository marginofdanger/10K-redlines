UNH Q2 2025 10-Q (filed August 11, 2025; quarter ended June 30, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q1 2025 10-Q.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $111.62B, +13%; Medicare & Retirement +22%, Community & State +20%. People served +1.03M;
   Medicare Advantage 8.35M (+580K). Optum Health revenue -7%.

2. Margins and unit economics [WORSE]
   MCR 89.4% vs 85.1%; operating income $5.15B (-35%). Pricing "well-short of the medical cost trends
   incurred"; cost overrun now covers all lines (Medical Cost Trends). UnitedHealthcare earnings -48%,
   Optum Health -67% ($636M, 2.5% margin), Optum Insight +83%, Optum Rx +2%.

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   No prior-year development in Q2 (six months $320M, same as Q1). Exchange losses for H2 2025 booked
   in Q2 ("acceleration of anticipated future losses"). Tax rate 12.5% vs 22.0% on lower pre-tax income.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Q2 operating cash flow $7.19B vs net income $3.41B (XBRL, derived); six months $12.64B vs $7.89B,
   helped by working capital and the IRA's Part D seasonality.

5. Capital allocation [WORSE]
   Six months: buybacks $5.55B (12.1M shares at $454.82), dividends $3.91B, capex $1.78B; $3.0B of notes
   issued in June. Buybacks + dividends $9.46B vs six-month FCF $10.86B.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [WORSE]
   Moody's, S&P and A.M. Best outlooks Stable -> Negative. Subsidiary dividends "may be impacted";
   six-month subsidiary dividends $1.9B. Total debt $79.2B. Cash for general corporate use $3.3B.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   Medicare Advantage 8.35M; Medicare & Retirement 49.5% of UnitedHealthcare revenue.

8. Accounting judgments [WORSE]
   Premium-deficiency-type charge for H2 2025 exchange losses taken early; IBNR $26.8B. Development
   still "no individual factors that were significant".

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   DOJ forward-looking factor widened to "legal actions concerning our participation in the Medicare
   program"; "shareholders" added to litigants; OIG joins CMS on RADV audits. CEO change (Hemsley).

10. Guidance and commitments [WORSE]
   Medicaid gap now "continues to be a mismatch" (FY2024 forecast missed). Medicaid membership losses
   expected in 2026. Dividend raised to $8.84 (+5.2% vs +11.7% in 2024). Pending acquisitions about $4B.
