UNH Q1 2026 10-Q (filed May 5, 2026; quarter ended March 31, 2026)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the FY2025 10-K.

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   Revenue $111.72B, +2% (pricing, Optum Rx specialty); UnitedHealthcare served 1.1M fewer people;
   Medicare Advantage 7.555M (-690K), Medicaid 7.16M (-410K), risk-based commercial -685K. Exchange
   2026 profits pledged as rebates.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   MCR 83.9% vs 84.8%; "As expected and contemplated" restored. Operating income $8.99B (-1%);
   operating cost ratio 13.8% vs 12.4%. UnitedHealthcare +9%; Optum Health -19%, Optum Insight -17%,
   Optum Rx -10% (recast segments).

3. Earnings quality [WORSE]
   $1,050M favorable development (= 13% of pre-tax income) and $137M loss-contract release; $230M net
   portfolio gain offset by a $400M foundation contribution. Without development the MCR would be about
   85.1%.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Operating cash flow $8.91B vs $5.46B (IRA pharmacy rebates, working capital); FCF $8.15B = 130% of
   net income.

5. Capital allocation [BETTER]
   No cash buybacks; forward contracts for up to $2.0B (1.7M shares at $285.68 completed). Dividends
   $2.0B. Dispositions brought $1.08B. $3.0B of acquisitions agreed.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Total debt $77.9B; commercial paper $3.4B (YE $2.25B). Cash for general corporate use $1.1B.
   Ratings unchanged (three Negative outlooks).

7. Concentration [BETTER]
   Medicare Advantage and Medicaid membership falling; Medicare & Retirement 49% of UnitedHealthcare
   revenue.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   First named development driver: "a favorable respiratory illness season". IBNR $27.6B vs $26.7B.
   Optum Financial moved to Optum Insight with prior periods recast.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]

   DOJ paragraph unchanged.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
