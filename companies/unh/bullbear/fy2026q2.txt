UNH Q2 2026 10-Q (filed August 10, 2026; quarter ended June 30, 2026)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q1 2026 10-Q.

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   Revenue $112.03B, flat (Q1 +2%); premiums -1%. UnitedHealthcare served 1.6M fewer people: Medicare
   Advantage 7.565M (-785K), Medicaid 6.78M (-710K), risk-based commercial 7.655M (-785K); fee-based
   +735K. Revenue growth from "pricing trends and actions, including increased Medicaid rates".

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   MCR 86.7% vs 89.4% (six months 85.3% vs 87.1%); operating income $7.99B (+55%); operating margin
   7.1% vs 4.6%. UnitedHealthcare $3,942M (+90%), Optum Health $1,190M (+177% vs a recast $429M),
   Optum Insight $1,369M (+14%), Optum Rx $1,490M (+3%). Operating cost ratio 12.7% vs 12.3%.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Six-month development $1,250M and loss-contract/premium-deficiency changes $236M = $1.49B (10% of
   six-month pre-tax income); Q2 alone about $250M (4%). Q2 net portfolio loss $39M. Tax rate 18.6% vs
   12.5% (a normal rate, not a benefit).

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Six-month operating cash flow $19.96B vs $12.64B (Q2 alone $11.05B vs net income $5.48B), credited
   partly to "timing of government payments" and IRA rebates. $3.2B of receivables sold, $2.0B
   remitted for 2025 sales. Six-month FCF $18.4B.

5. Capital allocation [BETTER]
   Six months: buybacks $3.73B (10.5M shares at $344.08; $2.0B forward settlement paid July 1),
   dividends $4.09B, capex $1.56B, net debt repayment $4.81B. Returns $7.8B vs FCF $18.4B. July 2
   acquisition: $1.5B cash plus $1.5B due within a year.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [BETTER]
   Total debt $73.3B vs $78.4B at year end; no commercial paper. Cash for general corporate use $1.1B;
   loan receivables $10.8B. Ratings unchanged: Moody's, S&P, Fitch Negative; A.M. Best A- Stable.

7. Concentration [HELD]
   Medicare & Retirement $42.4B = 49% of UnitedHealthcare revenue; Optum Rx scripts 387M vs 414M as
   UnitedHealthcare membership shrinks.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   Development again attributed to "a favorable respiratory illness season". IBNR $26.5B (Q1 $27.6B).
   No critical-estimate changes.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   New commercial cost driver: No Surprises Act independent resolution and "more aggressive billing".
   IRS NOPAs and DOJ paragraph unchanged. Two new execution factors added to forward-looking list.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   Medicare Advantage and value-based care contraction "expected to continue throughout 2026"; state
   exit now reported. Forward repurchase settled as stated ($2.0B by July 1). Dividend +5.0% to $9.28.
   Exchange rebate pledge repeated; amount not disclosed.
