Latest filing: 10-Q Q2 2026

What the Q2 2026 10-Q adds, for and against. The quarter is the clearest evidence yet that the 2026 repricing is working: unlike Q1, the margin gain does not rest mainly on prior-year reserves. The cost is a smaller company, with 1.6 million fewer UnitedHealthcare members and lower Optum Rx volume.

Bull case

P1 Medical cost trend and pricingStated
MCR 86.7% vs 89.4%; UnitedHealthcare earnings $3,942M vs $2,075M (+90%) on flat revenue
Q2 2026: "The MCR decreased due to favorable prior period reserve development, affordability and medical cost management initiatives, and pricing trends, partially offset by medical costs trend which remains above historical levels"
Q2 2026: "Medical care ratio (a) | 86.7 | 89.4 | (2.7) | 85.3 | 87.1 | (1.8)"

Size: 2.7 points on $86.96B of premiums = about $2.3B of quarterly medical cost. Q2 reserve development was about $200M (0.2 points; six-month $1,250M less Q1 $1,050M), so roughly 2.5 points is pricing and cost management.

Q1 2026's 0.9-point improvement was mostly reserve releases. Q2's is not, which is the first sign that the 2026 benefit and pricing actions are lifting the underlying margin.

Next filing: Q3 2026 MCR below Q3 2025's 89.9% by a similar margin with little prior-year development.

P6 Cash, capital return and balance sheetStated
Debt down $5.1B in six months; commercial paper repaid to zero; operating cash flow $20.0B
Q2 2026: "As of June 30, 2026, the Company had no commercial paper outstanding."
Q2 2026: "Cash flows from operations for the six months ended June 30, 2026 were $20.0 billion."

Size: total debt $73.3B vs $78.4B at year end; net repayments $4.81B. Six-month operating cash flow = 164% of net earnings ($12.15B). Commercial paper was $3.4B at March 31.

Deleveraging out of operating cash, not asset sales, is what the rating agencies' Negative outlooks need to see. Interest expense already fell 6% in the quarter.

Next filing: debt held near $73B after the $1.5B July acquisition payment and the $2.0B forward-repurchase settlement; any outlook revision.

P6 Cash, capital return and balance sheetStated
Buybacks back to 10.5M shares and dividend +5.0%, both covered by free cash flow
Q2 2026: "During the six months ended June 30, 2026, we repurchased approximately 10.5 million shares at an average price of $344.08 per share"
Q2 2026: "In June 2026, our Board of Directors increased our quarterly cash dividend to an annual rate of $9.28 compared to $8.84 per share"

Size: buybacks $3.73B (equity statement) + dividends $4.09B = $7.8B against six-month FCF of $18.4B ($19.96B less $1.56B capex). Diluted shares 906M vs 910M.

Capital return resumed after three quarters without buybacks, at about 24% below the 2025 average price ($454.82), and debt fell at the same time. In 2025, deals and returns had exceeded FCF.

Next filing: buybacks continuing within FCF; the authorization (10.6M shares left) topped up.

P4 Optum Health and value-based careStated
Optum Health earns $1,190M (5.1% margin) vs a recast $429M (1.7%)
Q2 2026: "Optum Health (a) | 1,190 | 429 | 761 | 177 | 2,331 | 1,840 | 491 | 27"
Q2 2026: "Optum Health (a) | 5.1 | 1.7 | 3.4 | 4.9 | 3.7 | 1.2"

Size: +$761M year over year. The Q2 restructuring benefit at Optum Health was $51M (loss-contract release), so the margin is about 4.9% without it.

After a $(278)M 2025, the segment is back to mid-single-digit margins on fewer, repriced contracts. The base is the recast figure (Optum Financial moved out), lower than the $636M first reported.

Next filing: margin at or above 4.5% in Q3 2026 with the loss-contract reserve largely used.

P5 Optum Rx and Optum InsightStated
Optum Insight earnings +14% to $1,369M (25.3% margin) after a weak Q1
Q2 2026: "Earnings from operations for the three months ended June 30, 2026, increased due to growth in business services."

Size: +$164M year over year; Q1 2026 had been −17%. Six-month earnings still −2% ($2,332M vs $2,369M).

Business services, which lost volume from Q3 2025 to Q1 2026, grew again. Q2 has no disposition gain at Insight (a $4M loss), so the growth is operating.

Next filing: a second quarter of business-services growth.

Bear case

P1 Medical cost trend and pricingStated
New commercial cost driver: No Surprises Act arbitration and “more aggressive billing”; trend “above historical levels”
Q2 2026: "Commercial medical cost trend is elevated, in part due to the independent resolution process under the No Surprises Act and more aggressive billing practice among providers."

Size: not quantified in the filing. Exposed book: 7.655M risk-based commercial members; Employer & Individual revenue $20.0B a quarter.

The pressure has moved from Medicare Advantage seniors (2025) to commercial (2026) inside a paragraph that still opens “as expected and contemplated”. Commercial pricing resets annually, so a 2026 miss would show in 2027 pricing.

Next filing: whether the commercial driver stays, widens or is quantified; risk-based commercial membership trend.

P3 Medicare Advantage and MedicaidStated
UnitedHealthcare serves 1.6M fewer people; Medicare Advantage −785K, Medicaid −710K, premiums −1%
Q2 2026: "UnitedHealthcare served 1.6 million fewer people due to benefit design and pricing actions and reduced Medicaid eligibility and the exit from one state."
Q2 2026: "Medicare Advantage | 7,565 | 8,350 | (785) | (9)"

Size: premiums $86.96B vs $87.91B (−$949M); Medicare Advantage −9%, Medicaid −9% to 6.78M (lowest in the series), risk-based commercial −9%.

Margin is being bought with volume. The filing says Medicare Advantage contraction will “continue throughout 2026”, so 2027 revenue starts from a smaller base.

Next filing: Medicare Advantage membership stable from Q2's 7,565K (it was 7,555K in Q1); 2027 bid posture.

P5 Optum Rx and Optum InsightStated
Optum Rx scripts 387M vs 414M (−6.5%) as UnitedHealthcare shrinks; six-month earnings −3%
Q2 2026: "Optum Rx fulfilled 387 million and 414 million adjusted scripts in the second quarters of 2026 and 2025, respectively."
Q2 2026: "Earnings from operations decreased due to lower script volumes, partially offset by growth in specialty pharmacy."

Size: 27M fewer scripts in the quarter; Q2 revenue $38.29B vs $38.46B; six-month earnings $2,682M vs $2,759M.

The integrated model carries UnitedHealthcare's membership loss into Optum Rx. Two consecutive quarters of script declines (Q1 −6%) after years of growth.

Next filing: scripts versus Q3 2025's 414M; new external client wins offsetting the internal loss.

P2 Reserves and earnings qualityStated
$1.25B of prior-year development plus $236M of reserve releases in six months
Q2 2026: "For the six months ended June 30, 2026, prior years’ medical cost reserve development was driven by a favorable respiratory illness season along with various other individually insignificant factors."
Q2 2026: "Prior years | ( 1,250 ) | ( 320 )"

Size: $1.49B = 10% of six-month pre-tax income ($14.93B); about $250M (4%) in Q2 alone. Full-year 2025 development was $140M.

Half-year earnings growth (+21% net) is partly a reversal of conservative 2025 year-end reserves. IBNR fell from $27.6B to $26.5B in the quarter as releases were taken.

Next filing: Q3 development near zero with the MCR still improving would show the recovery does not need releases.

P4 Optum Health and value-based careStated
Optum Health revenue −5%; earnings credited to reserve releases; value-based care still contracting
Q2 2026: "Earnings from operations increased due to cost management, increased favorable reserve development and the net decrease in loss contract reserves"
Q2 2026: "the number of people served under value-based care arrangements has contracted and is expected to continue throughout 2026"

Size: revenue $23.47B vs $24.73B (−$1.25B); loss-contract releases $187M year to date = 8% of six-month Optum Health earnings ($2,331M); six-month portfolio losses at Optum Health $341M.

Two of the three earnings drivers named are reserve items, and the business is smaller. The growth case for value-based care is on hold for at least 2026.

Next filing: people served under value-based arrangements and the remaining loss-contract reserve.

P6 Cash, capital return and balance sheetStated
Cash surge credited partly to timing; ratings outlooks still Negative; parent cash $1.1B
Q2 2026: "Increased cash flows provided by operating activities were driven by increased earnings, timing of government payments, other favorable working capital dynamics and legislative changes from the Inflation Reduction Act impacting pharmacy rebates."
Q2 2026: "Senior unsecured debt | A2 | Negative | A+ | Negative | A | Negative | A- | Stable"

Size: timing effect not quantified. Of $28.6B of cash, $1.1B is available for general corporate use. $3.2B of receivables were sold under the financing facility in H1 (counted as operating cash).

Part of the $7.3B cash improvement can reverse. Three agencies have kept Negative outlooks for four quarters, and the parent still depends on subsidiaries that took capital in 2025.

Next filing: nine-month operating cash flow still well above net income; any upstream dividend disclosure; outlook changes.
