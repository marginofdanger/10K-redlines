VEEVA Q3 FY2025 10-Q (filed December 9, 2024; quarter ended October 31, 2024)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 FY2025 10-Q (quarter ended July 31, 2024).
Figures from the filing text and SEC XBRL.

1. Growth and pricing [WORSE]
   Revenue $699.2M, +13% (Q2 +15%); subscription $580.9M, +17% (Q2 +19%). R&D Solutions subscription
   $302.5M, +24%; Commercial $278.4M, +11% (MD&A Revenues; note 14). Commercial growth "to a lesser
   extent" from the annual inflation price adjustment. Professional services -3% on fewer
   implementations. Deferred revenue $739.7M, +14.8% year over year.

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Operating income $181.4M vs $128.5M (+41%); operating margin 25.9% vs 20.8% (Q2 24.6%). Total gross
   margin 75% vs 73%; subscription 86% vs 85%. Sales and marketing 14% of revenue vs 16%. Non-GAAP
   operating margin 43.5% vs 38.1%.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Other income $60.9M = 25% of pre-tax income ($242.3M), from interest on $5.1B of cash and
   investments. Effective tax rate 23.3% vs 20.8% on lower excess tax benefits. G&A stock comp $34.0M
   vs $17.5M from the June 2024 CEO grant.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Nine-month operating cash flow $1,020.5M vs $853.6M (+20%) despite higher TCJA cash taxes (MD&A
   Liquidity). Q3 operating cash flow $164.1M vs $82.6M. DSO about 33 days (seasonal low).

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   No buybacks, no dividends, no acquisitions. Cash went to net purchases of short-term investments.
   CEO granted 2,650,000 options at $236.90 (grant-date fair value about $172M).

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments $5.1B; no debt. IQVIA contingency-fee
   arrangements not accrued ("unable to make an estimate").

7. Concentration [HELD]
   Top 10 customers 28%, 29% and 31% of revenue in FY2024-FY2022 (risk factors). China about 2% of
   revenue. Subscription 59% North America.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   No changes to critical accounting estimates. ASU 2023-09 (tax disclosure) pending.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   IQVIA trade-secret claims to be tried first; "we do not expect trial to begin on February 10, 2025".
   The special master's recommended adverse-inference instruction is still on appeal. California
   non-compete trial set for June 16, 2025. Salesforce and IQVIA to jointly market a life sciences CRM.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   R&D Solutions to rise as a share of revenue (Q3: 52% of subscription). Veeva CRM supported until
   September 1, 2030. TCJA cash taxes to reduce FY2025 operating cash flow; Q4 cash flow to be
   "substantially less". No numeric revenue guidance in the 10-Q.
