VEEVA Q3 FY2026 10-Q (filed November 21, 2025; quarter ended October 31, 2025)
Fundamentals answers. Marker = versus the Q2 FY2026 10-Q.

1. Growth and pricing [HELD]
   Revenue $811.2M, +16%; subscription $682.5M, +17.5%. Commercial subscription $317.7M, +14.1% (Q2
   +13.1%); R&D Solutions $364.8M, +20.6%. Deferred revenue $822.5M, +11.2% year over year, the
   slowest in the window (Q2 +15.8%).

2. Margins and unit economics [BETTER]
   Operating income $240.9M, +33%; margin 29.7% vs 25.9%. Services gross margin 18% vs 22% (headcount
   ahead of demand). G&A fell $4M as the 2018 CEO grant finished expensing. Non-GAAP 45.0% vs 43.5%.

3. Earnings quality [HELD]
   Other income $71.9M = 23% of pre-tax. Tax rate 24.5% vs 23.3% (OBBBA cut the FDII benefit). No
   one-off charges in the quarter.

4. Cash conversion [BETTER]
   Nine-month operating cash flow $1,308.4M vs $1,020.5M (+28%), helped by OBBBA. Q3 $192.8M vs
   $164.1M.

5. Capital allocation [HELD]
   No buybacks; net purchases of short-term investments. Option exercises drove a $168.7M financing
   inflow over nine months.

6. Balance sheet and contingent exposure [HELD]
   Cash and investments $6.6B; no debt. Litigation note reduced to the closed IQVIA settlement.

7. Concentration [WORSE]
   New risk language: customers have "exercised their right to reduce usage or exercise termination
   rights"; EU Data Act lets EU customers cancel without cause from September 12, 2025.

8. Accounting judgments [HELD]
   No changes to critical estimates.

9. Legal, regulatory and governance [WORSE]
   Salesforce agreement expired September 1, 2025: no Veeva CRM sales to new customers, existing
   customers capped at 150% of seats until September 1, 2030. Non-practicing entity patent claim
   still disclosed.

10. Guidance and commitments [HELD]
   FY2026 cash-flow increase from OBBBA: on track (nine months +28%). Veeva CRM support to September 1,
   2030 still stated.
