Latest filing: 10-Q Q2 FY2027

What the Q2 FY2027 10-Q (quarter ended July 31, 2026) adds, for and against. The top line is the best in two years and buybacks are now funded from cash flow; the headline earnings growth leans on lapping last year's litigation charge and a lower tax rate, and the CRM defection language has not softened.

Bull case

P1 Subscription growth / R&D and QualityStated
Revenue +17.6% to $928.0M, the fastest growth in the eight filings; deferred revenue +18.3%
Q2 FY2027: "Total revenues | 927,963 | 789,081 | 138,882 | 18% | 1,810,911 | 1,548,124 | 262,787 | 17%"
Q2 FY2027: "The increase in subscription revenues attributable to R&D and Quality Solutions and Commercial Solutions for the three and six months ended July 31, 2026 was driven by the expanding use by existing customers and higher prices in connection with our annual inflation adjustment for our products."

Size: +$138.9M year over year; subscription +16.3% (Q1 +15.0%); R&D and Quality subscription $419.4M, +19.3%; deferred revenue $1,310.5M vs $1,107.7M (+18.3%).

Subscription growth turned back up after Q1's 15.0% low, and deferred revenue, the best lead indicator Veeva discloses (remaining performance obligations beyond a year are "not significant"), has grown faster than subscription revenue for two quarters. Since Q1 FY2027 the filing names price alongside volume as a driver; through FY2026 price was a driver only "to a lesser extent".

Next filing: subscription growth at or above 16% and deferred revenue growth above subscription growth in Q3 FY2027 (Q3 FY2026 deferred revenue grew only 11.2%).

P2 CRM franchise / Vault CRM migrationStated
Commercial subscription reaccelerates: $347.4M, +13.0% (Q1 +10.6%), adding $40M
Q2 FY2027: "The increase in subscription revenues for the three months ended July 31, 2026 consisted of $68 million attributable to R&D and Quality Solutions and $40 million attributable to Commercial Solutions."
Q2 FY2027: "•We face intense competition in markets in which we operate and if we do not compete effectively, we may lose customers and our business and operating results could be adversely affected."

Size: Commercial subscription +$39.9M year over year = 45% of Q2 subscription revenue; growth back to the FY2026 range (+13.8%).

Despite announced defections to Salesforce, the Commercial line has not shrunk; Q1's 10.6% now looks like a low rather than a new run-rate. The risk-factor summary dropped the words "particularly in the CRM market as we transition customers" (Q1 FY2027 wording), the first softening of CRM language in the window.

Next filing: Commercial subscription growth of 12% or more as Veeva CRM support (to December 31, 2029) draws nearer.

P5 Cash generation and capital returnStated
H1 buybacks $467M (2.66M shares) funded from operating cash flow of $1,366M; diluted shares −1.6%
Q2 FY2027: "During the six months ended July 31, 2026, we repurchased and subsequently retired 2,663,443 shares of our common stock for an aggregate amount of approximately $467 million."
Q2 FY2027: "The repurchase program is funded using our cash flows from operations."

Size: $467M = 34% of H1 operating cash flow; 1.6% of shares outstanding; diluted shares 165.1M vs 167.7M; $1.4B of the $2B authorization left; cash and investments still $7.2B.

Buybacks now more than offset dilution from equity awards, and since Q1 FY2027 the filing says they are funded from cash flows from operations (the FY2026 10-K said "working capital"). Average price $175.28 vs the CEO's $236.90 option strike.

Next filing: buybacks within operating cash flow in the seasonally weak Q3, and diluted shares below 165M.

P3 Margins and operating leverageStated
Gross margins stop falling and G&A drops to 8% of revenue (12%): the FY2026 promise of lower G&A is being kept
Q2 FY2027: "The decrease in general and administrative expenses for the three and six months ended July 31, 2026 was primarily due to $31 million in litigation settlement-related charges that was incurred in the quarter ended July 31, 2025."
Q2 FY2027: "Total gross margin percentage | 75 | 75 | 75 | 76"

Size: G&A $71.3M vs $95.8M; subscription gross margin 86% (flat), services 22% (flat; Q1 was 20% vs 23%); non-GAAP operating margin 44.8% vs 44.7%.

Q1's margin squeeze from hiring did not repeat. The G&A fall is a lapping effect, so this card shows stability, not new leverage (see the stance change on P3).

Next filing: non-GAAP operating margin above the prior year's 45.0% in Q3 FY2027 would restart the leverage story.

Bear case

P4 Earnings quality and dilutionInferred
Operating income +40% and EPS +39% lean on lapping a $30.6M charge and a tax rate of 21.8% vs 24.5%
Q2 FY2027: "During the three and six months ended July 31, 2026, as compared to the same periods in the prior fiscal year, our effective tax rate decreased primarily due to an increase in FDDEI deduction under the OBBBA provisions effective in the current fiscal year, partially offset by discrete tax deficiencies related to equity compensation."
Q2 FY2027: "Income before income taxes | 349,530 | 265,364 | 84,166 | 32% | 697,060 | 564,185 | 132,875 | 24%"

Size: operating income $275.0M vs $226.5M excluding last year's charge = +21%, not +40%; at last year's 24.5% tax rate net income would be about $9.5M lower (3.5%, about $0.06 a share); other income $74.5M = 21% of pre-tax income.

Underlying growth is solid but close to revenue growth; the reported acceleration is mostly comparison and tax. A fifth of pre-tax income is interest on the cash pile, which buybacks and lower rates will shrink.

Next filing: Q3 FY2027 operating income growth against a clean prior-year quarter ($240.9M); tax rate drivers.

P5 Cash generation and capital returnInferred
Q2 operating cash flow flat at $238.7M while net income rose 36%; cash taxes paid +20% and the OBBBA catch-up is ending
Q2 FY2027: "Net cash provided by operating activities on a GAAP basis | 238,709 | 238,433 | 1,365,825 | 1,115,591"
Q2 FY2027: "The OBBBA modified provisions around FDDEI and we are in the final year of our two-year election to accelerate the deduction of unamortized capitalized domestic research and development expenditures."
Q2 FY2027: "Cash paid for income taxes, net of refunds | 151,543 | 126,683"

Size: Q2 operating cash flow = 87% of net income vs 119% a year earlier; H1 cash taxes +$24.9M although the MD&A credits OBBBA with a "reduction in income tax payments"; the catch-up deduction is not quantified ("unable to estimate").

Part of FY2026–FY2027 cash growth comes from deducting three years of previously capitalized R&D. From FY2028 that tailwind is gone, so cash conversion should step down unless working capital offsets it.

Next filing: Q3 operating cash flow vs $192.8M a year earlier; any quantification of the OBBBA benefit in the FY2027 10-K.

P4 Earnings quality and dilutionStated
Equity redesign: 3.96M RSUs granted, most cliff-vesting after four years; $893M of compensation still to be expensed
Q2 FY2027: "In April 2026, we transitioned our long-term equity incentive for employees (excluding our CEO) from annual stock option grants to RSUs that generally cliff-vest after four years of continuous service"
Q2 FY2027: "As of July 31, 2026, there was a total of $581 million in unrecognized compensation cost related to unvested RSUs."

Size: 3.96M RSUs = 2.4% of 161.8M shares outstanding; $581M (RSUs) + $312M (options) = $893M unrecognized = 1.9x FY2026 stock comp ($472.7M); Q2 stock comp $136.8M, +12%.

RSUs are full-value shares, so each grant dilutes more than an option of the same value at today's price. The filing says Long-Term Grants are "not expected to be annually recurring", which leaves the steady-state cost unclear.

Next filing: stock comp as a share of revenue (14.7% in Q2) and whether buybacks keep net share count falling.

P2 CRM franchise / Vault CRM migrationStated
Defection sentence unchanged for a third filing; Commercial falls to 46% of subscription revenue
Q2 FY2027: "For example, Salesforce, our primary CRM competitor, has announced that certain large Veeva CRM customers have committed to purchasing its CRM solutions and a number of our customers have informed us of their intent to move to Salesforce as their CRM provider."
Q2 FY2027: "For the six months ended July 31, 2026, we derived approximately 46% and 54% of our subscription revenues and 44% and 56% of our total revenues from our Commercial Solutions and R&D and Quality Solutions, respectively."

Size: lost seats not quantified; Commercial share of subscription 48% (FY2025) → 47% (FY2026) → 46% (H1 FY2027); Commercial grows 13.0% vs 19.3% for R&D and Quality.

Announced losses take effect at renewal and migration dates, so revenue lags the notices. The 10-Q gives no Vault CRM customer or seat count to show the other side of the ledger.

Next filing: any change to the defection sentence, Commercial customer count in the FY2027 10-K (767 at FY2026), Commercial growth below 10% would confirm.

P3 Margins and operating leverageStated
Services grow 24%, faster than subscription (16%), at a 22% gross margin
Q2 FY2027: "Professional services and other | 161,199 | 129,898 | 31,301 | 24% | 313,972 | 254,173 | 59,799 | 24%"

Size: services 17.4% of revenue vs 16.5%; services gross profit $34.9M on $161.2M; total gross margin flat at 75% only because subscription margin held at 86%.

Services demand (implementation and business consulting) is a sign of activity, but every point of mix shift toward services costs roughly 0.6 points of gross margin.

Next filing: services growth back toward subscription growth, or services gross margin above 22%.
